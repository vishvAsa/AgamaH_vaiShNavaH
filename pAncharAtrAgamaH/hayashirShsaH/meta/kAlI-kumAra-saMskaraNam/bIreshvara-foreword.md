FOREWORD

The Asiatic Society undertook the publication of a critical edition of the text Hayaśīrṣapañcarātra, the oldest and the best of the literary documents of the Pañcarātra sect of the Vaiṣṇavas. Besides its religious bearings, the text furnishes extremely significant details relating to iconographical features of deities and architectural norms of temple construction and reconstruction. Several manuscripts of the text have been traced, and the task of collating and editing them for the use of scholars was assigned to Prof. K. K. Dutta Sastri and the work of writing iconographical notes to Dr. K. K. Das Gupta. Due to some unavoidable reasons the publication of the volume took several years and we are sorry for it. Both Prof. K. K. Dutta Sastri and Dr. K. K. Das Gupta did their best in editing the text and compiling iconographical notes. The Society is thankful to them for their untiring efforts and interests shown in the publication of this important Vaiṣṇava text.

We believe, the scholars at home and abroad will find the text extremely useful in their academic pursuits, more particularly in the fields of religion, iconography and architecture.

Aug. 18, 1976.  
The Asiatic Society,  
Calcutta.  

**BIRESWAR BANERJEE**  
*General Secretary*

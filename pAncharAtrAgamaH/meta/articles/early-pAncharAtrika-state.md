For pAncharAtra/ vaiShNava origin in yajur-veda/ Adhvaryava movement, see - [TW](/devaH/AryaH/hindukaH/viShNuH/meta/articles/MT_lekhAH/2020-01-02_the-roots-of-vaiShNavam-a-view-from-the-numerology).

Oldest pAncharAtrika is nArAyaNIya in MBh, which has the 4 vyUha-s with association with jIva-s etc.. Classical claim was always - pAncharAtra was closely affiliated with yajur-veda (ekAyana shAkhA).

'Pancharatra is also veda only, the shandilya statement is not literal but figurative' was the mainstream position of early pancharatrikas, and they didn't see themselves as transgressive, and there's nothing too unique about Ramanuja's defence.

viShNu-chitta, a famed aLvAr from late 7th CE was a shrauta-sUtra commentator (work still extant).

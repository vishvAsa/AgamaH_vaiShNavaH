Chief reason was that they did not chant the usual Savitri mantra(Gayatri) that is recited during sandhyavandana by usual Brahmins. Instead they chanted Narayana ashtakshari, Vāsudeva dvādashākshari and Vishnu Gayatri in the three sandhyas or only Vishnu Gayatri in all the three sandhyas.

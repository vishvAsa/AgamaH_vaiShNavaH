The least elaborate ones are just mantropadesha where mantra is imparted at special muhurtas or at special places in accordance with tantric manuals. The most elaborate ones are Tantrika(like the Kramadīpikā sourced one) where the deity is invoked in a Diksha mandala where the astrological asterism and various deities are invoked and bali to varies deities are given; alternatively it is based on Ritual manuals like Jayamadhavamanasollasa from Varahapurana but originally based on a Tantrika ritual which invokes the Deity(Vishnu) and His parivāra in a Navābja mandala or Chakrābjamandala.

Other than that the vaidika samskaras are performed as usual for the dvijatis. Tantrika initiations/abhishekas or just mantropadesha are additional rites that may be done separately or along with a vaidika Samskara.

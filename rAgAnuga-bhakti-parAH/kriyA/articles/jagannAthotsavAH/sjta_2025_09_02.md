## SJTA RESPONSE
PART A: SJTA RESPONSE TO VIEWS OF ISKCON SCHOLARS.

RESPONSE DATED [[2™°|2^nd]] SEPTEMBER - 2025 OF SCHOLARS OF SHREE JAGANNATHA TEMPLE ADMINISTRATION (SJTA) TO ISKCON PRESENTATIONS AT THE MEETING HELD IN THE CONFERENCE HALL OF STATE GUEST HOUSE ANNEXE (BHUBANESWAR) ON [[20™|20^th]] MARCH 2025 REGARDING ISKCON SNANA YATRA & RATHA-YATRA CELEBRATIONS WORLD-WIDE  


 
**1. ISKCON View:** Continuity and change is an essential and integral part of Vaidika Sanatana Dharma. While certain prescriptions in the *shastras* remain without change, certain other stipulations are subject to change depending upon the particular time, place and conveniences (‘*deśa-kāla-vibhāgavit*’, Shrimad Bhagavatam 4:8:54). A qualified Acharya is competent to prescribe such changes to the stipulations of Shastras. Srila Prabhupada was such a qualified Acharya and he directly authorized celebration of ISKCON Ratha-yatras worldwide on *tithis* other than those sanctioned in Skanda-purana. “What is convenient in India may not be convenient in Western countries”. The followers of ISKCON are bound to faithfully follow the instructions of their most revered Guru HDG Srila Prabhupada in celebrating Ratha-yatra around the world (‘*mahājanoyenagatahsapanthāh*’, Mahabharata Vana Parva 313:117)..... “While ISKCON respects the insights, traditions and customs of other authentic sanātana-dharma paramparās, ISKCON’s allegiance is first to following and maintaining the traditions and customs handed down to ISKCON from the acaryas within their own paramparā.”  
 
**1A. SJTA Response:-**  
The ISKCON view correctly states that on the basis of “*desa-kala vibhagavit*” *nyaya* “certain prescriptions in the shastras remain without change, certain other stipulations are subject to change depending upon particular time, place and conveniences”. The vital question here is: which prescriptions in the shastras remain without change and which are subject to change on the basis of the above principle. It is pertinent here to quote the shloka relied upon by ISKCON in this regard: [[P4]]

---

ओं नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ।  
मन्त्रेणानेन देवस्य कुर्याद् द्रव्यमयीं बुध: ।  
सपर्या विविधैद्रव्यैः देशकालविभागवित् ।।  
(Shreemad Bhagavatam, Skandha 4, Adhyaya 8, Shloka 54)  
 
“Om Namo Bhagavate Vāsudevāya. This is the twelve-syllable mantra for worshiping Lord Kṛṣṇa. One should install the physical forms of the Lord, and with the chanting of the mantra one should offer flowers and fruits and other varieties of foodstuffs exactly according to the rules and regulations prescribed by authorities. But this should be done in consideration of place, time, and attendant conveniences and inconveniences.”  
(Srila Prabhupada, Shreemad Bhagavatam)  
 
A proper understanding of the above *shloka* will reveal that while chanting of the twelve-syllable mantra for worshipping Lord Krishna cannot be altered, the offering of flowers, fruits and other varieties of foodstuffs can be altered “in consideration of place, time and attendant conveniences and inconveniences”. On the same basis and as elaborately explained below, it is firmly asserted here that while certain aspects of celebrating our festivals can be adapted/changed “in consideration of place, time and attendant conveniences and inconveniences”, the most sacred *tithi* of a Divine Manifestation as proclaimed in our sacred scriptures is one of those essential prescriptions which cannot, under any circumstance whatsoever, be changed at all and the principle of “*desa-kala-vibhagavit*” is wholly inapplicable in such matters. It is, for instance, absolutely inconceivable to celebrate anywhere (whether in India or abroad) Shree Krishna Janmashtami, Shree Rama Navami or Shree Ganesh Chaturthi on any date of the year other than the *tithi* specifically proclaimed in the scriptures. If such is the case, then there can be no valid justification for not celebrating the most holy *tithi* of the Divine Manifestation of Lord Jagannatha (Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas) — who is none other than the Supreme Lord (the “Avatari’’) -- on the *tithi*/date specifically prescribed in the scriptures and proclaimed as such by Lord Jagannatha Himself. It is a blatant violation of sacred scriptures and the Divine Commandment to perform the Lord’s Snana-yatra commemorating His ‘Birthday’ on just any random date in the year which is not sanctioned by any scripture at all.  
 
As elaborately explained in Part B below, the sacred scriptures, including Skanda Purana and Brahma Purana (Shree Purusottama-kshetra Mahatmyam) [[P5]]

---

composed by Maharshi Vedavyasa, clearly declare that the Most Holy Divine Manifestation of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas took place on Jyestha Poornima *tithi* in the Second Satya-yuga of the First Manvantara (of Svayambhuva Manu) in this Svetavaraha Kalpa. This most sacred Manifestation Tithi of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas and performance of the “Holy Bath” (Snana-yatra) on that Tithi is proclaimed by none other than Lord Jagannatha Himself at the time of His sacred Installation by Lord Brahma in the Adi-Jagannatha Temple built by Maharaja Indradyumna. This sacred Tithi of Manifestation (Jyestha Poornima) and performance of the Holy Bath Festival (Snana-yatra) on that Tithi has been faithfully observed in the Moola-Peetha of Lord Jagannatha at Puri (hereinafter referred to as “Shreemandira”) since time immemorial and is implicitly followed in all Jagannatha Temples in India and abroad. Deviation from this ancient and well-established tradition in breach of inviolable proclamation of sacred scriptures is seen only in the celebration of Snana-yatra by ISKCON centers around the world. Celebration of Snana-yatra commemorating the Most Holy “Birthday” of Lord Jagannatha on any date other than Jyestha-Poornima is contrary to the proclamation of sacred scriptures and ancient tradition and can, under no circumstance, be justified on the basis of “*desa-kala-vibhagavit*” *nyaya*.  
 
In the same way, celebration of Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha on *tithis*/dates not sanctioned by scriptures and tradition is absolutely unacceptable. The most sacred festivals of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma, such as, Shree Mahashivaratri, Holi, Diwali, Durga Pooja (Navaratri), Dussehra, Vasant Panchami, Makara-sankranti, Guru Poornima, are celebrated all over the world on *tithis* sanctioned by sacred scriptures and cannot be altered on the basis of “*desa-kala-vibhagavit*” *nyaya*. Even in all other world religions, holy festivals are celebrated according to lunar calendar/dates sanctioned by sacred scriptures and/or tradition, whether it be Christmas or Eid-al Fitr, Wesak (Buddha Day) or Paryushan Mahaparva (Jainism), Yom Kippur celebrated by the Jews or Vaisakhi celebrated by our Sikh community. There can therefore be no valid justification for celebrating Ratha-yatra (the greatest and most sacred festival of Lord Jagannatha, the Supreme Lord) on just about any day of the year on the basis of “*desa-kala-vibhagavit*” *nyaya*.  
 
As fully explained in Part B below, sacred scriptures such as Skanda Purana and Brahma Purana, specifically prescribe that Ratha-yatra is a nine-day festival commencing on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi*. As in the case of Snana-yatra, Skanda Purana clearly states that on the sacred occasion of Installation of Lord Jagannatha in the Adi-Jagannatha Temple, the Lord directly instructed [[P6]]

---

Maharaja Indradyumna to perform Ratha-yatra (that is, Gundicha-yatra and Bahuda-yatra) on specific *tithis*. The scriptural proclamations for performance of Ratha-yatra on specific *tithis* has been followed by the Moola-peetha Shreemandira since ancient times and faithfully observed in Shree Jagannatha Temples in India and abroad — with the exception of ISKCON Temples. As in the case of observance of Snana-yatra, it is found that ISKCON Centres around the world are violating the proclamation of sacred scriptures and long-established tradition by performing Ratha-yatras of Lord Jagannatha on various dates throughout the year which has no sanction at all in any sacred scripture.  
 
We wish to clarify here that our Sanatana Vaidika Dharma does allow for certain adjustments and adaptations in performance of our festivals on the basis of “*desa-kala-vibhagavit*” *nyaya*. Thus, for instance, even in Odisha at a few places, for one or the other reason, Ratha-yatra is not held on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi* but is performed within the nine-day period commencing from that *tithi*. In some other places, due to lack of resources or other local circumstances, Ratha-yatra is celebrated even without construction of the Rathas and Ratha-pulling. However, under no circumstance is there a departure from the *tithis* sanctioned by scriptures and tradition; and the holy event is always performed within the nine-day auspicious period prescribed in the scriptures commencing on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi*.  
 
From the records it is clear that His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada was also aware of the importance and sanctity of performing Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha on the sacred *tithis* prescribed in the scriptures. Srila Prabhupada, as a five-year old child (Abhay Charan De), built his first little Ratha of Lord Jagannatha in his home-town Calcutta with the help of his father and, inspired by the great Ratha-yatra tradition of Puri, he began devoutly holding his miniature Ratha-yatra from year to year in the streets of Calcutta with the participation of children and local devotees during the 8 or 9 days commencing from Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi*. The first Ratha-yatra to be ever performed in a country outside India was, under the direct instructions of Srila Prabhupada, performed in San Francisco on the [[97 of July|9^th of July]] 1967, which was precisely Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi*. During the life-time of Srila Prabhupada and with his blessings, subsequent Ratha-yatras of Lord Jagannatha performed in San Francisco (on 30^th June 1968, [[5""|5^th]] July 1970, [[27"|27^th]] June 1971 & [[18"|18^th]] July 1972), London (on [[5'"|5^th]] July 1970, [[1%|1^st]] July 1971, [[13°"|13^th]] July 1972, [[7"|7^th]] July 1973 & [[21%|21^st]] June 1974), Melbourne (on [[17"|17^th]] July 1972 & [[29"|29^th]] June 1974), Philadelphia (on [[12"|12^th]] July 1975) and New York (on [[18"|18^th]] July 1976) were all held either on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi* or within the auspicious [[P7]]

It appears however that looking at the considerable success and increasing popularity of these Ratha-yatras and the unique opportunity that it offered for propagating Krishna Consciousness across the globe, Srila Prabhupada was prevailed upon to also permit conduct of Ratha-yatras on *tithis*/dates not sanctioned by scriptures and tradition. It is most respectfully submitted here that, for the reasons briefly alluded to earlier and more elaborately explained below, this violation of scriptures and tradition in conducting Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra on *tithis*/dates not sanctioned by scriptures or tradition was most inappropriate and should not have been permitted by an Acharya of the stature and eminence of HDG Srila Prabhupada. The Commandment for performing Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra on specific *tithis* was proclaimed by none other than the Supreme Lord Jagannatha himself as has been clearly mentioned in Skanda Purana (Shree Purusottama-kshetra Mahatmayam). **2. ISKCON View:** Srila Prabhupada was justified in recommending celebration of ISKCON Ratha-yatras outside India on the basis of more conducive climatic conditions making it more convenient for the local people to participate in the yatra. Thus, for countries in the Southern Hemisphere, such as Australia, South America and South Africa, he has advised for celebration of Ratha-yatra in January (which is a summer month there) instead of the *tithi* in June-July sanctioned by the scriptures when it is mid-winter in the Southern Hemisphere countries making it difficult/inconvenient to perform the Ratha-yatra.  
 
**2A. SJTA Response:-**  
For the reasons briefly stated above and more elaborately explained in Part B below, climatic conditions cannot be a justification for performing Snana-yatra or Ratha-yatra on dates/*tithis* not sanctioned by scriptures. It may be stated here that the *tithis* in the month of Asadha prescribed for Ratha-yatra is in-fact not really conducive for performance of Ratha-yatra in Puri. There is either heavy rainfall or hot and very humid weather during that time. Several decades ago, and over past centuries, the Grand Road of Puri (on which Ratha-yatra is performed) was a mud-road. If there was a heavy downpour during Ratha-pulling, the wheels of the Rathas would sometimes get stuck in the muddy conditions and the situation would be overcome only with the assistance of the Temple’s elephants. Moreover, several centuries ago, there was a rivulet across the Grand Road near Gundicha Temple which would make the crossing difficult during the rainy season. On the other hand, if the weather is sunny and humid during Ratha-pulling, devotees find it very uncomfortable standing for long hours in the very dense crowd on the Grand Road and there are cases of devotees fainting in the sweltering heat and humid conditions. If favourable climatic condition is a valid consideration for conduct of Ratha-yatra, then the Ratha-yatra in Puri should have been held during anytime of the year other than the peak summer/rainy season. But that is not so. It is [[P11]]

---

absolutely mandatory to perform the Yatra during the month of Asadha (notwithstanding non-conducive weather conditions) because the prescription in the sacred scriptures cannot be altered in this regard. We may add here, that while the *tithis*/dates for celebration of Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra cannot be altered, the manner of celebration of these Yatras on those *tithis*/dates can be modified and adapted to suit adverse climatic conditions in different parts of the world. It is pertinent to note here that adverse climatic conditions have not always deterred ISKCON Centres from performing Ratha-yatra. Thus, for instance, ISKCON organised Ratha-yatras in the Southern Hemisphere Countries even in the winter months.  
 
**3. ISKCON View:** In some countries, it is possible to conduct Ratha-yatra only on a Sunday. If however there are other functions, then Ratha yatra is postponed to the next Sunday. It is therefore not practically possible to observe Ratha-yatra only on *tithis* prescribed in the *shastras*.  
 
**3A. SJTA Response:-**  
As mentioned earlier, Ratha-yatra is a nine-day (*[[navadinatmaka|navadinātmaka]]*) festival prescribed in the scriptures commencing from Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya *tithi*. At-least one Sunday will certainly fall within those nine days thereby making it possible to perform Ratha-yatra on a Sunday. However, for reasons briefly stated above and elaborately explained in Part B below, conduct of Ratha-yatra on a Sunday falling outside the nine-day auspicious period is just not permissible. Ratha-yatra is by far the most important festival of Lord Jagannatha. In any Temple where Lord Jagannatha is worshipped there ought to be no situation where some other event is given priority so as to postpone the Ratha-yatra to a subsequent Sunday outside the nine-day prescribed period.  
 
**4. ISKCON View:** In Russia, the regional climate, the government and the local culture are often not favorable to the observance of Rathayatra on dates prescribed in [[58913|śāstra]].  
 
**4A. SJTA Response:-**  
As indicated earlier, while it is not permissible to perform Snana-yatra or Ratha-yatra on *tithis*/dates not sanctioned by scriptures, it is certainly possible to modify and adapt the celebrations to suit regional climatic conditions, Governmental orders, local cultures or for that matter any adverse local circumstances. A glaring illustration of this situation is the celebration of Snana-yatra, [[P12]]

---

yatra, Ratha-yatra and other important festivals in Puri during COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 and 2021. The Central Government and the State Government of Odisha, with the objective of containing spread of COVID-19 disease, issued Orders in 2020 and 2021 prohibiting religious functions and other large congregations and directing closure of religious places for public. Petitions were filed in Orissa High Court and Supreme Court of India by members of the public to restrict or postpone the Ratha-yatra in Puri which was scheduled to be held on [[23 June|23^rd June]] 2020 and on [[12"|12^th]] July 2021. The issue was finally settled by Hon’ble Supreme Court in *Odisha Vikash Parishad vs Union of India* ((2020) 7 SCC 264) and in *Madan Patri & Ors vs State of Orissa & Ors* (Special Leave to Appeal (C) no. 8394/2021). The plea for postponement of Ratha-yatra in Puri was rejected and Hon'ble Supreme Court permitted the conduct of Ratha-yatra (Gundicha-yatra and Bahuda-yatra) in Puri on the *tithis*/dates sanctioned by the scriptures subject to compliance with certain guidelines/directions in order to prevent spread of COVID-19 disease. **f)** An impartial and correct understanding of the above scriptural prescriptions and long established traditions will clearly establish that celebration of Snana-yatra on any *tithi*/day of the year other than Jyestha-poornima and non-observance of sacred Anabasara rituals is a flagrant violation of our sacred scriptures and well-established tradition. It is particularly significant to note that Lord Jagannatha has Himself proclaimed that Jyestha-poornima *tithi* is His Most Holy “Birthday” and that Snana-yatra is to be celebrated on that *tithi*/day. **(ii)** It commences on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya tithi with Gundicha-yatra, that is, the journey of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas from the Temple to Gundicha-Mundupum; 
**(iii)** It concludes on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dashami tithi with Bahuda-yatra, that is, upon the return journey of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas to the Temple after seven days stay in Gundicha-Mundupum;

Jagannatha Himself in fulfilment of His Divine Wish to visit His “Birth-place” which is very Dear to Him. 
 
**k)** The above-mentioned prescriptions in sacred scriptures, regarding performance of Ratha-yatra on specific tithis in the month of Asadha Shukla-paksha has been observed since time immemorial in the Moola-peetha Shreemandira at Puri and has been, over the last few centuries, followed in countless Temples of Lord Jagannatha around the world, with the exception of ISKCON Temples. As in the case of Snana-yatra, this ancient tradition of Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha has been codified and has been given statutory sanction in the Record-of-Rights compiled under The Puri Sri Jagannath Temple (Administration) Act, 1952. Although, as mentioned earlier, the provisions of this Record-of-Rights is applicable, as such, only with regard to Shreemandira, the stipulation of various festivals and rituals recorded therein, including Ratha-yatra, are irrefutable evidence of long and well established traditions and customs of Lord Jagannatha. 
 
**l)** It clearly follows from the above that celebration of Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha on a tithi/date not prescribed in the scriptures, as mentioned above, is a breach of inviolable proclamations of sacred scriptures and long established tradition. 
 
### 3. ABIDING BY DECLARATIONS OF SCRIPTURES, ESPECIALLY WITH REGARD TO TITHI: 
 
**a)** In all religions of the world, faith and reverence for sacred scriptures and abiding by their essential prescriptions is an integral and inviolate principle for every follower of that religion. For followers of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma this vital principle is very succinctly stated by Lord Krishna in Adhyaya 16 of Shreemad Bhagavad Geeta: 
 
यः [[शाख््रविधिमुत्सृज्य|शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य]] वर्तते [[कामकारत:|कामकारतः]] ।  
न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न [[सुखे|सुखं]] न परां [[गतिमू|गतिम्]] ।। १६/२३।।  [[P63]]

---

“But he who discards scriptural injunctions and acts according to his own whims attains neither perfection nor happiness nor the supreme destination” [16/23]  
(Srila Prabhupada, Bhagavad-Gita As It Is, page 721) 
 
तस्मा[[तस्माच्छाग्रे|च्छास्त्रं]] प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।  
ज्ञात्वा [[शाख््रविधानोक्तं|शास्त्रविधानोक्तं]] कर्म [[कर्तुमिहाहसि|कर्तुमिहार्हसि]] ।। १६/२४।।  
 
“One should understand what is duty and what is not duty by the regulations of the scriptures. Knowing such rules and regulations, one should act so that he may gradually be elevated.” [16/24]  
(Srila Prabhupada, Bhagavad-Gita As It Is, page 722) 
 
Srila Prabhupada, through his prolific writings and lectures concerning Vedic Knowledge, consistently emphasised the necessity and importance of abiding by the injunctions of sacred scriptures, as noted briefly in Part A above. The prescription in sacred scriptures for performing Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra on specific tithis, as mentioned earlier, must therefore be strictly followed. 
 
**b)** Similar proclamations are also found in Shreemad Bhagavatam: 
 
वेदप्रणिहितो धर्मो ह्यधर्मस्तद्विपर्ययः ।  
वेदो नारायणः [[साक्षात्स्वयम्भूरिति|साक्षात् स्वयम्भूरिति]] शुश्रुम ।। [[६/१-४० । ।|६/१/४० ।।]] 
 
“The Yamadūtas replied: That which is prescribed in the Vedas constitutes Dharma, the religious principles, and the opposite of that is irreligion. The Vedas are directly the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Nārāyaṇa, and are self-born. This we have heard from Yamarāja.”  
(Srila Prabhupada, Srimad Bhagavatam, Skandha 6, Adhyaya 1, Shloka 40). 
 
In his commentary on the above Shloka, Srila Prabhupada writes:  
“*Bhāgavata-dharma* comprises only that which is given by the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Bhāgavata [[Dharmais|Dharma is]] [[sarvadharman parityajya mam ekam saranam vraja:|*sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja*]] [Bg. 18.66]. [[P64]]

---

One must accept the authority of the Supreme Personality of Godhead and surrender to Him and whatever He says. That is *dharma*. Arjuna, for example, thinking that violence was *adharma*, was declining to fight, but Kṛṣṇa urged him to fight. Arjuna abided by the orders of Kṛṣṇa, and therefore he is actually a *dharmī* because the order of Kṛṣṇa is *dharma*. .........The transcendental words of the Vedas emanated from the mouth of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Therefore the Vedic principles should be understood to be Vaiṣṇava principles because Viṣṇu is the origin of the Vedas. The Vedas contain nothing besides the instructions of Viṣṇu, and one who follows the Vedic principles is a Vaiṣṇava.”  
 
कर्माकर्मविकर्मेति वेदवादो न लौकिकः ।  
वेदस्य [[चेश्वरात्मत्वातू|चेश्वरात्मत्वात्]] तत्र मुह्यन्ति [[सूर्य:|सूरयः]] [[।। ११/३ - ४३।।|।। ११/३/४३ ।।]] 
 
“Śrī Āvirhotra replied: Prescribed duties, non-performance of such duties and forbidden activities are topics one can properly understand through authorized study of the Vedic literature. This difficult subject matter can never be understood by mundane speculation. The authorized Vedic literature is the sound incarnation of the Personality of Godhead Himself, and thus Vedic knowledge is perfect. Even the greatest learned scholars are bewildered in their attempts to understand the science of action if they neglect the authority of Vedic knowledge.”  
(Srila Prabhupada, Srimad Bhagavatam, Skandha 11, Adhyaya 3, Shloka 43). 
 
It is well understood that Maharshi Vedavyas composed the Purana shastras in order to vividly and elaborately expound the fundamental and subtle spiritual wisdom of the Vedas compiled by him. The Puranas are therefore considered as the “Fifth Veda” revealing the great knowledge of the Vedas through narration of the glory of the Gods and Goddesses of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma. Srila Prabhupada has clarified in his commentary mentioned above that “one must accept the authority of the Supreme Personality of Godhead and surrender to Him and whatever He says” and that the aforesaid statements in skandhas 6 and 11 of Shreemad Bhagavatam will apply not only to the Vedas but also to the Divine Proclamations of Supreme Personality of Godhead [[P65]]

---

narrated in the Purana shastras and Shreemad Bhagavad Geeta. As explained below, the Purana shastras, especially Skanda Purana and Brahma Purana, establish beyond any iota of doubt that Lord Jagannatha is none other than the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Therefore, the Divine Commandments of Lord Jagannatha regarding performance of Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra on specific tithis as proclaimed in the Skanda Purana and reiterated in several sacred scriptures, as noted earlier, cannot be violated in any manner whatsoever. 
 
**c)** Adhyaya 2 in Manu Smriti states as follows:

### 4. CHATURDHA DARU VIGRAHAS ARE MOOLA-VIGRAHAS: 
 
**a)** The Vedas indicate that Supreme Lord Vishnu manifested in Shree Purushottama-kshetra (Puri) as Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas: [[P70]]

---

अदो [[यह्दारु प्लव॑ते सिन्धों:|यद्दारु प्लवते सिन्धोः]] पारे अपूरुषम्‌ ।  
तदार॑भस्व दुर्हाणो तेन॑ गच्छ परस्तरम्‌ ॥ (ऋ.वे. [[१०/१५५-३|१०/१५५/३]]) 
 
Shree Sayanacharya, the eminent Vedic Scholar of the 14^th century, has interpreted the above mantra of the Rig Veda (10/155.3) as follows:  
“That (*adah*) which exists in a distant place (*viprakṛṣṭadeśe*), without a human creator (*apūruṣam*), is the Wooden Form (*dāru*), referring to the Divine Body (*devatāśarīraṁ*) known as Puruṣottama (the Supreme Personality of Godhead). It floats (*plavate*) on the farther shore of the ocean (*sindhoḥpāre*), at the edge of the sea (*samudratīre*), upon the waters. O difficult one (*durha*, one who is hard to overcome or destroy), O singer (*stotaḥ*), take hold of (*ā rabhasva*), rely upon (*ālambasva*), and worship (*upāssva*) that Divine Wooden Form. By worshipping that Divine Form (*dārumayeṇadevenaupāsyamānena*), reach the Supreme Destination (*parastaram*), the transcendent Vaiṣṇava Realm (*vaiṣṇavamlokam*), which is supremely attainable (*atiśayenataraṇīyam*).” 
 
This interpretation has been accepted by Raghunandana Bhattacharya (16th century CE Dharmashastra scholar of Bengal) who, in his treatise “Purushottama-kshetra tattvam”, while endorsing the view of Sayanacharya, has stated that a similar verse is found in the Paippalada-sakha of Atharva Veda (VI -8/7).

“सुदूर उत्कल क्षेत्रमें विद्यमान सर्व प्रथम स्वयं वृक्ष रूपमें अवतीर्ण अतएव पुरुष अनिर्मित दारुमय श्री पुरुषोत्तम भगवान्‌ का श्रीविग्रह समुद्र के तट पर विद्यमान है । हे अमरपद के इच्छुक मनुष्य! तू उनकी शरण में [[जाओर|जाओ और]] उनकी उपासना के [[अमोधघ प्रभावस े|अमोघ प्रभाव से]] सर्वोत्कृष्ट वैष्णव पद को प्राप्त कर ।”  
(ऋग्वेद. १०/१५५/३) [[P71]]

**b)** The most comprehensive narration of the Glory of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas is found in Shree Purushottama-kshetra Mahatmya in Skanda Purana (Vaishnava Khanda). A plain reading of this sacred scripture composed by Maharshi Vedavyasa will make it abundantly clear that the Supreme Lord — the Avatari — referred to in our sacred scriptures as Purushottama, Paramatma, Parameshwara, Parama-Brahman, Bhagavan — has Himself manifested as Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas in Shree Purushottama-kshetra (Puri); and that they are Moola-Vigrahas and not Utsava Vigrahas. It will suffice here to mention only a few narrations from this sacred scripture to establish this point: 
 
**(i)** As revealed by Lord Brahma to the Gods in Brahma-loka, during the first Fifty Years (First Parardha) of Lord Brahma, the Supreme Lord Vishnu was present on top of Neela-parvata (Blue Mountain) in Shree Purushottama-kshetra (Puri) in the divine form of Shree Chaturdha Neelamoni Vigraha Form as Lord Madhava, Lord Sesha Ananta, Devi Mahalakshmi and Chakraraj Sudarshana (Adhyaya 23, shlokas 55 & 56). On account of the prayers of Lord Yamaraja, the Supreme Lord Vishnu gave up His Divine Shree Chaturdha Neelamoni Vigraha Form and re-manifested again in Shree Purushottama-kshetra (Puri) as Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas (Lord Jagannatha, Lord Balabhadra, Devi Subhadra and Chakraraj Sudarshana) on account of the prayers and devotion of Maharaja Indradyumna (Adhyayas 1-4, Adhyaya 14 - Shlokas 41-44). 
 
**(ii)** Upon being installed in the Adi-Jagannatha Temple by Lord Brahma in the Second Manvantara, Lord Jagannatha Himself declared that His Divine Re- [[P72]]

---

manifestation as Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas occurred in the Satya-yuga of the second Chatur-yuga during the First Manvantara of Manu Svayambhuva in the current First Day (Svetvaraha Kalpa) of the Fifty-First year of Lord Brahma. The Lord moreover proclaimed that He will remain in this Chaturdha Daru Vigraha Form till the end of the Second Parardha (second Fifty Years) of Lord Brahma (Adhyaya 29, Shlokas 14-16). This was also been mentioned by Lord Brahma to the Gods in Brahma-loka (Adhyaya 23, Shlokas 57 & 59). 
 
**(iii)** In Adhyaya 1 (Shlokas 35-39) the Supreme Lord Vishnu revealed to Lord Brahma that Lord Madhava on Neela-parvata in Shree Purushottama-kshetra is none other than He Himself -- the Supreme Lord Vishnu. In Adhyaya 2, it is stated that, being directed by Lord Vishnu, Lord Brahma came to Shree Purushottama-kshetra and, while praying before Lord Madhava, witnessed a crow attaining liberation by darshan of Lord Madhava. The prayer of Lord Yamaraja to Lord Madhava (Shlokas 16-30) and the words spoken by Devi Mahalakshmi to Lord Yamaraja (Shlokas 33-42) also establish that Lord Madhava in Shree Purushottama-kshetra was the Supreme Lord Himself. Lord Madhava along with Devi Mahalakshmi, Lord Sesha Ananta and Chakraraj Sudarshana were thus Moola-vigrahas and not Utsava Vigrahas. 
 
**(iv)** Moreover, it is mentioned in Adhyaya 9 that these Divine Chaturdha Neelamoni Vigrahas on Neela-parvata were “day after day worshipped by the Gods led by Lord Brahma, Lord Shiva and Lord Indra” (Shlokas 63-64). During the First Parardha of Lord Brahma, the Supreme Lord Vishnu resided thus in His Chaturdha Neelamoni Vigraha Form in very dense forest on Neela-parvata in Purushottama-kshetra virtually unknown to humanity. There was no Temple of the Lord existing at that time nor were there, quite obviously, any Utsava Vigrahas. 
 
**(v)** As narrated in Adhyaya 14, Lord Brahma revealed to Devrishi Narada that, on account of the prayer of Lord Yamaraja, the Supreme Lord Vishnu in the Divine Form of Chaturdha Neelamoni Vigraha on Neela-parvata became invisible and re-manifested in Shree Purushottama-kshetra as Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas (shlokas 38, 43-44). This disappearance and re-manifestation of [[P73]]

---

Supreme Lord Vishnu was predicted earlier by Devi Mahalakshmi, as narrated in Adhyaya 4 (Shlokas 55-62). 
 
**(vi)** In Adhyaya 15, Devrishi Narada narrates to Maharaja Indradyumna that, just as Supreme Lord Vishnu is present in His Eternal Abode Svetadvipa, He is also present eternally in Shree Purushottama-kshetra (Puri) and that He is the Avatari from whom emanate all Divine Incarnations in the various Yugas (shlokas 24-34) In Adhyaya 31, Maharshi Jaimini tells the assembled Sages that during this Manvantara of Manu Vaivasvata at the end of Dvapara-yuga in the 27^th Chatur-yuga, Lord Krishna and Arjuna came from Dwarka to Shree Purushottama-kshetra (Puri) and worshipped Supreme Lord Jagannatha (Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas) for three days (Shlokas 52-53). 
 
**(vii)** In Adhyaya 23, Lord Brahma reveals to the Gods in Brahma-loka that the Divine Tree which appeared in the Ocean from which Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas manifested was a Divine Hair from the Body of the Supreme Being and that it was indeed “Lord Vishnu who has assumed the Wooden Body for enjoying the Pleasures of the three worlds” (shlokas 60-66). This was also earlier indicated to Maharaja Indradyumna by Devrishi Narada just before the mystical Appearance of the Divine Tree in the Ocean, as narrated in Adhyaya 18 (shlokas 12-16). In Adhayaya 18, Maharshi Jaimini states that the “old carpenter” who made Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas from the Divine Tree was none other than the Supreme Lord Himself (*sakshatnarayanovibhuh*) in the guise of the carpenter (shlokas 44-46). 
 
**(viii)** In Adhayaya 24, the Prayer of the Gods, upon first darshan of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas in Shree Purushottama-kshetra (shlokas 6-25) and the Prayers of Lord Brahma before Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas seated in the Rathas near the Adi Jagannatha Temple (Adhyaya, shlokas 15-66) clearly establish that the Supreme Lord Vishnu Himself manifested as Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas. 
 
**(ix)** Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas were installed in the Adi-Jagannatha Temple by Lord Brahma, as narrated in Adhyaya 27, Shlokas 93-95. Lord Brahma concluded this Divine Installation with these words: [[P74]]

**c)** Each and every sacred incident mentioned in sub-paragraphs (i) to (x) above clearly establish that Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas are Moola Vigrahas and not Utsava Vigrahas.  
 
**d)** **Brahma Purana** and other sacred scriptures also establish that Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas (just as Shree Chaturdha Neelamoni Vigrahas before Them) are verily the Divine Manifestations of Supreme Lord Vishnu Himself. They are therefore Moola-vigrahas and not Utsava Vigrahas. पुरुषाणां सहस्रेषु स [[भवेदुत्तम: पुमानू|भवेदुत्तमः पुमान्]] ।। १७७/२१ ।।  
प्रकृतेः सः परो यस्मात्‌ [[पुरुषादपि STA:|पुरुषादपि चोत्तमः]] ।  
तस्माद्वेदे पुराणे च [[लोकेडस्मिन्‌|लोकेऽस्मिन्]] [[पुरुषोत्तम:|पुरुषोत्तमः]] ।। १७७/२२ ।।  
 
“He who once sees Shree Purushottama with faith is considered to be the best of human beings among thousands. Since He is beyond Prakriti and is also greater than Purusha, therefore, He is known as Purushottama in this mundane world as well as in the Vedas and Puranas.” (Adhyaya 177, shlokas 21 & 22)  
 
**e)** According to long-established tradition and culture of Lord Jagannatha as followed in Shreemandira since many centuries and as recorded in Part 1 of the Record-of-Rights compiled under The Puri Sri Jagannath Temple (Administration) Act, 1952, Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas have their respective Utsava (Chalanti) Vigrahas, such as, Shree Madanamohana, Shree Rama Krushna, Shree Narayana, Shree Dolo-Gobinda, Shree Baadi Narasimha [[P77]]

---

and Shree Lakshmi-Narasimha, who are taken out of the Temple in procession during various festivals throughout the year (pages 214 & 215).  
 
**f)** The fact that Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas are taken out of the Temple in procession on two holy occasions during the year, namely, Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra, does not detract from Their Status as Moola-Vigrahas because this is done only in faithful obedience to the Divine Commandments of Lord Jagannatha Himself, as mentioned earlier. In the absence of the aforesaid Divine Commandments, it would not be permissible to remove Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas from their sanctum-sanctorum as they are Moola-Vigrahas. Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas are firmly installed on the *Ratnasimhasana* (Be-jeweled Throne) through a traditional procedure called “Rundha” which clearly indicates that the Lords are not to be removed from Their Throne during the various sacred festivals of the year except on the aforesaid two most holy occasions. It is pertinent here to recollect the Divine Words of Lord Brahma when, in the Satya-yuga of the Second Manvantara, He installed Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas in the Adi-Jagannatha Temple. As narrated in Adhyaya 27 (shloka 29) of Skanda Purana Shree Purushottama-kshetra Mahatmyam, Lord Brahma prayed before Supreme Lord Jagannatha “to remain firmly steady in the Temple” ([[“Gutdsrhacteaited Wate Berta”|“सुप्रतिष्ठाखिलव्यापिन् प्रासादे सुस्थिरो भव”]]).  
 
**g)** It is a well-established rule of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma and also acknowledged by the ISKCON Scholars that, after sacred installation and consecration, the Moola-Vigrahas are not removed from Their Throne in the sanctum-sanctorum and that on all festive occasions it is the Utsava (Chalanti) Vigrahas that are taken out in the processions. The fact that Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas are taken out in procession on two holy occasions is only because of the Divine Commandments of Lord Jagannatha Himself and, as explained above, it does not in any way alter Their status as Moola-Vigrahas. #### 5. CONCLUSION:  
 
**a)** Snana-yatra of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas is to be performed only on Jyestha Poornima tithi because it is celebrated in commemoration of the Most Holy Manifestation (‘Birthday’) of the Lords on that auspicious tithi during the Satya-yuga of the First Manvantara, as declared in sacred scriptures and proclaimed by Lord Jagannatha Himself. Performance of Snana-yatra of Lord Jagannatha on any other tithi/date will be a blatant violation of the Divine Commandment of the Lord and prescription of sacred scriptures, including Mahapuranas composed by Maharshi Vedavyasa, and a breach of ancient well-established tradition followed since time immemorial at the Moola-Peetha of Lord Jagannatha at Shree Jagannatha-Dham Puri. Just as the “Birthday” of Lord Krishna (Shree Krishna-janmashtami), Lord Rama (Shree Rama-navami), Lord Ganesh (Shree Ganesh Chaturthi) and other Deities of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma are observed throughout the world only on specific tithis prescribed in sacred scriptures, so also the Snana-yatra commemorating the “Birthday” of Lord Jagannatha is to be observed all over the world only on the specific tithi prescribed in sacred scriptures, that is, on Jyestha Poornima and on no other tithi/date during the year.  
 
**b)** As prescribed in sacred scriptures and as is well-established in the tradition of Lord Jagannatha, Anabasara Ritual is to be observed for fifteen days immediately following Snana-yatra, that is, from the night of Jyestha Poornima till Pratipad tithi of Asadha (dark fortnight); during which period darshan by devotees of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas is not permitted. The Nava-jouvan Darshan and Netrotsava Ritual of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas should be performed on Asadha Pratipad tithi.  
 
**c)** As declared in sacred scriptures (including Mahapuranas composed by Maharshi Vedavyasa) and the Divine [[P80]]

---

Commandment of Lord Jagannatha, Ratha-yatra of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas is a nine-days festival (*“navadinatmakayatra”*) commencing on Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya tithi and it is celebrated every year in commemoration of the sojourn of the Lords to their sacred “Birth-place” - Gundicha-Mundupum. Pulling of Rathas must therefore be confined within the auspicious nine days, that is, from Asadha Shukla-paksha Dvitiya to Dashami tithi. It may be pertinent to mention here that, in countries outside India, the practical difficulties in performing Ratha-yatra during the weekdays is well taken into consideration by the fact that at-least one week-end will be available for performing Ratha-yatra within the aforesaid nine-day period.  
 
**d)** Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas, being Moola Vigrahas (and not Utsava Vigrahas), cannot be removed from their Throne/sanctum-sanctorum except on the two holy occasions, namely, Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra — and this is done only in devotional obedience of the Divine Commandments of Lord Jagannatha and the prescription of sacred scriptures, as mentioned herein above.  
 
**e)** The various grounds put forward by ISKCON scholars, such as, *‘deśa-kāla-vibhāgavit’* nyaya, *‘mahājanoyenagatahsapanthāḥ’*, adverse climatic conditions and local circumstances etc do not at all justify violation by ISKCON or any other Organisation of the prescription of sacred scriptures and the Divine Commandments of Lord Jagannatha regarding celebration of Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra on the prescribed tithis.  
 
**f)** While the tithis/dates for performing Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra cannot be altered under any circumstance whatsoever, the manner of celebrating these Most Holy Yatras of Lord Jagannatha can be adapted to suit local conditions and circumstances, as briefly indicated above in Part A (Para 4A). [[P81]]

---

**g)** Shree Jagannatha Ratha-yatra is a most holy religious event commemorating the Divine Visit of Lord Jagannatha to His sacred ‘Birth-place’. It cannot be celebrated as part of a pageant or parade in any political, social or cultural event.  
 
**h)** Besides, Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra, the other festivals of Shree Chaturdha Daru Vigrahas should also be celebrated on the specific tithi prescribed for the festival in the sacred scriptures, especially Skanda Purana.  
 
**i)** As a spiritual-religious Organisation dedicated to the promotion and propagation of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma and with extensive following around the world, ISKCON is expected to honour and abide by the injunctions and prescriptions of sacred scriptures (especially the Mahapuranas composed by Maharshi Vedavyasa) and well-established traditions of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma and to not indulge in any activity in violation of essential prescriptions of sacred scriptures and long tradition. Successful propagation of Krishna Consciousness around the world should be done by upholding the injunctions and prescriptions of sacred scriptures of Sanatana Vaidika Dharma and well-established religious traditions and not by violating them.  
 
**j)** As stated in the scriptures, disobeying Divine Commandments of the Supreme Lord of the Universe proclaimed in sacred scriptures will not be conducive for the welfare of global humanity.  
 
**k)** His Holiness Param Pujyapad Jagadguru Shankaracharya Shreemad Swami Nischalananda Saraswati Maharaj of Shree Goverdhan-Peeth (Puri) has declared his firm decision that Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha all around the world should be [[P82]]

---

performed only on the tithis prescribed in sacred scriptures, as mentioned above. Shreemad Jagadguru Shankaracharya is the highest and most revered religious authority, particularly in matters concerning the worship, tradition and culture of Lord Jagannatha. He is also the Permanent President of Muktimundupa Pundita Sabha which is the highest religious body in matters relating to the worship, tradition and culture of Lord Jagannatha.  
 
**l)** After several years of dialogue and discussions, the Governing Council (Bureau) of ISKCON resolved at its meeting held at Mumbai on [[21%|21^st]] July 2021, that all ISKCON Centres within India will henceforward celebrate Shree Jagannatha Ratha-yatra only within the auspicious period prescribed in the scriptures and tradition. However, ISKCON Temples/Centers both within India and abroad continue to perform Snana-yatra on tithis/dates other than the prescribed tithi of Jyestha-Poornima; while ISKCON Temples/Centres outside India continue to observe Ratha-yatra on tithis/dates not permitted by scriptures and tradition. A list of dates fixed by ISKCON for celebrating Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra during the current year (2025), which are in violation of prescription of scriptures and tradition, is annexed hereto as **Annexure II**.  
 
**m)** The performance of Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha by ISKCON Temples/Centres in violation of the prescription of sacred scriptures and well-established tradition is hurting the religious sentiments of countless devotees around the world.  
 
For the reasons stated above, we urge the ISKCON Governing Body Commission (Mayapur) to adopt appropriate resolution and prohibit celebrations of Snana-yatra and Ratha-yatra of Lord Jagannatha by its Temples/Centres around the world on dates/tithis not prescribed in sacred scriptures, as mentioned herein above. There is nothing further our Scholars wish to add or clarify regarding this issue. As the matter has been pending for a very long time resulting in severe discontent among devotees of Lord Jagannatha around the world, we request you to communicate us your final decision on this very sensitive and vital issue [[P83]]

at your earliest, preferably within a period of one month from the date of this submission by our Scholars.

ବିଷ୍ଣୁଧର୍ମରେ ମଧ୍ୟ ଏହି ପବିତ୍ର ଉତ୍ସବର ତିଥି ନିର୍ଣ୍ଣୟ କରାଯାଇଛି ।  
ଆଷାଢସ୍ୟ ସିତେ ପକ୍ଷେ ଦ୍ଵିତୀୟା ପୁଷ୍ୟା ସଂଯୁତା ।  
ତସ୍ୟା ରଥେ ସମାରୋପ୍ୟ ରାମ ମା ଭଦ୍ରୟା ସହ ।।  
ମହୋତ୍ସବ ପ୍ରବୃତ୍ତୟାଃ ପ୍ରୀଣୟିତ୍ଵା ଦ୍ଵିଜାନ୍ ବହୂନ୍ ।  
ଋକ୍ଷାଭାବେ ତିଥୌ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟା ସଦା ସା ପ୍ରୀତୟେ ମମ ।।  
ସପ୍ତାହଂ ସରସସ୍ତୀରେ ମମ ଯାତ୍ରା ଭବିଷ୍ୟତି ।  
ଅଷ୍ଟମେ ଦିବସେ ସର୍ବାନ୍ ରଥାନ୍ ମାଲ୍ୟୈର୍ବିଭୂଷୟେତ୍ ।।  
ନବମ୍ୟା ମାନୟେଦ୍ ଦେବାନ୍ ତେଷୁ ପ୍ରାତଃ ସମୃଦ୍ଧିମାନ୍ ।  
ଦକ୍ଷିଣාଭିମୁଖୀ ଯାତ୍ରା ବିଷ୍ଣୋରେଷା ସୁଦୁର୍ଲଭା ।  
ଯଥା ପୂର୍ବା ତଥା ଚେୟଂ ଦ୍ଵେ ଚେ ମୁକ୍ତି ପ୍ରଦାୟି କେ ।।

ଅର୍ଥାତ୍‌ - ଆଷାଢ଼ ମାସର ଶୁକ୍ଳା ଦ୍ବିତୀୟା ତିଥିରେ ଶ୍ରୀଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ଦେବଙ୍କ ରଥଯାତ୍ରାର ଅନୁଷ୍ଠାନ କରିବା କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ । ସେହି ତିଥି ଆଷାଢ଼ ମାସର ଶୁକ୍ଳା ଦ୍ବିତୀୟା ତିଥି ପୁଷ୍ୟା ନକ୍ଷତ୍ର ଯୁକ୍ତ ହେଲେ ପ୍ରଶସ୍ତ ହୁଏ । ସୁଭଦ୍ରା, ବଳରାମଙ୍କ ସହିତ ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ଦେବଙ୍କୁ ରଥରେ ଆରୋହଣ କରାଇ ଏହି ଉତ୍ସବ କରାଯାଇଥାଏ । ଯଦି ଏହି ତିଥିରେ ପୁଷ୍ୟା ନକ୍ଷତ୍ରର ଯୋଗ ନଥାଏ, ତେବେ ସୁଦ୍ଧା କେବଳ ଏହାର ଅନୁଷ୍ଠାନ କରିବା କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ । ଏ ସ୍ଥଳେ କେବଳ ତିଥିରହି ପ୍ରାଧାନ୍ୟ । ଅଧିକନ୍ତୁ ନକ୍ଷତ୍ର ଯୋଗ ହେଲେ ବିଶିଷ୍ଟ ଶୁଭ ମାତ୍ର ହୁଏ । ଏହି ଦିନ ନାନାବିଧ ଭକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ଓ ହବ୍ୟ ଭୋଜନ କରାଯାଇଥାଏ । ସୁଭଦ୍ରା ଓ ବଳରାମଙ୍କ ସହିତ ଶ୍ରୀଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ଦେବଙ୍କୁ ରଥରେ ଆରୋହଣ କରାଇ ଯାତ୍ରା କରିବା ବିଧେୟ । ..... ତେଣୁ ସେହି ସାତଦିନ ଏହି ରଥ ତଟରେ ରଖିଦେବେ । ଅଷ୍ଟମ ଦିବସରେ ନାନା ପ୍ରକାର ଭୂଷଣାଦି ଦ୍ଵାରା ରଥ ସଜ୍ଜିତ କରି ନବମ ଦିବସରେ ପୁନର୍ଯାତ୍ରା କରିବାକୁ ହେବ । ବିଷ୍ଣୁଙ୍କର ଦକ୍ଷିଣାଭିମୁଖୀ ଯାତ୍ରା ଦୁର୍ଲଭ, ମୁକ୍ତି ପ୍ରଦାୟକ । ଦ୍ଵିତୀୟା ଦିନ ଯାତ୍ରା କଲେ, ନବମ ଦିନରେ ପୁନର୍ଯାତ୍ରା କଲେ, ତାହା ଏକାଦଶୀ ତିଥିରେ ପଡିବ ।  
 
ଏ ସମ୍ବନ୍ଧେ ପଦ୍ମ ପୁରାଣର ପ୍ରମାଣ ମଧ୍ୟ ଦିଆଯାଇଛି -  
ଆଷାଢସ୍ୟ ଦ୍ଵିତୀୟାୟାଂ ରଥ କୁର୍ଯ୍ୟାତ୍‌ ବିଶେଷତଃ ।  
ଆଷାଢ ଶୁକ୍ଳୈକାଦଶ୍ୟାଂ ଜପ ହୋମ ମହୋତ୍ସବମ୍‌ ।।  
ରଥେସ୍ଥିତଂ ବ୍ରଜନ୍ତଂ ତଂ ମହାବେଦୀ ମହୋତ୍ସବେ ।  
ଯେ ପଶ୍ୟନ୍ତି ମୁଦା ଭକ୍ତ୍ୟା ବାସସ୍ତେଷା ହରେଃ ପଦେ ।।

ନାତଃ ଶ୍ରେୟଃ ପ୍ରଦୋ ବିଷ୍ଣୋରୁତ୍ସବଃ ଶାସ୍ତ୍ର ସମ୍ମତଃ ।।  
 
ଅର୍ଥାତ୍‌ - ଆଷାଢ଼ ମାସର ଶୁକ୍ଳା ଦ୍ବିତୀୟା ତିଥିରେ ଶ୍ରୀଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ଦେବଙ୍କ ରଥଯାତ୍ରାର ଅନୁଷ୍ଠାନ କରିବା କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ । ସେହି ତିଥି ଆଷାଢ଼ ମାସର ଶୁକ୍ଳା ଦ୍ବିତୀୟା ତିଥି ପୁଷ୍ୟା ନକ୍ଷତ୍ର ଯୁକ୍ତ ହେଲେ ପ୍ରଶସ୍ତ ହୁଏ । ସୁଭଦ୍ରା, ବଳରାମଙ୍କ ସହିତ ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ଦେବଙ୍କୁ ରଥରେ ଆରୋହଣ କରାଇ ଏହି ଉତ୍ସବ କରାଯାଇଥାଏ । ଯଦି ଏହି ତିଥିରେ ପୁଷ୍ୟା ନକ୍ଷତ୍ରର ଯୋଗ ନଥାଏ, ତେବେ ସୁଦ୍ଧା କେବଳ ଏହାର ଅନୁଷ୍ଠାନ କରିବା କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ । ଏ ସ୍ଥଳେ କେବଳ ତିଥିରହି ପ୍ରାଧାନ୍ୟ । ଅଧିକନ୍ତୁ ନକ୍ଷତ୍ର ଯୋଗ ହେଲେ ବିଶିଷ୍ଟ ଶୁଭ ମାତ୍ର ହୁଏ । ଏହିଦିନ ନାନାବିଧ ଭକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ଓ ହାବ୍ୟ ଭୋଜନ କରାଯାଇଥାଏ । ସୁଭଦ୍ରା ଓ ବଳରାମଙ୍କ ସହିତ ଶ୍ରୀଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ଦେବଙ୍କୁ ରଥରେ ଆରୋହଣ କରାଇ ଯାତ୍ରା କରିବା ବିଧେୟ । ଏହି ଭାବରେ ସହୁ କରିବାକୁ ହେବ । ଏ ଭାବ ଯଦି ତୁମର ନାହିଁ, ତେବେ ସେ ଭାବ ବିନୋଦିଆ, ଭାବକୁ ନିକଟ ଅଭାବକୁ ଦୂର । ଭାବ ଯଦି ନାହିଁ, ତେବେ ସେ ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ କାଷ୍ଠ ପିତୁଳା ହୋଇଯିବେ । ପରେ ସାତଦିନ ଏହି ରଥ ତଟରେ ରଖିଦେବେ । ଅଷ୍ଟମ ଦିବସରେ ନାନା ପ୍ରକାର ଭୂଷଣାଦି ଦ୍ଵାରା ରଥ ସଜ୍ଜିତ କରି ନବମ ଦିବସରେ ପୁନର୍ଯାତ୍ରା କରିବାକୁ ହେବ । ବିଷ୍ଣୁଙ୍କର ଦକ୍ଷิଣାଭିମୁଖୀ ଯାତ୍ରା ଦୁର୍ଲଭ, ମୁକ୍ତି ପ୍ରଦାୟକ । ଦ୍ଵିତୀୟା ଦିନ ଯାତ୍ରା କଲେ, ନବମ ଦିନରେ ପୁନର୍ଯାତ୍ରା କଲେ ତାହା ଏକାଦଶୀ ତିଥିରେ ପଡିବ ।

**CHAPTER-18**  
**RATHA YATRA**  
 
**(Collected from the discourse delivered by Shreela Shree Gurudev)**  
 
The *tithi* (date & time) of the sacred celebration has been determined in the Puranas related to Bhagavan Vishnu as follows:  
 
१- आषाढस्य सिते पक्षे द्वितीया पुष्यसंयुता ।  
तस्यां रथे समारोप्य रामं मां भद्रया सह ।  
२- [[महोत्सवप्रवृत्त्यर्थ|महोत्सवप्रवृत्त्यर्थं]] प्रीणयित्वा [[द्विजानबहूनू|द्विजान्बहून्]] ।  
[[ऋत्षाभावे|ऋक्षाभावे]] तिथौ कार्या सदा सा प्रीतये मम ।  
[[P88]]

---

३- सप्ताहं सरसस्तीरे मम यात्रा [[भवष्यति|भविष्यति]] ।  
४- अष्टमे दिवसे सर्वान् रथान् माल्यैर्विभूषयेत् ।  
५- नवम्यामानयेद्देवांस्तेषु प्रातः समृद्धिमान् ।  
६- दक्षिणाभिमुखी यात्रा विष्णोरेषा सुदुर्लभा ।  
७- यथा पूर्वा तथा चेयं द्वे च मुक्तिप्रदायिके ।  
 
**(English translation of these above Shlokas is as follows:**  
 
**Verses 1 & 2 -**  
“In the bright fortnight of Āṣāḍha month, on the second day when there is conjunction with the Puṣya star, Balabhadra and I should be placed along with Subhadrā on the Chariot. After having pleased many Brahmins for the purpose of conducting the great festivals, it should be always performed only on that day (second day of the month of Asadha) for My pleasure, even in the absence of the Star”  
 
**Verse-3**  
My Yatra will be performed for seven days near the Indradyumna Sarovara.  
 
**Verse-4**  
On the eighth day of the Yatra, all the Chariots should be decorated with garlands.  
 
**Verse-5**  
On the ninth day in the morning, the Deities should be brought to the chariots with all grandeur.  
 
**Verse-6**  
This south-ward Festive Procession of Lord Vishnu is very rare.  
 
**Verse-7 -**  
As is the previous procession, so is this, and both are bestowers of Liberation.) [[P89]]

---

The Shlokas mentioned above mean that it is mandatory to perform Ratha-yatra of Shree Jagannatha Deb on the second day of the bright fortnight of the Asadha month when there is conjunction with the Pushya Star. The said Shukla Dwitiya Tithi of the month of Asadha if conjuncted with Pushya Star, is the most appropriate time (for celebration of Ratha Yatra). This celebration should be conducted after having placed Lord Jagannatha along with Subhadra and Balaram on the Chariot. In case, the said Shukla Dwitiya Tithi is not conjunct with Pushya Star, it is also mandatory to celebrate the festival (on the said tithi). In this context, only tithi is significant.

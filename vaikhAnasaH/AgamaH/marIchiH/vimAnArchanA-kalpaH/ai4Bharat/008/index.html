008: Adhiṣṭhāna-Bheda-Vidhiḥ

What's in this page?
🐎💪 32%

(Varieties and Dimensions of the Basement Plinth)

This document contains the Sanskrit text, IAST transliteration, and English translation of the 8th Chapter (Aṣṭamaḥ Paṭalaḥ) of the Vimānārcanākalpa (attributed to Sage Marīci, representing the Vaikhānasa school of Vaishnavism).

This chapter outlines the technical designs and proportional measurements of the temple’s basement plinth (adhiṣṭhāna). It defines the eight classical types of basements (Pādabandha, Padmabandha, Puṣpabandha, Śrī-priyāmbuja, Pratibandha, Pratikrama, Kapotabandha, and Śrībandha), detailing the exact allocation of height units (bhāga) to each architectural molding—such as the base slab (upāna or khura), basal molding (jagatī), torus molding (kumuda), fillets (kampa or āliṅga), neck recess (kaṇṭha), large band (mahāpaṭṭī), and corbel slab (kapota). It also describes the guidelines for projections, recesses, and the placement of niches and pilasters.

Section 1: Types and Proportions of the First Four Basements

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ अष्टमः पटलः अधिष्ठानकरण-रूपभेदाः atha aṣṭamaḥ paṭalaḥ adhiṣṭhānakaraṇa-rūpabhedāḥ

पादबन्धं, प्रत्यङ्गबन्धं, हरिहर-हिरण्यगर्भाणां सामान्यकरणं, विशेषविधानं च; यद् द्वारि तत्प्रवृत्ति-तल-निर्गमं स्यात्। पादबन्धं, पद्मबन्धं, पुष्पबन्धं, श्रीप्रियाम्बुजं, प्रतिबन्धं, प्रतिक्रमं, कपोतबन्धं, श्रीबन्धम् इति अष्ट-अधिष्ठानानि भवन्ति। pādabandhaṃ, pratyaṅgabandhaṃ, harihara-hiraṇyagarbhāṇāṃ sāmānyakaraṇaṃ, viśeṣavidhānaṃ ca; yad dvāri tatpravṛtti-tala-nirgamaṃ syāt. pādabandhaṃ, padmabandhaṃ, puṣpabandhaṃ, śrīpriyāmbujaṃ, pratibandhaṃ, pratikramaṃ, kapotabandhaṃ, śrībandham iti aṣṭa-adhiṣṭhānāni bhavanti.

तेषां क्रमेण भागान् वक्ष्यामि - तत्र अधिष्ठान-उत्सेधं त्रयोविंशतिभागं कृत्वा, जन्मतलोपरि एकांश-खुरः, अष्टांशा जगती, समांश-त्रिपट्ट-कुमुदम्, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंशं कण्ठं, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंश-महापट्टिका, एकांश-ऊर्ध्वपट्टिका च; एतत् पादबन्धं स्यात्। teṣāṃ krameṇa bhāgān vakṣyāmi - tatra adhiṣṭhāna-utsedhaṃ trayoviṃśatibhāgaṃ kṛtvā, janmatalopari ekāṃśa-khuraḥ, aṣṭāṃśā jagatī, samāṃśa-tripatta-kumudam, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśa-mahāpaṭṭikā, ekāṃśa-ūrdhvapaṭṭikā ca; etat pādabandhaṃ syāt.

उत्सेधं त्र्यष्टभागं (चतुर्विंशतिभागं) कृत्वा, एकांश-उपानत्, द्व्यंशं पद्मम्, एकांश-कम्पं, षडंशा जगती, पञ्चांश-त्रिवर्ग-कुमुदम्, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंशं कण्ठं, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंश-महापट्टिका, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-कम्पं च; एतत् पद्मबन्धं स्यात्। utsedhaṃ tryaṣṭabhāgaṃ (caturviṃśatibhāgaṃ) kṛtvā, ekāṃśa-upānat, dvyaṃśaṃ padmam, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, ṣaḍaṃśā jagatī, pañcāṃśa-trivarga-kumudam, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśa-mahāpaṭṭikā, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ ca; etat padmabandhaṃ syāt.

उत्सेधं षड्विंशतिभागं कृत्वा, एकांश-खुरः, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-कम्पं, पञ्चांशा जगती च, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-धृक्, एकांशं पद्मं, चतुरश्र-वृत्त-कुमुदं त्रिवर्गं वा इति केचित्; एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंशं कण्ठं, एकांश-कम्पं, एकांशं पद्मं, द्व्यर्धांशा महापट्टिका, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-कम्पं च; एतत् पुष्पबन्धं स्यात्। utsedhaṃ ṣaḍviṃśatibhāgaṃ kṛtvā, ekāṃśa-khuraḥ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, pañcāṃśā jagatī ca, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-dhṛk, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, caturaśra-vṛtta-kumudaṃ trivargaṃ vā iti kecit; ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, dvyardhāṃśā mahāpaṭṭikā, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ ca; etat puṣpabandhaṃ syāt.

English Translation

  • The Eight Varieties of Basements (Adhiṣṭhāna-rūpa-bhedāḥ):

    • I shall describe the designs of the basement plinths (adhiṣṭhāna), including the Pādabandha, Pratyagabandha, and the general and special layouts for temples of Harihara (Siva-Viṣṇu) and Hiranyagarbha (Brahmā). At the doorways, there should be proper projection (nirgama) corresponding to the floor levels.
    • There are eight canonical types of basements: Pādabandha, Padmabandha, Puṣpabandha, Śrī-priyāmbuja, Pratibandha, Pratikrama, Kapotabandha, and Śrībandha.

  • Molding Dimensions for the First Three Plinths:

    • Pādabandha plinth (23 parts):
      • Divide the plinth height (utsedha) into twenty-three equal parts.
      • Above the base ground level (janma-tala), assign: Khura (plinth-heel) = 1 part; Jagatī (basal slab) = 8 parts; Tripatta-kumuda (three-faceted torus) = equal parts (typically 5 or 6 parts); Kampa (fillet) = 1 part; Kaṇṭha (neck recess) = 2 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Mahāpaṭṭī (large band) = 2 parts; Urdhva-paṭṭikā (upper fillet) = 1 part.
    • Padmabandha plinth (24 parts):
      • Divide the plinth height into twenty-four parts.
      • Assign: Upāna (base plinth) = 1 part; Padma (lotus molding) = 2 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Jagatī = 6 parts; Trivarga-kumuda (three-layered torus) = 5 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Kaṇṭha = 2 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Mahāpaṭṭī = 2 parts; Padma = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part.
    • Puṣpabandha plinth (26 parts):
      • Divide the plinth height into twenty-six parts.
      • Assign: Khura = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part; Jagatī = 5 parts; Padma = 1 part; Dhṛk (retaining fillet) = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Caturaśra-vṛtta-kumuda (square-circular torus) = 4 parts (some say three parts); Padma = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part; Kaṇṭha = 2 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Mahāpaṭṭī = 2.5 parts (dvy-ardha-aṃśā); Padma = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part.

Section 2: Proportions of the Remaining Five Basements

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

उत्सेधम् एकविंशतिभागं कृत्वा - एकांश-पादुका, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांशं कण्ठं, एकांशं दलं, त्र्यंशं वृत्त-कुमुदं, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंशं कण्ठं, एकांश-कम्पं, एकांशं पद्मं, द्व्यर्धांशा महापट्टिका, एकांशं पद्मं, एकांश-ऊर्ध्वपट्टिका च इति एतत् श्रीप्रियाम्बुजम्। utsedham ekaviṃśatibhāgaṃ kṛtvā - ekāṃśa-pādukā, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ dalaṃ, tryaṃśaṃ vṛtta-kumudaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, dvyardhāṃśā mahāpaṭṭikā, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśa-ūrdhvapaṭṭikā ca iti etat śrīpriyāmbujam.

उत्सेधम् अष्टादशांशं कृत्वा - एकांश-खुरः, एकांशं पद्मं, षडंशा जगती, पञ्चांशं वृत्त-कुमुदं, एकांशम् आलिङ्गम्, एकांशम् अन्तरितं, द्व्यंशं प्रतिमुखं, एकांशं वाजनं च; एतत् प्रतिबन्धं स्यात्। utsedham aṣṭādaśa-aṃśaṃ kṛtvā - ekāṃśa-khuraḥ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ṣaḍaṃśā jagatī, pañcāṃśaṃ vṛtta-kumudaṃ, ekāṃśam āliṅgam, ekāṃśam antaritaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ pratimukhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ vājanaṃ ca; etat pratibandhaṃ syāt.

उत्सेधम् एकोनविंशतिभागं कृत्वा - एकांश-पादुकं, समांशा (षडंशा) जगती, षडंशं वृत्त-कुमुदं, एकांशम् आलिङ्गम्, एकांशम् अन्तरितं, द्व्यंशं प्रतिमुखं, एकांशं वाजनं (नागवक्त्रसमं प्रत्यवसानम्) च; एतत् प्रतिक्रमं स्यात्। utsedham ekonaviṃśatibhāgaṃ kṛtvā - ekāṃśa-pādukaṃ, samāṃśā (ṣaḍaṃśā) jagatī, ṣaḍaṃśaṃ vṛtta-kumudaṃ, ekāṃśam āliṅgam, ekāṃśam antaritaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ pratimukhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ vājanaṃ (nāgavaktrasamaṃ pratyavasānam) ca; etat pratikramaṃ syāt.

उत्सेधं सप्तविंशतिभागं कृत्वा - द्व्यंशं पादुकं, षडंशं पद्मं, एकांशं कण्ठं, एकांशं दलं, त्र्यंशं वृत्त-कुमुदं (त्रिवर्गं वा इति केचित्), एकांशं दलं, एकांश-कम्पं, द्व्यंशं कण्ठं, एकांश-कम्पं, एकांशम् उपरिदलं, त्र्यंशं कपोतं, एकांशम् आलिङ्गम्, एकांशम् अन्तरितं, द्व्यंशं प्रतिमुखं, एकांशं वाजनं च; एतत् कपोतबन्धं स्यात्। utsedhaṃ saptaviṃśatibhāgaṃ kṛtvā - dvyaṃśaṃ pādukaṃ, ṣaḍaṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ dalaṃ, tryaṃśaṃ vṛtta-kumudaṃ (trivargaṃ vā iti kecit), ekāṃśaṃ dalaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, ekāṃṣam uparidalaṃ, tryaṃśaṃ kapotaṃ, ekāṃśam āliṅgam, ekāṃśam antaritaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ pratimukhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ vājanaṃ ca; etat kapotabandhaṃ syāt.

उत्सेधं द्वात्रिंशद्भागं विभज्य - द्व्यंशम् उपानत्, एकांशं क्षुद्रोपानत्, समांशं पद्मं, एकांशं कण्ठं, एकांशं दलं, चतुरंशं वृत्त-कुमुदं, एकांशं दलं, एकांश-कम्पं, त्र्यंशं कण्ठं, एकांश-कम्पं, एकांशं पद्मं, चतुरंशं कपोतं, एकांशम् आलिङ्गम्, एकांशम् अन्तरितं, द्व्यंशं प्रतिमुखं, एकांशं वाजनं च; एतत् श्रीबन्धं स्यात्। utsedhaṃ dvātriṃśadbhāgaṃ vibhajya - dvyaṃśam upānat, ekāṃśaṃ kṣudropānat, samāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ dalaṃ, caturaṃśaṃ vṛtta-kumudaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ dalaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, tryaṃśaṃ kaṇṭhaṃ, ekāṃśa-kampaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ padmaṃ, caturaṃśaṃ kapotaṃ, ekāṃśam āliṅgam, ekāṃśam antaritaṃ, dvyaṃśaṃ pratimukhaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ vājanaṃ ca; etat śrībandhaṃ syāt.

English Translation

  • Molding Proportions for the Remaining Five Basements:
    • Śrī-priyāmbuja plinth (21 parts):
      • Assign: Pādukā (foot plinth) = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Kaṇṭha = 1 part; Dala (petal molding) = 1 part; Vṛtta-kumuda (circular torus) = 3 parts; Padma = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part; Kaṇṭha = 2 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Mahāpaṭṭī = 2.5 parts; Padma = 1 part; Urdhva-paṭṭikā = 1 part.
    • Pratibandha plinth (18 parts):
      • Assign: Khura = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Jagatī = 6 parts; Vṛtta-kumuda = 5 parts; Āliṅga = 1 part; Antarita = 1 part; Pratimukha (facing mold) = 2 parts; Vājana = 1 part.
    • Pratikrama plinth (19 parts):
      • Assign: Pāduka = 1 part; Jagatī = 6 parts; Vṛtta-kumuda = 6 parts; Āliṅga = 1 part; Antarita = 1 part; Pratimukha = 2 parts; Vājana (resembling a cobra’s hood at its slope/termination) = 1 part.
    • Kapotabandha plinth (27 parts):
      • Assign: Pāduka = 2 parts; Padma = 6 parts; Kaṇṭha = 1 part; Dala = 1 part; Vṛtta-kumuda = 3 parts (some say three layers); Dala = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part; Kaṇṭha = 2 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Upari-dala (upper petal) = 1 part; Kapota (corbel drip-molding) = 3 parts; Āliṅga = 1 part; Antarita = 1 part; Pratimukha = 2 parts; Vājana = 1 part.
    • Śrībandha plinth (32 parts):
      • Assign: Upāna = 2 parts; Kṣudropānat (minor plinth) = 1 part; Padma = 4 parts (equal parts); Kaṇṭha = 1 part; Dala = 1 part; Vṛtta-kumuda = 4 parts; Dala = 1 part; Kampa = 1 part; Kaṇṭha = 3 parts; Kampa = 1 part; Padma = 1 part; Kapota = 4 parts; Āliṅga = 1 part; Antarita = 1 part; Pratimukha = 2 parts; Vājana = 1 part.

Section 3: Projections, Recesses, and Niches

Verse / Paragraph 1

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

एतेषु नासिका-अङ्घ्रीन् युक्त्या योजयेत्। पादबन्धे जन्मादि-पञ्चवर्गेषु तत्तदवसाने द्वारस्थलं सङ्कल्पयेत्; अन्येषु पट्टिकान्ते च एवं युक्त्या कारयेत्। प्रत्यङ्गेषु प्रतेः उपर्युपरि द्वारस्थलं सङ्कल्पयेत्, प्रतिच्छेदं न कुर्यात्, कुर्यात् चेत् विनाशाय भवति।

Transliteration (IAST):

eteṣu nāsikā-aṅghrīn yuktyā yojayet. pādabandhe janmādi-pañcavargeṣu tattadavasāne dvārasthalaṃ saṅkalpayet; anyeṣu paṭṭikānte ca evaṃ yuktyā kārayet. pratyaṅgeṣu prateḥ uparyupari dvārasthalaṃ saṅkalpayet, praticehedaṃ na kuryāt, kuryāt cet vināśāya bhavati.

Translation (English):

  • Rules for Pilasters, Niches, and Doors:
    • Arrange the niches (nāsikā) and pilasters (aṅghri) systematically.
    • In the Pādabandha basement, position the doorway relative to the five moldings starting from the base plinth (janmādi-pañca-varga). For other styles, design it at the top of the fillet moldings (paṭṭikā-ante) using similar logic.
    • In the secondary moldings (pratyaṅga), determine the doorway position above the top-most fillet (prati). Never break or cut the moldings (praticchedaṃ na kuryāt); doing so leads to the ruin of the temple (vināśāya bhavati).

Verse / Paragraph 2

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

सर्वेषाम् अङ्गानां प्रवेशनिर्गमम् एकदण्डादि-त्रिदण्डान्तं पाददण्डवृद्ध्या विमान-विस्तार-बाह्याङ्ग-निर्गमं नवविधं स्यात्। एतेषु इष्टप्रमाणेना उपानद्विस्तारं, तच्च त्रि-चतुः-पञ्च-षट्-सप्त-अष्टांशं कृत्वा एकांशं खुर-निर्गमं, शेषं जगतीविस्तारं, तत्समं कुमुदं, तत्समं तद्बाह्यात् कण्ठप्रवेशं; सर्वेषां पट्टिका-अम्बुज-कम्पादीनां तत्तदुत्सेध-समनिर्गमं; वेत्राणाम् उत्सेधं त्रि-चतुः-पञ्च-षडंशं कृत्वा एकांशं निर्गमम्; एवं युक्त्या कारयेत्।

Transliteration (IAST):

sarveṣām aṅgānāṃ praveśanirgamam ekadaṇḍādi-tridaṇḍāntaṃ pādadaṇḍavṛdhyā vimāna-vistāra-bāhyāṅga-nirgamaṃ navavidhaṃ syāt. eteṣu iṣṭapramāṇena upānadvistāraṃ, tacca tri-catuḥ-pañca-ṣaṭ-sapt-aṣṭāṃśaṃ kṛtvā ekāṃśaṃ khura-nirgamaṃ, śeṣaṃ jagatīvistāraṃ, tatsamaṃ kumudaṃ, tatsamaṃ tadbāhyāt kaṇṭhapraveśaṃ; sarveṣāṃ paṭṭikā-ambuja-kampādīnāṃ tattadutsedha-samanirgamaṃ; vetrāṇām utsedhaṃ tri-catuḥ-pañca-ṣaḍaṃśaṃ kṛtvā ekāṃśaṃ nirgamam; evaṃ yuktyā kārayet.

Translation (English):

  • Projections and Recession Module Units:
    • The projection and recession (praveśa-nirgama) of all moldings should range from one to three modules (daṇḍa), incrementing by quarters of a module (pāda-daṇḍa), offering nine varieties of projections relative to the outer boundary of the temple’s width.
    • The width of the base plinth (upāna) is divided into 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, or 8 parts:
      • Assign one part for the projection of the Khura mold.
      • The remaining part equals the width of the Jagatī molding.
      • The Kumuda molding is equal to the Jagatī width.
      • The recess of the Kaṇṭha (neck) is aligned with the outer face of those moldings.
    • For all fillets (paṭṭikā), lotus molds (ambuja), and rills (kampa), their projection should equal their height (utsedha-sama-nirgamam).
    • For the reed moldings (vetra), divide their height into 3, 4, 5, or 6 parts, and assign one part for their projection. One should execute all measurements intelligently in this manner.

Colophon

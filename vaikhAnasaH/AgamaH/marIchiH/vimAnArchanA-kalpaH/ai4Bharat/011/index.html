011: Prākāra-Gopurādi-Vidhiḥ

What's in this page?
🐎💪 32%

(Rules for Enclosures, Gateways, and Retinue Shrines)

This document contains the Sanskrit text, IAST transliteration, and English translation of the 11th Chapter (Ekādaśaḥ Paṭalaḥ) of the Vimānārcanākalpa (attributed to Sage Marīci, representing the Vaikhānasa school of Vaishnavism).

This chapter details the architectural and ritual rules for constructing enclosure walls (prākāra), gateway towers (gopura), retinue shrines (parivārālaya), and various auxiliary structures (such as the great pavilion mahāmaṇḍapa, dance hall nṛtta-maṇḍapa, bath-house snapanāgāra, kitchen pacanālaya, and sacrificial hall yāgāśālā).

Section 1: Enclosure Walls (Prākāra-vidhiḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ एकादशः पटलः प्राकारविधिः atha prākāravidhiṃ vakṣye - rakṣārthaṃ maṅgalārthaṃ śobhārthaṃ ca; tat prāsāda-ardhaṃ, tripādaṃ, samaṃ, pādādhikaṃ, adhyardhādhikaṃ vā prathamāvaraṇam. tatsamaṃ, pādādhikaṃ, adhyardhādhikaṃ, pādona-dviguṇaṃ, dviguṇaṃ, triguṇaṃ, caturguṇaṃ, pañcaguṇaṃ vā dvitīyāvaraṇam. tasya ukta-pramāṇeṣu iṣṭamānena tṛtīyāvaraṇādi sapta-prākārāntaṃ kārayet.

अथ प्राकारविधिं वक्ष्ये - रक्षार्थं मङ्गलार्थं शोभार्थं च; तत् प्रासाद-अर्धं, त्रिपादं, समं, पादाधिकं, अध्यर्धाधिकं (अर्धाधिकं) वा प्रथमावरणम्। तत्समं, पादाधिकं, अध्यर्धाधिकं (अर्धाधिकं), पादोन-द्विगुणं, द्विगुणं, त्रिगुणं, चतुर्गुणं, पञ्चगुणं वा द्वितीयावरणम्। तस्य उक्त-प्रमाणेषु इष्ट-मानेन तृतीयावरणादि सप्त-प्राकारान्तं कारयेत्। prāsādaṃ paritaḥ caturaśrīkṛtya, tat-pādādhikam adhyardhādhikaṃ pādona-dviguṇaṃ dviguṇaṃ dviguṇa-ardhaṃ triguṇa-ardhaṃ caturguṇaṃ vā mukha-āyāmaṃ prathamāvaraṇasya; tat-paritaḥ caturaśrīkṛtya eva dvitīyāvaraṇādi-sarva-prākārāṇāṃ mukha-āyāmaṃ prayoktavyam. athavā sarva-prākārāṇāṃ prāsādaṃ paritaḥ caturaśrīkṛtya evaṃ mukha-āyāmaṃ yojayed iti kecit.

प्रासादं परितः चतुरश्रीकृत्य, तत्-पादाधिकम् अध्यर्धाधिकं (मर्धाधिकं) पादोन-द्विगुणं, द्विगुणं, द्विगुण-अर्धं, त्रिगुण-अर्धं, चतुर्गुणं वा मुख-आयामं प्रथमावरणस्य; तत्-परितः चतुरश्रीकृत्य एव द्वितीयावरणादि-सर्व-प्राकाराणां मुख-आयामं प्रयोक्तव्यम्। अथवा सर्व-प्राकाराणां प्रासादं परितः चतुरश्रीकृत्य एवं मुख-आयामं योजयेत् इति केचित्। hasta-mānaṃ tridhā proktam - adhamaṃ, madhyamaṃ, uttamam iti. yathākrasmeṇa - tri-pañca-saptahastam adhama-trayaṃ; nava-ekādaśa-trayodaśahastaṃ madhyama-trayaṃ; pañcadaśa-saptadaśa-ekonaviṃśatihastam uttama-trayam. evam eva sapta-prākārāntaṃ kārayet. teṣāṃ mukha-āyāmaṃ pūrvavat.

हस्त-मानं त्रिधा प्रोक्तम् - अधमं, मध्यमं, उत्तमम् इति। यथाक्रमेण - त्रि-पञ्च-सप्त-हस्तम् अधम-त्रयं; नव-एकादश-त्रयोदश-हस्तं मध्यम-त्रयं; पञ्चदश-सप्तदश-एकोनविंशति-हस्तम् उत्तम-त्रयम्। एवम् एव सप्त-प्राकारान्तं कारयेत्। तेषां मुख-आयामं पूर्ववत्। pramukhe dakṣiṇe paścime uttare vā vistīrṇe deśe tattaddiśi bāhya-prākāra-sama-āyāmaṃ, tatsamaṃ tripādam ardhaṃ vā vistāram aṅkaṇaṃ kṛtvā tasmin āsthāna-maṇḍapaṃ kārayet.

प्रमुखे दक्षिणे पश्चिमे उत्तरे वा विस्तीर्णे देशे तत्तद्दिशि बाह्य-प्राकार-सम-आयामं, तत्समं त्रिपादम् अर्धं वा विस्तारम् अङ्कणं कृत्वा तस्मिन् आस्थान-मण्डपं कारयेत्। ālaya-abhimukhe vā uttare vā bahūdakaṃ vistīrṇaṃ taṭākaṃ kṛtvā, tatparitaḥ śilayā iṣṭakayā vā tīraṃ kārayet. tat-tīre manorme vā āsthāna-maṇḍapaṃ kuryāt iti kecit.

आलय-अभिमुखे वा उत्तरे वा बहूदकं विस्तीर्णं तटाकं कृत्वा, तत्परितः शिलया इष्टकया वा तीरं कारयेत्। तत्-तीरे मनोरमे वा आस्थान-मण्डपं कुर्यात् इति केचित्। taṭākasya paścime tīre tīrtha-sthānaṃ prāṅmukhaṃ tri-pañca-sama-nava-ekādaśahastaiḥ samāśram āyatāśraṃ vā, yugma-yugma-aṅghri-bhittikaṃ maṇḍapaṃ kūṭaṃ prapāṃ vā kuryāt. tad-bāhye prākāraṃ kārayet iti kecit.

तटाकस्य पश्चिमे तीरे तीर्थ-स्थानं प्राङ्मुखं त्रि-पञ्च-सम-नव-एकादश-हस्तैः सम-अश्रम् आयत-अश्रं वा, युग्म-युग्म-अङ्घ्रि-भित्तिकं मण्डपं कूटं प्रपां वा कुर्यात्। तद्-बाह्ये प्राकारं कारयेत् इति केचित्। eka-prākāram adhamaṃ, dvi-prākāraṃ madhyamaṃ, tri-prākāram uttamaṃ syāt. tri-prākāraṃ madhyamādhamaṃ, catuṣprākāraṃ madhyamamadhyamaṃ, pañca-prākāraṃ madhyamottamaṃ syāt. pañca-prākāram uttamādhamaṃ syāt, ṣaṭ-prākāram uttamamadhyamaṃ, sapta-prākāram uttamottamaṃ syāt.

एक-प्राकारम् अधमं, द्वि-प्राकारं मध्यमं, त्रि-प्राकारम् उत्तमं स्यात्। त्रि-प्राकारं मध्यमाधमं, चतुष्प्राकारं मध्यममध्यमं, पञ्च-प्राकारं मध्यमोत्तमं स्यात्। पञ्च-प्राकारम् उत्तमाधमं स्यात्, षट्-प्राकारम् उत्तममध्यमं, सप्त-प्राकारम् उत्तमोत्तमं स्यात्। prākārāṇāṃ bhitti-viṣkambhaṃ hastaṃ, pādādhikaṃ, adhyardhādhikaṃ, pādona-dviguṇaṃ, dviguṇaṃ vā. tat-tri-catuṣ-pañca-ṣaḍ-sama-aṣṭa-nava-guṇitaṃ bhitti-utsedhaṃ vistārād aṣṭamāṃśa-ūnam agra-vipulaṃ prāsādasya pādād vā, prāgupānād vā; prākāra-bhitti-antaram uttamaṃ bhitti-madhye madhyamaṃ, bhitti-bāhye ca adhamam. teṣāṃ pramukha-dvāraṃ gopuraṃ kṛtvā, adhastād dvāri mārgaṃ prācyāṃ, udīcyāṃ vā; dakṣiṇa-uttara-paścimeṣu kṣudra-dvāraṃ ca yuktitāḥ kārayet. eteṣāṃ bahir-āvaraṇa-pṛṣṭhe’pyevaṃ dvāraṃ gopuraṃ vā kuryāt; prathamāvaraṇaṃ vinā anyeṣāṃ kārayet iti eke.

प्राकाराणां भित्ति-विष्कम्भं हस्तं, पादाधिकं, अध्यर्धाधिकं (मर्धाधिकं), पादोन-द्विगुणं, द्विगुणं वा। तत्-त्रि-चतुष्-पञ्च-षड्-सम-अष्ट-नव-गुणितं भित्ति-उत्सेधं विस्ताराद् अष्टमांश-ऊनम् अग्र-विपुलं प्रासादस्य पादाद् वा, प्रागुपानाद् (प्राडु पानाद्) वा; प्राकार-भित्ति-अन्तरम् उत्तमं भित्ति-मध्ये मध्यमं, भित्ति-बाह्ये च अधमम्। तेषां प्रमुख-द्वारं गोपुरं कृत्वा, अधस्ताद् द्वारि मार्गं प्राच्यां, उदीच्यां (मुदीच्यां) वा; दक्षिण-उत्तर-पश्चिमेषु क्षुद्र-द्वारं च युक्तितः कारयेत्। एतेषां बहिर-आवरण-पृष्ठेऽप्येवं द्वारं गोपुरं वा कुर्यात्; प्रथमावरणं विना अन्येषां कारयेत् इति एके।

English Translation

  • Enclosure Walls (Prākāra-vidhiḥ):
    • Now, I shall describe the rules for enclosure walls (prākāra), built for protection (rakṣārtha), auspiciousness (maṅgalārtha), and beauty (śobhārtha).
    • First Enclosure (Prathamāvaraṇa): Its distance from the temple should be 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, or 1.5 times the temple width (prāsāda-vistāra).
    • Second Enclosure (Dvitīyāvaraṇa): Its distance should be equal to the first enclosure, or 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 3, 4, or 5 times that measurement.
    • Using these prescribed proportions and desired dimensions, one may construct up to seven enclosures (sapta-prākāra).
    • Front Extension (Mukhāyāma): Making the area around the temple square, the front length of the first enclosure should be increased by 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5, 3.5, or 4 times. Using the same square layout, design the front length of the second and subsequent enclosures. Alternatively, some say the front length of all enclosures should be planned symmetrically around the temple square.
    • Hand-Module Measurements (Hasta-māna): The module measurements are threefold: inferior (adhama), middling (madhyama), and superior (uttama).
      • Inferior (three types): 3, 5, or 7 hands.
      • Middling (three types): 9, 11, or 13 hands.
      • Superior (three types): 15, 17, or 19 hands.
      • Build up to seven enclosures using these modules. Their front lengths are determined as before.
    • Courtyard and Pavilions:
      • In the front, South, West, or North, in a spacious area, make a courtyard (aṅkaṇa) whose length is equal to the outer enclosure, and width is equal to, or 3/4, or 1/2 of that length. In it, construct an assembly hall (āsthāna-maṇḍapa).
      • Facing the temple or in the North, dig a spacious tank (taṭāka) with abundant water, and build its banks with stone or brick. Some say a beautiful assembly hall (āsthāna-maṇḍapa) should be built on its bank.
      • On the western bank of the tank, build a bathing place (tīrtha-sthāna) facing East. Construct a square or rectangular pavilion (maṇḍapa), pillared shrine (kūṭa), or water booth (prapā) measuring 3, 5, 7, 9, or 11 hands, with walls supported by pairs of pillars. Some say an enclosure wall should be built outside it.
    • Grades of Enclosures:
      • A single enclosure (eka-prākāra) is inferior (adhama); two enclosures are middling (madhyama); three enclosures are superior (uttama).
      • Further classifications: three enclosures = superior-inferior (madhyamādhama); four enclosures = middling-middling (madhyamamadhyama); five enclosures = middling-superior (madhyamottama); five enclosures = superior-inferior (uttamādhama); six enclosures = superior-middling (uttamamadhyama); seven enclosures = superior-superior (uttamottama).
    • Enclosure Wall Dimensions:
      • The wall width (bhitti-viṣkambha) should be 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, or 2 hands.
      • The height of the wall (bhitti-utsedha) should be 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9 times its width, with the top width tapered by 1/8.
      • The distance of the enclosure wall from the temple should be measured from the pillar foot (prāsādasya pādād vā) or from the plinth (prāg-upānād vā).
      • A measurement starting from the inner face of the wall is superior (uttama); from the middle of the wall is middling (madhyama); from the outer face of the wall is inferior (adhama).
      • Provide a gateway tower (gopura) at the main entrance, with the passage below. In the East, North, South, West, or where appropriate, construct smaller side doors (kṣudra-dvāra) using reasoning.
      • Gateways or towers can also be built on the back of the outer enclosures. Some say this should be done for all enclosures except the first one.

Section 2: Masonry and Pillar Guidelines (Bhitti-stambha-lakṣaṇam)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

तद् नवधा कृत्वा - द्विभागम् अधिष्ठानं, समांशं पादायामं; मानाङ्गुलेन षष्ट्यङ्गुलाद् ऊर्ध्वं षडङ्गुल-वृद्ध्या अष्टोत्तरशताङ्गुलान्तं स्तम्भोच्चं नवधा भवति; समाङ्गुलं समारभ्य एकाङ्गुल-वृद्ध्या पञ्चदशाङ्गुलान्तं नवविधं पाद-विष्कम्भं; सार्ध-द्विहस्ताद् ऊर्ध्वं पाद-वृद्ध्या सार्ध-चतुष्करान्तं नवधा भित्ति-मानम्। उत्तराणां परितः प्राक्-पश्चिमयोः उत्तराग्रं, दक्षिण-उत्तरयोः प्रागग्रं संयोज्य तस्य उपरितलादीनि सन्यसेत्; एवम् एव शिलाजं च आस्तरेत्; ऊर्ध्वं लुपाभिः सञ्छादयेत् इति केचित्। शिला-स्तम्भोपरि दारु-स्तम्भं प्रयोक्तव्यं, दारु-स्तम्भोपरि शिला-स्तम्भं न योजयेत्। tad navadhā kṛtvā - dvibhāgam adhiṣṭhānaṃ, samāṃśaṃ pādāyāmaṃ; mānāṅgulena ṣaṣṭyaṅgulād ūrdhvaṃ ṣaḍaṅgula-vṛddhyā aṣṭottara-śatāṅgulāntaṃ stambhoccaṃ navadhā bhavati; samāṅgulaṃ samārabhya ekāṅgula-vṛddhyā pañcadaśāṅgulāntaṃ navavidhaṃ pāda-viṣkambhaṃ; sārdha-dvihastād ūrdhvaṃ pāda-vṛddhyā sārdha-catuṣkarāntaṃ navadhā bhittimānam. uttarāṇāṃ paritaḥ prāk-paścimayor uttarāgraṃ, dakṣiṇa-uttarayoḥ prāgagraṃ saṃyojya tasya uparitalādīni sanyaset; evam eva śilājaṃ ca āstaret; ūrdhve lupābhiḥ sañchādayet iti kecit. śilā-stambhopari dāru-stambhaṃ prayoktavyaṃ, dāru-stambhopari śilā-stambhaṃ na yojayet.

अथवा तन्मुखायामं युग्म-भित्ति-समायुक्तं द्वारादिकं नानोत्तराणि सन्यस्य, तस्य उपरि प्रागन्तम् उत्तरान्तं च तुलादीनि आस्तरेत्। द्वारे द्वारशोभादि-भेदेषु एकमार्गेण गोपुरं कारयेत्। अथवा अधिष्ठान-अन्ते द्वारं हित्वा, द्वार-बाह्ये च उभयोः पार्श्वयोः अभ्यन्तर-भित्तिवशाद् एकभक्ति-युक्तं सोपानं कारयेत्। तद्वत् स्तम्भ-तुलादीनि युग्मं च संयोजयेत्। अत्र अनुक्तं सर्वं शिल्पशास्त्रोक्त-मार्गेण कुर्यात्॥ athavā tanmukhāyāmaṃ yugma-bhitti-samāyuktaṃ dvārādikaṃ nānottarāṇi sanyasya, tasya upari prāgantam uttarāntaṃ ca tulādīni āstaret. dvāre dvāraśobhādi-bhedeṣu ekamārgeṇa gopuraṃ kārayet. athavā adhiṣṭhāna-ante dvāraṃ hitvā, dvāra-bāhye ca ubhayoḥ pārśvayoḥ abhyantara-bhittivaśād ekabhakti-yuktaṃ sopānaṃ kārayet. tadvat stambha-tulādīni yugmaṃ ca saṃyojyet. atra anuktaṃ sarvaṃ śilpaśāstroktamārgeṇa kuryāt.

English Translation

  • Structural Details for Gateways (Bhitti-stambha-lakṣaṇam):
    • Divide the height into nine parts: 2 parts for the basement (adhiṣṭhāna), and the remaining parts for the pillars (pāda).
    • Pillar Height (Stambhocca): Measured in mānāṅgula (standard digits), from 60 digits up to 108 digits, increasing by 6 digits, yielding nine height varieties.
    • Pillar Width (Pāda-viṣkambha): Starting from a specific digit, increasing by 1 digit up to 15 digits, yielding nine width options.
    • Wall Thickness (Bhitti-māna): From 2.5 hands, increasing by 0.25 hand up to 4.5 hands, yielding nine wall thickness options.
    • Beams and Roofs (Uttara): Connect the beams (uttara) around the structure such that the East-West beams extend over the North-South ones, and the North-South ones project Eastward. Above them, place the upper tiers. Lay the stone slab roof in the same manner. Some say the top should be covered with rafters (lupā).
    • Material Rule: A wooden pillar (dāru-stambha) may be placed above a stone pillar (śilā-stambha), but a stone pillar must never be placed above a wooden pillar.
    • Alternatively, design the front length of the gateway with double walls, construct the door frame, place various beams, and over them lay the rafters and cross-beams (tulā) extending to the eastern and northern ends.
    • For the entrance doorway, build a gateway tower (gopura) using one of the styles like dvāra-śobhā.
    • Alternatively, at the level of the basement, leave the doorway open and on both outer sides build steps (sopāna) aligned with the inner walls, featuring a single offset (eka-bhakti).
    • Symmetrically arrange the pillars, cross-beams, and other elements in pairs.
    • Anything not specified here should be executed according to the canonical manuals of architecture (shilpa-śāstra).

Section 3: Gateway Towers (Gopura-lakṣaṇam)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ गोपुराणां लक्षणं वक्ष्ये - द्वारशोभा, द्वारशाला, द्वारप्रासादं, द्वारहर्म्यं, द्वारगोपुरम् इति पञ्चधा भवन्ति। atha gopurāṇāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ vakṣye - dvāraśobhā, dvāraśālā, dvāraprāsādaṃ, dvāraharmyaṃ, dvāragopuram iti pañcadhā bhavanti.

English Translation

  • Gateway Towers (Gopura-lakṣaṇam):
    • Now, I shall describe the characteristics of gateway towers (gopura). They are of five types in ascending scale:
      1. Dvāra-śobhā (doorway beauty - 1 story)
      2. Dvāra-śālā (doorway hall - 2 stories)
      3. Dvāra-prāsāda (doorway mansion - 3 stories)
      4. Dvāra-harmya (doorway palace - 4 stories)
      5. Dvāra-gopura (doorway tower - 5 stories or more)
    • Width Proportions relative to the Temple Width:
      • For Abhāsa class (small): Divide the width of the main temple (mūla-prāsāda) into 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 parts; the width of the gateway should be one part less than those divisions.
      • For Vikalpa class (medium): Divide the temple width into 7, 8, 9, 10, or 11 parts, and subtract 1 part. Divide it into 4, 5, 6, 7, or 8 parts, subtract 1 part, and assign the remaining parts for the width of middling gateway towers.
      • For Uttama class (large): Assign 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, or 4/5 of the temple width for the superior gateway towers.
    • Absolute Measurements in Hands:
      • Kṣudra (inferior): From 2 hands up to 16 hands, increasing by 1 hand, yielding 15 varieties for the different types of gateways.
      • Alpa (small): From 3 hands up to 31 hands, increasing by 2 hands, yielding 15 varieties.
      • Madhyama (middling): From 9 hands up to 37 hands, increasing by 2 hands, yielding 15 varieties.
      • Uttama (superior) widths for the five types:
        • Dvāra-śobhā: 5 hands to 13 hands, increasing by 2 hands (5 varieties).
        • Dvāra-śālā: 15 hands to 23 hands, increasing by 2 hands.
        • Dvāra-prāsāda: 25 hands to 33 hands, increasing by 2 hands.
        • Dvāra-harmya: 35 hands to 43 hands, increasing by 2 hands.
        • Dvāra-gopura: 49 hands to 53 hands, increasing by 2 hands.
        • This provides a total of 25 width varieties across the five classes of gateways.
    • Length/Depth (Yāma): The depth of the gateway should be 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, 1, or 1.25 times its width, yielding five varieties.
    • Height and Decoration: The basement moldings, decoration, and other features of the towers should be planned for one to twelve stories (ekatalādyarkatalāntam) following the rules established for the main temple.
    • Doorway Plinth and Aperture:
      • The plinth should be of pādabandha type.
      • Doorway Width (Dvāra-viṣkambha): 1.5, 1.75, 2.0, 2.25, 2.5, 2.75, or 3.0 hands, yielding seven varieties. The height of the opening should be twice its width.
      • Alternatively, the height of the doorway opening should be equal to the height of the basement and pillar of the ground floor combined, and its width should be half of that height.
      • Alternatively, the doorway width can range from a base of 2 hands, increasing by 2 digits up to 21 digits, yielding ten varieties.
      • Projection (Nirgama): The projection of the gateway towers beyond the enclosure wall should be 1/2, 1/3, or 2/5 of its width. All other details should follow the rules of the main temple.

अथ परिवाराणाम् आलयं गर्भव्यास-समं, पाद-हीनं, अर्धं वा; अथवा त्रि-हस्तं, पञ्च-हस्तं वा मुखमण्डप-सहितं कृत्वा स्थापयेत्॥ atha parivārāṇām ālayaṃ garbhavyāsa-samaṃ, pāda-hīnaṃ, ardhaṃ vā; athavā tri-hastaṃ, pañca-hastaṃ vā mukhamanḍapa-sahitaṃ kṛtvā sthāpayet.

परिवाराणां ध्रुव-कौतुक-संयुक्तम् उत्तमं; कौतुकं विना तत्-स्थाने पीठं कृत्वा अर्चयेन् मध्यमम्; आलयं ध्रुव-कौतुकं च विना यथोक्ते देशे शैलानि ऐष्टकानि (शैलान्यैष्टकानि) वा, मानाङ्गुलेन मात्राङ्गुलेन वा, द्वादश-अङ्गुलोत्सेधानि तद्-द्विगुण-आयत-विस्ताराणि त्रि-वेदि-सहितानि पीठानि कुर्यात्। अथवा एक-वेदिकं यथा-लाभ-प्रमाणेन पीठं कृत्वा कारयेत् इति केचित्। एतद् अधम-मार्गेण अर्चनं स्यात्; अन्यथा महत्तरो दोषो भवति। परिवाराणाम् आलयं मुखमण्डपं कूटं वा प्राकार-आश्रयम् एव कर्तव्यम्; पीठं चेद् आलय-आश्रयम् एव कारयेत् इति एके॥ parivārāṇāṃ dhruva-kautuka-saṃyuktam uttamam; kautukaṃ vinā tat-sthāne pīṭhaṃ kṛtvā arcayen madhyamam; ālayaṃ dhruva-kautukaṃ ca vinā yathokte deśe śailāni aiṣṭakāni vā, mānāṅgulena mātrāṅgulena vā, dvādaśa-aṅgulotsedhāni tad-dviguṇa-āyata-vistārāṇi tri-vedi-sahitāni pīṭhāni kuryāt. athavā eka-vedikaṃ yathā-lābha-pramāṇena pīṭhaṃ kṛtvā kārayet iti kecit. etad adhama-mārgeṇa arcanaṃ syāt; anyathā mahattaro doṣo bhavati. parivārāṇām ālayaṃ mukhamanḍapaṃ kūṭaṃ vā prākāra-āśrayam eva kartavyam; pīṭhaṃ ced ālaya-āśrayam eva kārayet iti eke.

अथ आलय-अभिमुखे महामण्डपं मूलालय-समं, अध्यर्धं (मध्यर्धं), द्विगुण-अर्धं, त्रिगुणं, त्रिगुण-अर्धं, चतुर्गुणम् इति समचतुरश्रम् आयतचतुरश्रं वा, युग्म-पाद-युग्म-भक्तिकं मूलधाम्नादि-भूमि-प्रमाणेन अधिष्ठान-पाद-प्रस्तराणि कृत्वा सञ्छाद्य मध्ये सभां युक्त्या कल्पयेत्। atha ālaya-abhimukhe mahāmaṇḍapaṃ mūlālaya-samaṃ, adhyardhaṃ, dviguṇa-ardhaṃ, triguṇaṃ, triguṇa-ardhaṃ, caturguṇam iti samacaturaśram āyatacaturaśraṃ vā, yugma-pāda-yugma-bhaktikaṃ mūladhāmnādi-bhūmi-pramāṇena adhiṣṭhāna-pāda-prastarāṇi kṛtvā sañchādya madhye sabhāṃ yuktyā kalpayet.

English Translation

  • Shrines for Retinue Deities (Parivārālaya-vidhiḥ):

    • Now, regarding the shrines for the retinue deities (parivāra-devatās):
    • The width of these shrines should be equal to, 3/4, or 1/2 of the main sanctum’s width (garbha-vyāsa); or absolutely 3 hands or 5 hands wide, built with a front porch (mukha-maṇḍapa).
    • Grades of Worship:
      • Superior (Uttama): Having both the immovable icon (dhruva-bera) and the portable festival icon (kautuka-bera).
      • Middling (Madhyama): Without the portable icon, worshipping the deity on a pedestal (pīṭha) built in its place.
      • Inferior (Adhama): Without both a separate shrine and a portable icon, installing stone or brick pedestals in the designated spots. These pedestals should be measured in standard or proportionate digits, 12 digits high, twice that in length and width, and have three tiers (tri-vedi). Alternatively, some say a single-tiered pedestal (eka-vedika) can be made according to available dimensions. This is the inferior mode of worship. Acting otherwise results in severe defects (mahattaro doṣaḥ).
    • The retinue shrines, front porches, or pillared cells (kūṭa) should be built adjoining the enclosure wall. If only a pedestal is made, it should be built attached to the main temple wall, as some say.

  • The Great Pavilion (Mahāmaṇḍapa-vidhiḥ):

    • In front of the temple, build the great pavilion (mahāmaṇḍapa).
    • Its width should be 1, 1.5, 2.5, 3, 3.5, or 4 times the width of the main shrine.
    • It can be square or rectangular, supported by even numbers of pillars (yugma-pāda) and divided into even bays (yugma-bhakti).
    • Construct its basement, pillars, and entablature in proportion to the main shrine’s dimensions, cover it with a roof, and design an assembly hall (sabhā) in the center using reasoning.

तत्प्रमुखे तथैव नृत्तमण्डपं, आग्नेयादि-कोणेषु तथैव आस्थान-मण्डपं कारयेत्। प्रथमावरणे द्वितीये वा मालिका-विहीने (वामाधिलिकाविहीने) च आलय-अभिमुखे, मण्डप-पूर्वे सोपान-पूर्वे वा स्नपनागारं मण्डपं कूटं वा, अनेकद्वारयुतम् (नेकद्वारयुतं) एकद्वारयुतं वा कृत्वा; tatpramukhe tathaiva nṛttamaṇḍapaṃ, āgneyādi-koṇeṣu tathaiva āsthāna-maṇḍapaṃ kārayet. prathamāvaraṇe dvitīye vā mālikā-vihīne ca ālaya-abhimukhe, maṇḍapa-pūrve sopāna-pūrve vā, snapanāgāraṃ, maṇḍapaṃ, kūṭaṃ vā, anekadvārayutam ekadvārayutaṃ vā kṛtvā;

द्वितीयावरणे तृतीये वा, आग्नेय्यां पचनालयं विमान-समं, पादाधिकं, अध्यर्धाधिकं (मर्धाधिकं), पादोन-द्विगुणं, द्विगुणं, द्विगुण-अर्धं, त्रिगुणम् इति विस्तारं सप्तधा भवति। विस्तारात् पादाधिकम् अध्यर्धाधिकं (मर्धाधिकं) वा आयामं चतुरश्रं चतुःशालोपेतं (चतुरश्रतुः शालोपेतं); तन्मध्ये गर्भं (रूपं/गृहं), तद्-दक्षिणतो मूलधाम-पादुकादि-प्रस्तरान्तोत्सेधं चतुर्था कृत्वा - एकांशम् अधिष्ठानं सोपपीठं वा, द्व्यंशं पादवर्गं, एकांशं (मेकांशं) प्रस्तरं; प्राकार-अभ्यन्तरं प्राकार-मध्यं प्राकार-बाह्यं वा अस्य भित्ति-विष्कम्भं हस्तमात्रं, पादहीनं, पादाधिकं वा, मण्डपाकारं लुपा-युक्तं वा कारयेत्। dvitīyāvaraṇe, tṛtīye vā, āgneyyāṃ pacanālayaṃ vimāna-samaṃ, pādādhikaṃ, adhyardhādhikaṃ, pādona-dviguṇaṃ, dviguṇaṃ, dviguṇārdhaṃ, triguṇam iti vistāraṃ saptadhā bhavati. vistārāt pādādhikam adhyardhādhikaṃ vā āyāmaṃ caturaśraṃ catuḥ-śālopetaṃ; tan-madhye garbhaṃ, tad-dakṣiṇato mūladhāma-pādukādivā-prastarāntotsedhaṃ caturthā kṛtvā - ekāṃśam adhiṣṭhānaṃ sopapīṭhaṃ vā, dvyaṃśaṃ pādavargaṃ, ekāṃśaṃ prastaraṃ; prākāra-abhyantaraṃ prākāra-madhyaṃ prākāra-bāhyaṃ vā asya bhitti-viṣkambhaṃ hastamātraṃ, pādahīnaṃ, pādādhikaṃ vā, maṇḍapa-ākāraṃ lupā-yuktaṃ vā kārayet.

तद्-द्वार-दक्षिणे पूर्ववत् प्रथमेष्टकाः शिलाः वा संस्थाप्य तथैव अन्ते च स्थापयेत्। सुधाकर्म कृत्वा, अभ्यन्तरे प्राच्यां दक्षिणादि-उत्तरान्तं चुल्लीः युग्म-अयुग्म-सङ्ख्याः (संख्या रव्य); दक्षिण-शाला-मध्ये नित्य-अग्निकुण्डं, पश्चिम-शाला-मध्ये सोपान-द्वारम्, उत्तरतो हविर्द्रव्य-उपसञ्चय-स्थानम् (हविर्द्रव्यो पसंचय स्थानम्), अधस्ताद् दक्षिणतः प्राच्यां वा वारि-मार्गं च कारयेत्। प्रथमावरणे द्वितीये वा दक्षिणे होम-स्थानं कारयेत् इति केचित्। tad-dvāra-dakṣiṇe pūrvavat prathama-iṣṭakāḥ śilāḥ vā saṃsthāpya tathaiva ante ca sthāpayet. sudhā-karma kṛtvā, abhyantare prācyāṃ dakṣiṇādi-uttarāntaṃ cullīḥ yugma-ayugma-saṅkhyāḥ; dakṣiṇa-śālā-madhye nitya-agni-kuṇḍaṃ; paścima-śālā-madhye sopāna-dvāram; uttarato haviḥ-dravya-upasañcaya-sthānam; adhastād dakṣiṇataḥ prācyāṃ vā vāri-mārgaṃ ca kārayet. prathamāvaraṇe dvitīye vā dakṣiṇe homa-sthānaṃ kārayet iti kecit.

यागशालां द्वितीयावरणे आग्नेय्यां वा पञ्चहस्तादि-नवहस्त-आयत-विस्तारं चतुर्द्वारयुतं मण्डपं कूटं वा कुर्यात्। yāgaśālāṃ dvitīyāvaraṇe āgneyyāṃ vā pañcahastād-navahasta-āyata-vistāraṃ caturdvārayutaṃ maṇḍapaṃ kūṭaṃ vā kuryāt.

अत्र अनुक्तं सर्वं शिल्पशास्त्रोक्त-विधिना कारयेत्॥ atra anuktaṃ sarvaṃ śilpaśāstroktavidhinā kārayet.

English Translation

  • Other Pavilions and Kitchen (Nṛtta-maṇḍapa-pacanālaya-vidhiḥ):
    • In front of the great pavilion, build the dance hall (nṛtta-maṇḍapa). In the South-East and other corners, build assembly halls (āsthāna-maṇḍapa) in the same manner.
    • In the first or second enclosure, where there is no cloister walk (mālikā-vihīne), facing the temple, in front of the pavilion or steps, build a bath-house (snapanāgāra), which can be a pavilion or a square cell, having one door or multiple doors.
    • The Kitchen (Pacanālaya):
      • In the second or third enclosure, in the South-East (āgneyyāṃ), construct the temple kitchen (pacanālaya).
      • Kitchen Width (7 options): Equal to the temple width (vimāna-samam), or 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5, or 3 times that width.
      • The length should be 1.25 or 1.5 times the width, forming a square or rectangular structure, and it should be built as a catuḥ-śālā (four-sided structure around a central courtyard).
      • In its center, make the kitchen chamber (garbha).
      • Divide the vertical height (from plinth up to the entablature of the main temple) into four parts: 1 part for the basement (adhiṣṭhāna) with or without a sub-plinth (upapīṭha), 2 parts for the pillars (pāda-varga), and 1 part for the entablature (prastara).
      • The kitchen wall thickness should be 1 hand, 3/4 hand, or 1.25 hands. It can be built inside the enclosure wall, aligned with the middle of the wall, or outside the wall. It should be built in the shape of a pavilion and equipped with rafters (lupā).
      • To the right of its doorway, perform the foundation stone-laying ceremony (prathameṣṭakā) with bricks or stone as described before, and also at the top/end of construction.
      • Apply plaster work (sudhā-karma).
      • Internal Layout:
        • In the East, install the ovens/hearths (cullīḥ) from South to North in even or odd numbers.
        • In the middle of the southern wing (dakṣiṇa-śālā), place the daily fire pit (nityāgni-kuṇḍa).
        • In the middle of the western wing, place the door with steps (sopāna-dvāra).
        • In the North, place the storehouse for offering items (havir-dravya-upasañcaya-sthāna).
        • At the bottom, in the South or East, construct a drain/water outlet (vāri-mārga).
      • Some say a sacrificial fire place (homa-sthāna) should be made in the South, in the first or second enclosure.
    • The Sacrificial Hall (Yāgāśālā):
      • In the second enclosure, in the South-East, build the sacrificial hall (yāgāśālā) as a pavilion or a square cell measuring from 5 hands to 9 hands in length and width, equipped with four doors.
    • Anything not specified here should be executed according to the canonical manuals of architecture (shilpa-śāstra).

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

इति श्रीवैखानसे मरीचिप्रोक्ते विमानार्चनाकल्पे प्राकारगोपुरादि-विधिर्नाम एकादशः पटलः ॥११॥

iti śrīvaikhānase marīciprokte vimānārcanākalpe prākāragopurādi-vidhirnāma ekādaśaḥ paṭalaḥ ||11||

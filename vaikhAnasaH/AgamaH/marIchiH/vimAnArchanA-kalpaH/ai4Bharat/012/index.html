012: Pariṣad-devatā-sthāna-nirṇayaḥ

What's in this page?
🐎💪 32%

(Rules for the Placements of Retinue Deities)

This document contains the Sanskrit text, IAST transliteration, and English translation of the 12th Chapter (Dvādaśaḥ Paṭalaḥ) of the Vimānārcanākalpa (attributed to Sage Marīci, representing the Vaikhānasa school of Vaishnavism).

This chapter outlines the precise locations, placements, and structural guidelines for the retinue deities (pariṣad-devatā or parivāra-devatā), altars (pīṭha), flagstaff (dhvaja), and protective guardians within the various enclosures (prākāra) of the temple complex.

Section 1: Placements of Flagstaff, Altars, and Storage Rooms (Dhvaja-balipīṭha-bhāga-vibhāgaḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ द्वादशः पटलः परिवारस्थाननिर्णयः atha dvādaśaḥ paṭalaḥ parivārasthānanirṇayaḥ

अथ परिवारस्थानं वक्ष्ये - मुखमण्डप-सोपान-मध्ये श्रीभूतं; प्रथमावरण-अग्र-आयामं चतुर्था कृत्वा, सर्वत्रापि तृतीयांशे श्रीभूत-गरुडस्य चक्र-ध्वज-शङ्ख-महाभूत-अक्षहन्तॄन् क्रमेण परिकल्पयेत्। अथवा द्वितीयावरणे द्वितीयांशे गरुडं, तृतीयांशे चक्रं; तृतीयावरणे द्वितीयांशे शङ्खं, तृतीयांशे ध्वजम्; चतुर्थावरणे तृतीयांशे यूथाधिपं; पञ्चमावरणे तृतीयांशे अक्षहन्तारम्। अथवा गोपुराद् बाह्ये प्रासादस्य अर्ध-सम-आयामं नीत्वा तत्रैव अक्षहन्तुः पीठं परिकल्पयेत्। अथवा सर्वत्र प्रथमावरणाद्यङ्गणस्य अग्र-आयामं चतुर्था कृत्वा, विमान-पार्श्वादि-द्वितीयांशे गरुडं, तृतीयांशे बलिपीठं च कल्पयेत् इति केचित्। atha parivārasthānaṃ vakṣye - mukhamanḍapa-sopāna-madhye śrībūtaṃ; prathamāvaraṇa-agra-āyāmaṃ caturthā kṛtvā, sarvatrāpi tṛtīyāṃśe śrībūta-garuḍasya cakra-dhvaja-śaṅkha-mahābhūta-akṣahantṝn krameṇa parikalpayet. athavā dvitīyāvaraṇe dvitīyāṃśe garuḍaṃ, tṛtīyāṃśe cakraṃ; tṛtīyāvaraṇe dvitīyāṃśe śaṅkhaṃ, tṛtīyāṃśe dhvajam; caturthāvaraṇe tṛtīyāṃśe yūthādhipaṃ; pañcāvaraṇe tṛtīyāṃśe akṣahantāram. athavā gopurād bāhye prāsādasya ardha-sama-āyāmaṃ nītvā tatraiva akṣahantuḥ pīṭhaṃ parikalpayet. athavā sarvatra prathamāvaraṇādyaṅgaṇasya agra-āyāmaṃ caturthā kṛtvā, vimāna-pārśvādi-dvitīyāṃśe garuḍaṃ, tṛtīyāṃśe balipīṭhaṃ ca kalpayet iti kecit.

परिवाराणां आलयं, पचनालयं, पुष्प-सञ्चय-स्थानं, उदक-पानीय-स्थानं, नृत्तमण्डपं च मूलधाम-प्रेक्षं; स्नपन-मण्डपम्, आस्थान-मण्डपं, प्राकारं, गोपुरं च बहिर्मुखं; विमान-गर्भगृहम् अभ्यन्तर-मुखं ज्ञात्वा एव मारभेत॥ parivārāṇām ālayaṃ, pacanālayaṃ, puṣpa-sañcaya-sthānaṃ, udaka-pānīya-sthānaṃ, nṛttamaṇḍapaṃ ca mūladhāma-prekṣaṃ; snapana-maṇḍapam, āsthāna-maṇḍapaṃ, prākāraṃ, gopuraṃ ca bahirmukhaṃ; vimāna-garbhagṛham abhyantara-mukhaṃ jñātvā eva mārabheta.

प्राद्वारे देवतागारे प्रथमावरणे द्वितीये वा इन्द्र-ईशानयोः अन्तरे (वैन्द्रेशान्योरन्तरे) स्नान-उदक-पानीय-पुष्प-सञ्चय-स्थानं, प्लोत-वस्त्र-उत्तरीय-गन्ध-ताम्बूल-आद्यर्चना-द्रव्य-सञ्चय-स्थानं (तांबूलोद्यर्चनाद्रव्यसंचयस्थानं) च; ऐन्द्र-अग्न्योः मध्ये शय्यादेशम्; उत्तरद्वारं चेत् सोम-वायव्ययोः (सोमोदानयोः) मध्ये स्नान-उदकादि-द्रव्य-सञ्चय-स्थानं, सोम-ईशानयोः मध्ये शय्यास्थानं च; सर्वत्रापि महानसम् आग्नेयम् एव कारयेत्॥ prāgdvāre devatāgāre prathamāvaraṇe dvitīye vā indra-īśānayor antare snāna-udaka-pānīya-puṣpa-sañcaya-sthānaṃ, plota-vastra-uttarīya-gandha-tāmbūla-ādyarcana-dravya-sañcaya-sthānaṃ ca; indra-agnyor madhye śayyādeśam; uttaradvāraṃ cet soma-vāyavyayor madhye snāna-udakādi-dravya-sañcaya-sthānaṃ, soma-īśānayor madhye śayyāsthānaṃ ca; sarvatrāpi mahānasam āgneyam eva kārayet.

English Translation

  • Placements of Flagstaff, Altars, and Storage Rooms:
    • Now, I shall describe the placements of the retinue shrines and structures (parivāra-sthāna).
    • Place Śrībūta in the middle of the front porch steps (mukha-maṇḍapa-sopāna).
    • Divide the front length of the first enclosure into four parts: in the third part, construct in order the altars/posts of Śrībūta, Garuḍa, the Cakra (discus), the Dhvaja (flagstaff), the Śaṅkha (conch), Mahābhūta, and Akṣahantar.
    • Alternatively, in the second enclosure, assign the second part for Garuḍa, the third part for the Cakra; in the third enclosure, the second part for the Śaṅkha, the third part for the Dhvaja; in the fourth enclosure, the third part for Yūthādhipa; and in the fifth enclosure, the third part for Akṣahantar.
    • Alternatively, extend a distance equal to half the temple width outside the gateway tower (gopura), and construct the altar of Akṣahantar there.
    • Alternatively, divide the front length of the first enclosure courtyard into four parts: place Garuḍa in the second part from the temple side, and the Balipīṭha (offering altar) in the third part, as some say.
    • Orientations:
      • The shrines of the retinue deities, the kitchen (pacanālaya), the flower storage (puṣpa-sañcaya-sthāna), the water storage (udaka-pānīya-sthāna), and the dance hall (nṛtta-maṇḍapa) must face the main shrine (mūladhāma-prekṣaṃ).
      • The bath-house (snapana-maṇḍapa), the assembly hall (āsthāna-maṇḍapa), the enclosure walls (prākāra), and the gateway towers (gopura) must face outwards (bahirmukham).
      • The inner sanctum (garbhagṛha) of the temple must be understood as facing inwards (abhyantara-mukham), and thus the construction should begin.
    • Auxiliary Placements:
      • For East-facing temples (prāgdvāre): In the first or second enclosure, between East (Indra) and North-East (Īśāna), place the storage area for bath water, drinking water, and flowers; and the storage for fabrics, upper garments, sandal-paste, betel leaves, and other worship materials. Between East (Indra) and South-East (Agni), place the bedchamber (śayyā-deśam).
      • For North-facing temples (uttaradvāraṃ cet): Between North (Soma) and North-West (Vāyavya), place the storage for bath-water and materials. Between North (Soma) and North-East (Īśāna), place the bedchamber.
      • In all layouts, place the kitchen (mahānasam) in the South-East (āgneyam) only.

Section 2: Placements of Retinue Deities in Enclosures (Pariṣad-devatā-sthāna-vibhāgaḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

परिषद्देवानां स्थानं वक्ष्ये - pariṣaddevānāṃ sthānaṃ vakṣye -

  • गर्भगृहे/गर्भगेहे: ध्रुवबेरस्य पादयोः मध्ये विष्णुः; तत्र प्रागादि-ईशानान्तं (तत्रागादीशनान्तं) पुरुष-कपिल-सत्य-यज्ञ-अच्युत-नारायण-अनिरुद्ध-पुण्याश्च।

  • द्वितीयावरणे: वाराह-सुभद्र-नारसिंह-ईशितात्म-वामन-सर्वोद्भव-त्रिविक्रम-सर्वविद्येश्वराश्च।

  • तृतीयावरणे: इन्द्र-अग्नि-यम-नैरृत-वरुण-वायु-कुबेर-ईशानाश्च; दक्षिणे श्रीः, वामे हरिणी च।

  • कर्मार्चा-स्थाने: सुभद्र-सर्वव्यात्मक-सुखावह-रामदेव-संवह-पुण्यदेव-सुवहाश्च।

  • द्वितीयावरणे (कर्मार्चायाः): मित्र-सनातन-अत्रि-सनन्दन-शिव-सनत्कुमार-विश्व-सनकाः।

  • तृतीयावरणे (कर्मार्चायाः): इन्द्रादयः; दक्षिणे भित्ति-पार्श्वे पितामह-पुण्यौ, वामे गङ्गाधर-प्रजापती च।

  • द्वारेषु: धाता-विधातृ-भुजङ्ग-पतङ्गपति-वरुणाश्च; न्यक्षादि-विमानपालाः शिखाग्रे स्तूप्यधस्तात् प्रागादि-चतुर्दिक्षु बहिर्मुखाः आसीनाश्च। अधस्ताद् अधिष्ठान-वर्गं परितः प्रागादि न्यक्ष-विवस्वन्-मित्र-क्षत्तारः पीठेषु समर्चनीयाः। मणिक-सन्ध्योः (मनिकसंध्ये) अभ्यन्तर-द्वारे मुखमण्डप-द्वारे च विखनः-तापसौ।

  • प्रथमावरणादि-सप्रप्राकार-द्वारेषु १८ द्वारपालाः: किष्किन्ध-तीर्थौ, विघ्नेश-नागेशौ, शङ्ख-पद्मौ, तुहिण-बलिन्दौ, नन्दक-शार्ङ्गौ, चक्रचूलि-शङ्खचूलिनौ, चण्ड-प्रचण्डौ च इति एतान् अष्टादश द्वारपालान् अर्चयेत्।

  • सोम-ईशानयोः अन्तरे: विष्वक्सेनम्; प्रथमावरण-आद्यङ्कणे चतुर्भागे द्विभागे श्रीभूत-गरुड-चक्र-ध्वज-शङ्ख-यूथाधिप-अक्षहन्तॄन् एतान् अनपायिनश्च तत्तत्-सूत्रे संयोज्य एव अर्चयेत् इति केचित्। ** garbhagehe: dhruvaberasya pādayor madhyaye viṣṇuḥ; tatra prāgādi-īśānāntaṃ puruṣa-kapila-satya-yajña-acyuta-nārāyaṇa-aniruddha-puṇyāś ca.* ** dvitīyāvaraṇe: vārāha-subhadra-nārasiṃha-īśitātma-vāmana-sarvodbhava-trivikrama-sarvavidyeśvarāś ca.* ** tṛtīyāvaraṇe: indra-agni-yama-nairṛta-varuṇa-vāyu-kubera-īśānāś ca; dakṣiṇe śrīḥ, vāme hariṇī ca.* ** karmārcā-sthāne: subhadra-sarvavyātmake-sukhāvaha-rāmadeva-saṃvaha-puṇyadeva-suvahāś ca.* ** dvitīyāvaraṇe (karmārcāyāḥ): mitra-sanātana-atri-sanandana-śiva-sanatkumāra-viśva-sanakāḥ.* ** tṛtīyāvaraṇe (karmārcāyāḥ): indrādayaḥ; dakṣiṇe bhitti-pārśve pitāmaha-puṇyau, vāme gaṅgādhara-prajāpatī ca.* ** dvāreṣu: dhātā-vidhātā-bhujaṅga-pataṅgapati-varuṇāś ca; nyakṣādi-vimānapālāḥ śikhāgre sthūpyadhastāt prāgādi-caturdikṣu bahirmukhāḥ āsīnāś ca. adhastād adhiṣṭhāna-vargaṃ paritaḥ prāgādi nyakṣ-vivasvan-mitra-kṣattāraḥ pīṭheṣu samarcanīyāḥ. maṇika-sandhyor abhyantara-dvāre mukhamanḍapa-dvāre ca vikhanah-tāpasau.* ** prathamāvaraṇādi-sapraprākāra-dvāreṣu 18 dvārapālān: kiṣkindha-tīrthau, vighneśa-nāgeśau, śaṅkha-padmau, tuhiṇa-balindau, nandaka-śārṅgau, cakracūli-saṅkhacūlinau, caṇḍa-pracaṇḍau ca iti etān aṣṭādaśa dvārapālān arcayet.* ** soma-īśānayor antare: viṣvaksenam; prathamāvaraṇa-ādyaṅgaṇe caturbhāge dvibhāge śrībhūta-garuḍa-cakra-dhvaja-śaṅkha-yūthādhipa-akṣahantṝn etān anapāyinaś ca tattatsūtre saṃyojya eva arcayet iti kecit.*

  • प्रथमावरणे द्वितीये वा:

  • प्रागादि-ईशानान्तं इन्द्रादीन् प्राग्द्वारादि-द्वार-दक्षिण-पार्श्वेषु कोणेषु च।

  • प्राग्द्वाराद् उत्तरे भास्करम्, इन्द्राद् दक्षिणाद्यन्तराले चन्द्र-अङ्गारक-बुध-बृहस्पति-काव्य-मन्द-राहु-केतून् ग्रहांश्च।

  • तृतीयावरणे:

  • गङ्गा-यमुना-नर्मदा-सिन्धवः प्रागादि-चतुर्दिक्षु तत्तद्-द्वार-दक्षिण-पार्श्वे।

  • पूर्व-द्वार-उत्तरे दुर्गा, आग्नेयादिषु कोणेषु सिद्धि-श्री-ज्येष्ठा-धात्रीश्च।

  • अन्तराले सुधासुन्दरी-स्वाहा-स्वधा-माया-संह्लादिनी-राका-सिनीवाल्यश्च।

  • पचनालये हविः-रक्षकं; पुष्पमण्डपे पुष्प-रक्षकं; वारि-सञ्चय-स्थान-पार्श्वे पुरुषं; प्लोत-वस्त्र-उत्तरीयादि-द्रव्य-सञ्चय-स्थान-पार्श्वे त्वष्टारं; शय्यास्थाने कौमोदकीं; हविर्द्रव्यादि-सञ्चय-स्थाने सर्वेश्वरं; होमस्थाने बलिरक्षकम् अर्चयेत्।

  • चतुर्थावरणे:

  • पूर्व-द्वार-उत्तरे गविष्ठं, दक्षिणे पृथिवीं, पश्चिमे [वरुणं], दक्षिणे [यमं], उत्तरे वायुं च।

  • आग्नेयादिषु कोणेषु ऋग्-यजुः-साम-अथर्वणश्च।

  • अन्तराले शिवं, विष्णुं, तक्षकं, शुभं, भूतनायकं, पवित्रं, पावनं, क्षितिजं च।

  • पञ्चमावरणे:

  • पूर्व-द्वार-उत्तरे दक्षिणे चक्र-ह्री-अनुमतीः; दक्षिणे ब्राह्मण्याद्याः सप्त मातृः; पश्चिमे जयाद्याः अप्सरसश्च; उत्तरे कुण्डिन्याद्याः सप्त रोहिणीश्च (सप्तरोहिणीश्च); आग्नेयादिषु कोणेषु वत्सरादीन् प्राणान् (प्राणादीन्) मरुतः; शतरुद्रान् अन्तराले वा।

  • ईशान-इन्द्रयोः अन्तराले अजैकपादं, एकादश-रुद्रादीन्; इन्द्र-अग्न्योः अन्तराले धात्रादि-द्वादश-आदित्यान्; यम-अग्न्योः अन्तराले गन्धर्वान्; यम-नैरृतयोः (यमनीलयोः) मध्ये मुनीन्; नैरृत-वरुणयोः (नीलवरुणयोः) मध्ये अमृतजान्; वरुण-वाय्वोः (वरुणोदानोः) मध्ये अश्विनौ; सोम-वाय्वोः (सोमोदानोः) मध्ये विद्याधरांश (विद्याधराग्र्यान्)।

  • षष्ठावरणे:

  • प्राग्द्वार-उत्तरे तुम्बुरुं, तद्दक्षिणे नारदं, दक्षिणे ऋतून्, पश्चिमे प्रजापतिम्, उत्तरे मुद्गलम्, आग्नेय्यां हलेशं, नैर्ऋत्यां यक्षं, वायव्यां जाम्बवन्तं, ऐशान्यां सुरमम्।

  • अन्तराले प्राच्याम् उत्तरे किन्नर-मिथुनं, तद्-दक्षिणे प्रह्लादं, दक्षिणे कामं रतिं (कामंविपां) च, पश्चिमे व्यालिनीं (व्याजिनीं) कामिनीं च, उत्तरे प्रभां सुन्दरीं च।

  • सप्तमावरणे:

  • पूर्व-द्वार-उत्तरे ब्रह्माणं, तद्-दक्षिणे अर्यमणं, दक्षिणे दण्डभृतम्, उत्तरे धनदम्; आग्नेयादिषु कोणेषु सवितृ-सावित्रौ, इन्द्र-इन्द्रानुजौ, रुद्र-रुद्रानुजौ, अप-अपवत्सौ च।

  • अन्तराले प्रागादि-चतुर्दिक्षु ईशादि-पददेवान् क्रमेण कारयेत्। ** prathamāvaraṇe dvitīye vā:* ** prāgādi-īśānāntaṃ indrādīn prāgdvārādi-dvāra-dakṣiṇapārśveṣu koṇeṣu ca.* ** prāgdvārād uttare bhāskaram, indrād dakṣiṇādyantarāle candra-aṅgāraka-budha-bṛhaspati-kāvya-manda-rāhu-ketūn etān grahāṃś ca.* ** tṛtīyāvaraṇe:* ** gaṅgā-yamunā-narmadā-sindhavaḥ prāgādi-caturdikṣu tattaddvāra-dakṣiṇapārśve.* ** pūrvadvārottare durgā, āgneyādiṣu koṇeṣu siddhi-śrī-jyeṣṭhā-dhātrīś ca.* ** antarāle sudhāsundarī-svāhā-svadhā-māyā-saṃhlādinī-rākā-sinīvālayas ca.* ** pacanālaye haviḥ-rakṣakaṃ; puṣpamaṇḍape puṣpa-rakṣakaṃ; vārisañcayasthānapārśve puruṣaṃ; plotavastrottarīyādidravyasañcayasthānapार्स्वे tvaṣṭāraṃ; śayyāsthāne kaumodakīṃ; havirdravyādisañcayāsthāne sarveśvaraṃ; homasthāne balirakṣakam arcayet.* ** caturthāvaraṇe:* ** pūrvadvārottare gaviṣṭhaṃ, dakṣiṇe pṛthivīṃ, paścime [varuṇaṃ], dakṣiṇe [yamaṃ], uttare vāyuṃ ca.* ** āgneyādiṣu koṇeṣu ṛg-yajuḥ-sam-atharvaṇaś ca.* ** antarāle śivaṃ, viṣṇuṃ, takṣakaṃ, śubhaṃ, bhūtanāyakaṃ, pavitraṃ, pāvanaṃ, kṣitijaṃ ca.* ** pañcamāvaraṇe:* ** pūrvadvārottare dakṣiṇe cakra-hrī-anumātīḥ; dakṣiṇe brāhmaṇyādyāḥ sapta mātṛḥ; paścime jayādyāḥ apsarasas ca; uttare kuṇḍinyādyāḥ sapta rohiṇīś ca; āgneyādiṣu koṇeṣu vatsarādīn prāṇān marutaḥ; śatarudrān antarāle vā.* ** īśāna-indrayor antarāle ajaikāpadaṃ, ekādaśa-rudrādīn; indra-agnyor antarāle dhātrādi-dvādaśa-ādityān; yama-agnyor antarāle gandharvān; yama-nairṛtayor madhye munīn; nairṛta-varuṇayor madhye amṛtajān; varuṇa-vāyvor madhye aśvinau; soma-vāyvor madhye vidyādharāṃś ca.* ** ṣaṣṭhāvaraṇe:* ** prāgdvārottare tumburuṃ, taddakṣiṇe nāradaṃ, dakṣiṇe ṛtūn, prajāpatim, uttare mudgalam, āgneyyāṃ haleśaṃ, nairṛtyāṃ yakṣaṃ, vāyavyāṃ jāmbavantaṃ, aiśānyāṃ suramam.* ** antarāle prācyām uttare kinnara-mithunaṃ, tad-dakṣiṇe prahlādaṃ, dakṣiṇe kāmaṃ ratiṃ ca, paścime vyālinīṃ kāminīṃ ca, uttare prabhāṃ sundarīṃ ca.* ** saptamāvaraṇe:* ** pūrvadvārottare brahmāṇaṃ, tad-dakṣiṇe aryamaṇaṃ, dakṣiṇe daṇḍabhṛtam, uttare dhanadam; āgneyādiṣu koṇeṣu savitṛ-sāvitriyau, indra-indrānujau, rudra-rudrānujau, apa-apavatsau ca.* ** antarāle prāgādi-caturdikṣu īśādi-padadevān krameṇa kārayet.*

अत्र अनुक्तान् सर्वान् बाह्ये महत्पीठं कृत्वा “सर्वे देवाः” इति सङ्कल्प्य अर्चयेत्। एतेषाम् अनुक्तं सर्वं भृगूक्त-विधिना कारयेत्। atra anuktān sarvān bāhye mahatpīṭhaṃ kṛtvā “sarve devāḥ” iti saṅkalpya arcayet. eteṣām anuktaṃ sarvaṃ bhrigūkta-vidhinā kārayet.

English Translation

  • Placements of Retinue Deities in Enclosures:
    • In the Inner Sanctum (Garbhageha):
      • Place Viṣṇu in the middle of the feet of the immovable main icon (dhruva-bera).
      • From the East to the North-East (prāgādi-īśānāntam), place the forms: Puruṣa, Kapila, Satya, Yajña, Acyuta, Nārāyaṇa, Aniruddha, and Puṇya.
    • In the Second Enclosure (Dvitīyāvaraṇa):
      • Place Varāha, Subhadra, Nārasiṃha, Īśitātman, Vāmana, Sarvodbhava, Trivikrama, and Sarvavidyeśvara.
    • In the Third Enclosure (Tritīyāvaraṇa):
      • Place the guardians of directions: Indra (East), Agni (South-East), Yama (South), Nirṛti (South-West), Varuṇa (West), Vāyu (North-West), Kubera (North), and Īśāna (North-East).
      • Place Goddess Śrī on the right (South) and Hariṇī (Bhumidevi) on the left (North).
    • For the Worship/Festival Icon (Karmārcā):
      • Place Subhadra, Sarvavyātman, Sukhāvaha, Rāmadeva, Saṃvaha, Puṇyadeva, and Suvaha.
      • In the second enclosure for the karmārcā, place Mitra, Sanātana, Atri, Sanandana, Śiva, Sanatkumāra, Viśva, and Sanaka.
      • In the third enclosure, place Indra and others. On the right (South) wall side, place Pitāmaha (Brahmā) and Puṇya. On the left (North) wall side, place Gaṅgādhara (Siva) and Prajāpati.
      • At the doorways, place Dhātā, Vidhātā, Bhujaṅga, Pataṅgapati, and Varuṇa.
      • Install the Nyakṣa and other temple guardians (vimāna-pālas) at the top of the shikhara, below the finial (sthūpy-adhastāt), seated facing outwards in the four cardinal directions.
      • Around the basement moldings (adhiṣṭhāna-vargam) below, place Nyakṣa, Vivasvat, Mitra, and Kṣattā to be worshipped on their respective pedestals in the four directions starting from the East.
      • At the corners of the thresholds (maṇika-sandhyau) of the inner door and front porch door, place the sages Vikhanas and Tapas.
    • The 18 Gate-Guardians (Dvāra-pālas) at Enclosure Gates:
      • Worship Kiṣkindha and Tīrtha, Vighneśa and Nāgeśa, Śaṅkha and Padma, Tuhiṇa and Balinda, Nandaka and Śārṅga, Cakracūlin and Śaṅkhacūlin, and Caṇḍa and Pracaṇḍa.
    • Auxiliary Gateways:
      • Between North and North-East, place Viṣvaksena.
      • In the first enclosure courtyard, in the fourth or second part, align and worship the everlasting forms: Śrībūta, Garuḍa, Cakra, Dhvaja, Śaṅkha, Yūthādhipa, and Akṣahantar on their respective axis.
    • First or Second Enclosure Placements:
      • From East to North-East, place Indra and others on the right side of the gates and in the corners.
      • North of the East gate: Bhāskara (Sun). Between East and South-East: the planets Candra, Aṅgāraka, Budha, Bṛhaspati, Kāvya (Śukra), Manda (Śani), Rāhu, and Ketu.
    • Third Enclosure Placements:
      • The sacred rivers Gaṅgā, Yamunā, Narmadā, and Sindhu on the right side of the gates in the four directions.
      • North of the East gate: Durgā. In the corners: Siddhi, Śrī, Jyēṣṭhā, and Dhātrī.
      • In the intermediate spaces: Sudhāsundarī, Svāhā, Svadha, Māyā, Saṃhlādinī, Rākā, and Sinīvālī.
      • In the kitchen: Havir-rakṣaka; in the flower pavilion: Puṣpa-rakṣaka; beside the water storage: Puruṣa; beside the vestment storage: Tvaṣṭar; in the bedchamber: Kaumodakī (the mace deity); in the ingredient store: Sarveśvara; in the fire temple: Balirakṣaka.
    • Fourth Enclosure Placements:
      • North of the East gate: Gaviṣṭha. South: Pṛthivī. West: Varuṇa. South: Yama. North: Vāyu.
      • In the corners: the personifications of the Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma, and Atharva Vedas.
      • In the intermediate spaces: Śiva, Viṣṇu, Tक्षक (Takṣaka), Śubha, Bhūtanāyaka, Pavitra, Pāvana, and Kṣitija.
    • Fifth Enclosure Placements:
      • North and South of the East gate: Cakra, Hrī, and Anumati.
      • South: the Seven Mothers (Saptamātṛkās) starting with Brāhmaṇī.
      • West: Jaya and other Apsarases.
      • North: Kuṇḍinyā and the Seven Rohiṇīs.
      • Corners: Vatsara and other Prāṇa-maruts.
      • Intermediate spaces: the Śatarudras; between North-East and East: Ajaikapad and the eleven Rudras; between East and South-East: Dhātā and the twelve Ādityas; between South and South-East: Gandharvas; between South and South-West: Sages (Munīs); between South-West and West: Amṛtajas; between West and North-West: the Aśvins; between North and North-West: Vidyādharas.
    • Sixth Enclosure Placements:
      • North of the East gate: Tumburu; South: Nārada. South: the Ṛtus (seasons). West: Prajāpati. North: Mudgala. South-East: Haleśa. South-West: Yakṣa. North-West: Jāmbavat. North-East: Surama.
      • Intermediate spaces: North of the East: Kinnara couple; South of it: Prahlāda. South: Kāma and Rati. West: Vyālinī and Kāminī. North: Prabhā and Sundarī.
    • Seventh Enclosure Placements:
      • North of the East gate: Brahmā; South: Aryaman. South: Daṇḍabhṛt (Yama). North: Dhanada (Kubera).
      • Corners: Savitṛ and Sāvitrī; Indra and Indrānuja; Rudra and Rudrānuja; Apa and Apavatsa.
      • Intermediate spaces: the pad deities of the Vāstu grid (pada-devatās) starting from Īśa in order.
    • For any deities not listed here, establish a great altar (mahat-pīṭha) outside, and worship them with the mental projection “All Deities” (sarve devāḥ). Execute everything else not mentioned according to the rules declared by Sage Bhrigu (Bhrigūkta-vidhinā).

Section 3: Altar Design and Architecture (Pīṭha-lakṣaṇam)

Verse / Paragraph 1

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

यूथेश-पीठं यथोक्ते देशे गर्भागार-विस्ताराद् (विस्तृताद्) द्वार-उत्तुङ्गाद् वा, पञ्चभाग-त्रिभाग-एकभागम् अर्धं वा; अथवा हस्त-मानेन त्रि-हस्तं, द्वि-हस्तं, एक-हस्तं वा पीठस्य आयाम-विस्तारं, तत्-सम-उत्सेधं; तन्मध्ये पूर्ववत् प्रथमेष्टकां शिलां वा विन्यस्य, पीठ-उत्तुङ्गं (पीठोत्सेधं) षोडशांशं कृत्वा - एकांशम् उपानं, चतुरंशा जगती, त्र्यंशं कुमुदम्, अर्धं-अंशं कम्पं, चतुरंशं कण्ठं, अर्धं-अंशं कम्पं, द्व्यर्धांशं महापट्टिका, तदूर्ध्वं कम्पम् अर्धं-अंशं; (चतुर्दिक्षु मध्य-महान्?) शङ्खनाद-सम-तद्-अद्भुताकार-युक्तं वियुक्तं वा कृत्वा, समाम् इष्टकां विन्यस्य, तत्-तुरीयांशम् ऊर्ध्व-पद्मं; तत्-पद्मं पञ्चधा कृत्वा एकांशं पद्म-आधार-वृत्त-कम्पं, तस्य ऊर्ध्वं द्व्यंशम् अधोदलं, शेषांशम् ऊर्ध्वदलम्, ऊर्ध्व-अधः (ऊर्ध्वाधः) षोडश-दलैः युक्तं कुर्यात्। पद्मोत्तुङ्गस्य (पद्यतुङ्गस्य) पादांशेनाऽर्धांशेन वा उपपीठं कारयेत् इति केचित्॥

Transliteration (IAST):

yūtheśa-pīṭhaṃ yathokte deśe garbhāgāra-vistārād dvāra-uttuṅgād vā, pañcabhāga-tribhāga-ekabhāgam ardhaṃ vā; athavā hastamānena tri-hastaṃ, dvi-hastaṃ, eka-hastaṃ vā pīṭhasya āyāma-vistāraṃ, tat-sama-utsedhaṃ; tanmadhye pūrvavat prathameṣṭakāṃ śilāṃ vā vinyasya, pīṭha-utsedhaṃ ṣoḍaśāṃśaṃ kṛtvā - ekāṃśam upānaṃ, caturaṃśā jagatī, tryaṃśaṃ kumudam, ardha-aṃśaṃ kampam, caturaṃśaṃ kaṇṭham, ardha-aṃśaṃ kampam, dvyardhāṃśaṃ mahāpaṭṭikā, tadūrdhvaṃ kampam ardha-aṃśaṃ; śaṅkhanāda-sama-tad-adbhutākāra-yuktaṃ viyuktaṃ vā kṛtvā, samām iṣṭakāṃ vinyasya, tat-turīyāṃśam ūrdhva-padmaṃ; tat-padmaṃ pañcadhā kṛtvā ekāṃśaṃ padma-ādhāra-vṛtta-kampaṃ, tasya ūrdhve dvyaṃśam adhodalaṃ, śeṣāṃśam ūrdhvadalam, ūrdhva-adhaḥ ṣoḍaśa-dalaiḥ yuktaṃ kuryāt. padmottuṅgasya pādāṃśena ardhāṃśena vā upapīṭhaṃ kārayet iti kecit.

Translation (English):

  • Altar Design and Architecture (Pīṭha-lakṣaṇam):
    • Construct the altar for the leader of host deities (yūtheśa-pīṭha) in the designated spot.
    • Its width and length should be 1/5, 1/3, 1/2, or equal to the width of the sanctum or the height of the doorway; or absolutely measured in hands: 3 hands, 2 hands, or 1 hand. Its height should be equal to its width.
    • Perform the foundation ceremony in its center by placing a brick or stone as before.
    • Moldings for the Altar Height (16 parts):
      • Plinth (upāna) = 1 part
      • Plinth base (jagatī) = 4 parts
      • Torus molding (kumuda) = 3 parts
      • Fillet (kampa) = 0.5 part
      • Dado/neck (kaṇṭha) = 4 parts
      • Fillet (kampa) = 0.5 part
      • Large band/entablature (mahāpaṭṭikā) = 2.5 parts
      • Upper fillet (tadūrdhva-kampa) = 0.5 part.
    • Decorate or leave undecorated the sides with carvings representing conch sounds (śaṅkha-nāda) and other wonderful forms.
    • Lay a level brick, and assign the upper 1/4 of the height for the upper lotus molding (ūrdhva-padma).
    • Lotus Molding Divisions (5 parts):
      • Lotus base fillet (padmādhāra-vṛtta-kampa) = 1 part
      • Lower petals (adhodala) = 2 parts
      • Upper petals (ūrdhvadala) = 2 parts.
      • It should be decorated with sixteen petals on the top and bottom.
    • Some say a sub-plinth (upapīṭha) measuring 1/4 or 1/2 of the lotus molding height should be constructed.

Section 1: Consecration Rites and Layouts of the Sacrificial Halls

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

नव-षट्-पञ्च-मूर्ति-प्रतिष्ठा-विधिः - अथ नव-षट्-पञ्च-मूर्तीनां प्रतिष्ठा-विशेषं वक्ष्ये — पूर्वोक्तेषु नक्षत्रेषु एकस्मिन् यजमान-अनुकूले नक्षत्रे प्रतिष्ठां कुर्यात्। तद्-दिनात् पूर्वं यथोक्तम् अङ्कुरान् अर्पयित्वा, नवमूर्तीः समुद्दिश्य प्रत्येकम् आचार्यं स्थापकान् ऋत्विजश्च पूर्वोक्त-लक्षण-सम्पन्नान् उक्त-दोष-विवर्जितान् वरयेत् ॥ nava-ṣaṭ-pañca-mūrti-pratiṣṭhā-vidhiḥ - atha nava-ṣaṭ-pañca-mūrtīnāṃ pratiṣṭhā-viśeṣaṃ vakṣye — pūrvokteṣu nakṣatreṣu ekasmin yajamana-anukūle nakṣatre pratiṣṭhāṃ kuryāt. tad-dināt pūrvaṃ yathoktam aṅkurān arpayitvā, navamūrtīḥ samuddicya pratyekam ācāryaṃ sthāpakān ṛtvijaśca pūrvokta-lakṣaṇa-sampannān ukta-doṣa-vivarjitān varayet.

आदिमूर्तेः यागशाला-लक्षणम् - तथैव सम्भारान् सम्भृत्य, आलय-अभिमुखे विष्णोः आदिमूर्त्तेः यागशालां पञ्च-हस्त-आयत-विस्तारां षट्त्रिंशत्-स्तम्भ-संयुक्तां प्रपां कृत्वा; तन्मध्ये वर्ष-निवारण-कूटं कृत्वा, तस्मिन् पञ्चविंशति-भागं कृत्वा, तन्मध्ये पदे शय्या-वेदिं तद्-भित्ति-सम-आयाम-विस्तारां तद्-तुरीयांश-उत्सेधां दर्पण-उदर-वत् स्थलं सङ्कल्प्य; ādimūrteḥ yāgāśālā-lakṣaṇam - tathaiva sambhārān sambhṛtya, ālaya-abhimukhe viṣṇoḥ ādimūtteḥ yāgāśālāṃ pañca-hast-āyata-vistārāṃ ṣaṭtriṃśat-stambha-saṃyuktāṃ prapāṃ kṛtvā; tanmadhye varṣa-nivāraṇa-kūṭaṃ kṛtvā, tasmin pañcavaiṃśati-bhāgaṃ kṛtvā, tanmadhye pade śayyā-vediṃ tad-bhitti-sama-āyāma-vistārāṃ tad-turīyāṃśa-utsedhāṃ darpaṇa-udara-vat sthalaṃ saṅkalpya;

परितश्च अष्टसु पदेषु उपपदेषु अष्टाङ्गुल-उत्सेधानि स्थलानि कृत्वा; शय्यावेद्याः पूर्वे सभ्यम् आहवनीयं च, याम्ये अन्वाहार्यम्, पश्चिमे गार्हपत्यं, सौम्ये चावसथ्यम्, आग्नेय्यां पौण्डरीकं, नैर्ऋत्यां विष्टरं, वायव्यां सम्भारवेदिम्, ऐशान्यां स्नानश्वभ्रं तद्-बाह्ये षोडशभाग-एकभाग-अङ्गुल-उत्सेधं स्थलं कृत्वा; paritaśca aṣṭasu padeṣu upapadeṣu aṣṭāṅgula-utsedhāni sthalāni kṛtvā; śayyāvedyāḥ pūrve sabhyam āhavanīyaṃ ca, yāmye anvāhāryam, paścime gārhapatyaṃ, saumye cāvasathyama, āgneyyāṃ pouṇḍarīkaṃ, nairṛtyāṃ viṣṭaraṃ, vāyavyāṃ sambhāravedim, aiśānyāṃ snānaśvabhraṃ tad-bāhye ṣoḍaśabhāga-ekabhāga-aṅgula-utsedhaṃ sthalaṃ kṛtvā;

इन्द्रादि-दिग्देवानां तत्-तत्-दिक्षु औपासन-अग्निकुण्डानि कुर्यात्। indrādi-digdevānāṃ tat-tat-dikṣu oupāsana-agnikuṇḍāni kuryāt.

मध्य-अन्तराल-अग्निकुण्डानि - इन्द्र-अग्न्योः मध्ये गरुडस्य; यम-अग्न्योः (यम-पावकयोः) मध्ये ब्रह्मणः; यम-निरृत्योः (यम-नीयोः) मध्ये भृगोः; निरृति-वरुणयोः (नील-वरुणयोः) मध्ये श्रीदेव्याः; वरुण-वाय्वोः (वरुण-उदानयोः) मध्ये महीदेव्याः; वायु-सोमयोः मध्ये मार्कण्डेयस्य; सोम-ईशानयोः मध्ये शङ्करस्य; इन्द्र-ईशानयोः मध्ये विष्वक्सेनस्य च; अन्येषां परिवाराणां तत्-तत्-दिक्षु यागशालां परितश्च औपासन-अग्निकुण्डानि कुर्यात्। madhya-antarāla-agnikuṇḍāni - indra-agnoyoḥ madhye garuḍasya; yama-agnoyoḥ (yama-pāvakayoḥ) madhye brahmaṇaḥ; yama-nirṛtyoḥ (yama-nīyoḥ) madhye bhṛgoḥ; nirṛti-varuṇayoḥ (nīla-varuṇayoḥ) madhye śrīdevyāḥ; varuṇa-vāyvoḥ (varuṇa-udānayoḥ) madhye mahīdevyāḥ; vāyu-somayoḥ madhye mārkaṇḍeyasya; soma-īśānayoḥ madhye śaṅkarasya; indra-īśānayoḥ madhye viṣvaksenasya ca; anyeṣāṃ parivārāṇāṃ tat-tat-dikṣu yāgāśālāṃ paritaśca oupāsana-agnikuṇḍāni kuryāt.

तेषां बेरालयं यदि स्यात्, तत्-तत्-अभिमुखे च औपासन-अग्निकुण्डं कुम्भ-पूजनं च पृथग् एव कुर्यात्। बिम्बाभावेऽपि इन्द्रादि-ईशान-पर्यन्तानां द्वारेषु द्वारपालानं न्यक्षादीनाम् अभिमुखे श्रीभूतस्य अनपायिनं च होमं कुर्याद् इति केचित्। teṣāṃ berālayaṃ yadi syāt, tat-tat-abhimukhe ca oupāsana-agnikuṇḍaṃ kumbha-pūjanaṃ ca pṛthag eva kuryāt. bimbābhāve’pi indrādi-īśāna-paryantānāṃ dvāreṣu dvārapālānaṃ nyakṣādīnām abhimukhe śrībhūtasya anapāyinaṃ ca homaṃ kuryād iti kecit.

पुरुषादीनां यागशाला-लक्षणम् - प्राच्यां पुरुषमूर्त्तेः यागशालां षोडश-स्तम्भ-संयुक्तां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं परितः चतुरग्निकुण्डानि च परिकल्प्य; puruṣādīnāṃ yāgāśālā-lakṣaṇam - prācyāṃ puruṣamūtteḥ yāgāśālāṃ ṣoḍaśa-stambha-saṃyuktāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ paritaḥ caturagnikuṇḍāni ca parikalpya;

आलयाद् दक्षिणे तद्-द्वार-अभिमुखे सत्यमूर्त्तेः यागशालां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं परितः चतुरग्निकुण्डानि परिकल्प्य; ālayād dakṣiṇe tad-dvāra-abhimukhe satyamūtteḥ yāgāśālāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ paritaḥ caturagnikuṇḍāni parikalpya;

तद्-दक्षिणे नरनारायणयोः यागशालां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं तन्मध्ये चतुरस्रं सङ्कल्प्य; tad-dakṣiṇe naranārāyaṇayoḥ yāgāśālāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ tanmadhye caturasraṃ saṅkalpya;

पश्चिमे अच्युतमूर्त्तेः यागशालां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं परितः चतुरस्र-अग्निकुण्डान्; paścime acyutamūtteḥ yāgāśālāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ paritaḥ caturasra-agnikuṇḍān;

तत्-पश्चिमे नृसिंहस्य यागशालां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं परितः चतुरस्र-अग्निकुण्डान् परिकल्प्य; tat-paścime nṛsiṃhasya yāgāśālāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ paritaḥ caturasra-agnikuṇḍān parikalpya;

उत्तरे अनिरुद्धमूर्त्तेः यागशालां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं परितः चतुरस्र-अग्निकुण्डान् परिकल्प्य; uttare aniruddhamūtteḥ yāgāśālāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ paritaḥ caturasra-agnikuṇḍān parikalpya;

तस्य उत्तरे वाराहस्य यागशालां तन्मध्ये शय्या-वेदिं परितः चतुरस्र-अग्निकुण्डान् परिकल्प्य; अन्यत् सर्वं पूर्ववत् कुर्यात्। tasya uttare vārāhasya yāgāśālāṃ tanmadhye śayyā-vediṃ paritaḥ caturasra-agnikuṇḍān parikalpya; anyat sarvaṃ pūrvavat kuryāt.

आलयस्य उत्तरे श्रामणकाग्निकुण्डं कृत्वा, सूत्रोक्त-विधिना वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा, सर्वत्र पर्यग्नि-पञ्चगव्याभ्यां संशोध्य, प्रोक्षणैः प्रोक्ष्य, अधिवासनगतान् देवान् आदाय अभिषिच्य, वस्त्र-उत्तरीयाद्यैः विभूष्य, अष्ट-उपचारैः अभ्यर्च्य, आलयम् आवेश्य, तत्-तत्-यागशालायां पश्चिमे विष्टरे प्राङ्मुखान् देवान् संस्थाप्य, सर्वेषु अग्निकुण्डेषु आघारं हुत्वा, सर्वेषां आचार्याः पूर्ववत् प्रत्येकं कुम्भ-पूजनं कुर्युः। यजमानः आचार्यान् वस्त्राद्यैः अर्चनं कुर्यात् ॥ ālayasya uttare śrāmaṇakagnikuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā, sūtrokta-vidhinā vāstu-homaṃ hutvā, sarvatra paryagni-pañcagavyābhyāṃ saṃśodhya, prokṣaṇaiḥ prokṣya, adhivāsanagatān devān ādāya abhiṣicya, vastra-uttarīyādyaiḥ vibhūṣya, aṣṭa-upacāraiḥ abhyarcya, ālayam āveśya, tat-tat-yāgāśālāyāṃ paścime viṣṭare prāṅmukhān devān saṃsthāpya, sarveṣu agnikuṇḍeṣu āghāraṃ hutvā, sarveṣāṃ ācāryāḥ pūrvavat pratyekaṃ kumbha-pūjanaṃ kuryuḥ. yajamānaḥ ācāryān vastrādyaiḥ arcanaṃ kuryāt.

English Translation

  • Auspicious Timing and Priestly Selection: I shall describe the specific consecration rites for the nine, six, and five forms of Viṣṇu. The priest should perform the consecration under an auspicious asterism (nakṣatra) that is favorable to the patron (yajamāna). Prior to that day, having sown the seeds (aṅkurārpaṇa), he should choose one principal teacher (Ācārya), assistants (Sthāpakas), and priests (Ṛtvijs) for each of the nine deities, ensuring they are endowed with scriptural qualities and free from blemishes.

  • The Main Altar for the Primary Deity (Ādimūrti):

    • Having gathered the materials, facing the temple, the builder sets up a sacrificial hall (yāgāśālā) for the primary Ādimūrti measuring five cubits in length and width, supported by thirty-six pillars.
    • In the center, he constructs a weather-proof canopy (kūṭa), divides it into twenty-five compartments, and sets up a couch-altar (śayyā-vedi) in the middle, sized to match the walls, with its height being one-quarter of the image, level and smooth like a mirror.
    • He raises eight platforms, eight inches high, in the surrounding intermediate compartments.

  • Layout of the Fire-Pits:

    • To the east of the couch-altar, he places the Sabhya and Āhavanīya fires; to the south (Yāmya), the Anvāhārya fire; to the west, the Gārhapatya fire; to the north, the Āvasathya fire. To the southeast, he places the Pauṇḍarīka fire; to the southwest, the Viṣṭara (grass seat); to the northwest, the material altar (sambhāra-vedi); and to the northeast, the bathing pit (snāna-śvabhra).
    • Along the outer perimeter, he raises a platform one-sixteenth of an inch high. He sets up domestic fire-pits (aupāsana-agni) for the directional guardians like Indra in their respective directions.

  • Intermediate Deities Fire-Pits:

    • Between East and Southeast (Indra and Agni): For Garuḍa.
    • Between South and Southeast (Yama and Agni): For Brahmā.
    • Between South and Southwest (Yama and Nirṛti): For Bhṛgu.
    • Between Southwest and West (Nirṛti and Varuṇa): For Goddess Śrī.
    • Between West and Northwest (Varuṇa and Vāyu): For Goddess Bhūmi (Mahī).
    • Between Northwest and North (Vāyu and Soma): For Mārkaṇḍeya.
    • Between North and Northeast (Soma and Īśāna): For Śiva (Śaṅkara).
    • Between Northeast and East (Īśāna and Indra): For Viṣvaksena.
    • If separate shrines exist for these deities, he should set up individual domestic fire-pits and conduct pot-worship directly facing them. Even if images are absent, some say he should offer fire oblations facing the doorways for the door-guardians and other gate deities.

  • Individual Sacrificial Halls for the Other Eight Images:

    • Puruṣa (East): A sacrificial hall with sixteen pillars, a couch-altar in the center, and four surrounding fire-pits.
    • Satya (South): Facing the southern door, a hall with a couch-altar and four fire-pits.
    • Nara-Nārāyaṇa (South-South): A hall with a couch-altar and square compartments.
    • Achyuta (West): A hall with a couch-altar and square fire-pits.
    • Nṛsiṃha (West-West): A hall with a couch-altar and square fire-pits.
    • Aniruddha (North): A hall with a couch-altar and square fire-pits.
    • Varāha (North-North): A hall with a couch-altar and square fire-pits.

  • Purification of the Halls:

    • To the north of the temple, having prepared the Śrāmaṇaka fire-pit and offered the site-homa (vāstu-homa), he purifies all areas with fire and the five products of the cow (pañca-gavya), and sprinkles them with water.
    • He brings the deities from their temporary placement (adhivāsana), bathes them, adorns them with garments, upper cloths, and ornaments, and worships them with eight services (aṣṭopacāra).
    • Entering the temple, he establishes the deities on seats facing east in the west of their respective sacrificial halls. Offering ghee in all the fire-pits, the teachers perform the pot-worship (kumbha-pūjā) for each. The patron honors the teachers with garments and gems.

Section 2: Preparations, couch Placement, and Chief Fires

Verse / Paragraph 1

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

कुम्भ-संन्यासादि-शयन-अधिवासन-विधिः - कुम्भान् आदाय तन्तुना परिवेष्ट्य, प्रक्षाल्य, नादेयैः वारिभिः पूरयित्वा, पृथक् पृथक् वस्त्र-युग्मेन आवेष्ट्य, प्रत्येकं नवरत्नानि सौवर्णानि अष्टमङ्गल-पञ्चायुध-वर्णचिह्नानि च विन्यस्य; तत्-तत्-अगारे, बालागारे वा देवाभिमुखे धान्यराश्युपरि कुम्भान् संन्यस्य, पूर्ववद् ध्यात्वा आवाह्य; पश्चात् स्थापकान् आहूय, आदिमूर्त्यादीन् यथाक्रमेण आचार्याः तत्-तत्-समादाय पुरतो गच्छेयुः।

Transliteration (IAST):

kumbha-saṃnyāsādi-śayana-adhivāsana-vidhiḥ - kumbhān ādāya tantunā pariveṣṭya, prakṣālya, nādeyaiḥ vāribhiḥ pūrayitvā, pṛthak pṛthak vastra-yugmena āveṣṭya, pratyekaṃ navaratnāni souvarṇāni aṣṭamaṅgala-pañcāyudha-varṇacihnāni ca vinyasya; tat-tat-agāre, bālāgāre vā devābhimukhe dhānyarāśyupari kumbhān saṃnyasya, pūrvavad dhyātvā āvāhya; paścāt sthāpakān āhūya, ādimūrtyādīn yathākrameṇa ācāryāḥ tat-tat-samādāya purato gaccheyuḥ.

Translation (English):

  • Preparation of the Ritual Pots: Taking the pots, the priests wrap them with thread, wash them, and fill them with river water. They wrap each pot in a pair of cloths and place inside them the nine gems, golden replicas of the eight auspicious signs, the five weapons, and color signs.

Verse / Paragraph 2

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

तत्-तत्-यागशालाम् आसाद्य, स्नानश्वभ्रे प्रतिष्ठाप्य, सप्तभिः कलशैः संस्नाप्य, वस्त्र-उत्तरीय-आभरणैः अलङ्कृत्य, अभ्यर्च्य; पुण्याहान्ते प्रतिसरं बद्ध्वा, तत्-तत्-वेद्यां धान्योपरि पञ्च-शयनानि वासांसि वा आस्तीर्य, तथैव शाययित्वा, उत्तराच्छादनं कृत्वा; होतृन् सर्वान् वस्त्र-उत्तरीयाद्यैः अलङ्कृत्य, तत्-तत्-प्रधानहोमे हौत्रं प्रशंस्य, तथैव आवाहनादीनि कुर्यात्।

Transliteration (IAST):

tat-tat-yāgāśālām āsādya, snānaśvabhre pratiṣṭhāpya, saptabhiḥ kalaśaiḥ saṃsnāpya, vastra-uttarīya-ābharaṇaiḥ alaṅkṛtya, abhyarcya; puṇyāhānte pratisaraṃ baddhvā, tat-tat-vedyāṃ dhānyopari pañca-śayanāni vāsāṃsi vā āstīrya, tathaiva śāyayitvā, uttarācchādanaṃ kṛtvā; hotṝn sarvān vastra-uttarīyādyaiḥ alaṅkṛtya, tat-tat-pradhānahome houtraṃ praśaṃsya, tathaiva āvāhanādīni kuryāt.

Translation (English):

  • Invoking and Moving the Images:
    • They place the pots on heaps of grain directly facing the deities in their respective shrines or in the temporary temple (bālālaya), meditate on them, and invoke the deities as before.

  • Secondary Homa Rites: Having performed the thousand-fold oblation (sahasrāhuti), he offers the five Vāruṇa offerings, the Kūṣmāṇḍa homa (using “yad devāḥ”), the सर्वदेवत्य mantras starting with “brahma jajñānaṃ”, the Vaiṣṇava offerings, the vinda-homa, and the vicchinna-homa once.

  • The Main Altar Oblations: In the Āhavanīya, he offers the Puruṣa-sūkta; in the Anvāhārya, the Viṣṇu-sūkta; in the Gārhapatya, the Vaiṣṇava homa; and in the Āvasathya, the Ekākṣara sūkta three times.

  • Special Offerings:

    • With the Viṣṇu-sūkta, he casts offerings mixed with milk, curd, ghee, parched grain, and raw rice in the five fires using the upajuhū ladle.
    • Taking ghee in the sruk, he offers the Vaiṣṇava homa once and then offers it in the Pauṇḍarīka fire.
    • He offers a hundred lotuses or Bilva leaves smeared with ghee while reciting the Viṣṇu-Gāyatrī.
    • Meditating on the Lord, he offers oblations with the Puruṣa-sūkta and the Pāramātmika hymns starting with “viṣṇuḥ sarveṣām”.

    • Puruṣa (East): In his sacrificial hall: Puruṣa-sūkta in the Āhavanīya, Viṣṇu-sūkta in the Anvāhārya, Vaiṣṇava homa (Śrī deity) in the Gārhapatya, the worshippers’ mantra, and Ekākṣara sūkta (Bhūmi deity) and the Raudra homa in the Āvasathya.

    • Achyuta (West): In his hall: Vaiṣṇava homa in the Gārhapatya, Ekākṣara sūkta (Śrī deity) and Brāhma homa in the Āvasathya, Puruṣa-sūkta (sages’ mantra) in the Āhavanīya, and Viṣṇu-sūkta (Bhūmi deity) and Raudra homa in the Anvāhārya.
    • Aniruddha (North): In his hall: Ekākṣara sūkta in the Āvasathya, Puruṣa-sūkta (Śrī deity) and Brāhma homa in the Āhavanīya, Viṣṇu-sūkta (sages’ mantra) in the Anvāhārya, and Vaiṣṇava homa (Bhūmi deity) and Vaiṣṇava homa in the Gārhapatya.
    • Nara-Nārāyaṇa (2nd Storey South): In their hall: Viṣṇu-sūkta and Viṣṇu-Gāyatrī in the Anvāhārya, Vaiṣṇava homa (Āditya deity) in the Gārhapatya, Ekākṣara sūkta in the Āvasathya, and Puruṣa-sūkta and Prājāpatya homa in the Āhavanīya.

    • Varāha (2nd Storey North): In his hall: Ekākṣara sūkta and “yat svayaṃ sṛṣṭam” once (Bhūmi deity) in the Āvasathya, Puruṣa-sūkta and Aindra homa in the Āhavanīya, Viṣṇu-sūkta in the Anvāhārya, and Vaiṣṇava homa and Vāruṇa homa in the Gārhapatya.

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अन्त-होम-आवाहन-प्रतिष्ठा-विधिः - विष्णुमूर्तेः सभ्यं पौण्डरीकं च, अन्येषां तत्-तत्-प्रधानाग्निं च विना, सर्व-होमे अन्त-होमं हुत्वा, रात्रि-शेषं व्यपोह्य; प्रभाते तु आचार्यः स्थापकाद्याश्च स्नात्वा, आलयम् आविश्य प्रदक्षिणं कृत्वा, तत्-तत्-यागशालाम् आगत्य, पूर्ववत् सर्वान् देवान् उत्थाप्य अभ्यर्च्य; anta-homa-āvāhana-pratiṣṭhā-vidhiḥ - viṣṇumūrteḥ sabhyaṃ pouṇḍarīkaṃ ca, anyeṣāṃ tat-tat-pradhānāgniṃ ca vinā, sarva-home anta-homaṃ hutvā, rātri-śeṣaṃ vyapohya; prabhāte tu ācāryaḥ sthāpakādyāśca snātvā, ālayam āviśya pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā, tat-tat-yāgāśālām āgatya, pūrvavat sarvān devān utthāpya abhyarcya;

गर्भालयेषु सर्वेषु कौतुक-पीठे रत्न-न्यासं कृत्वा, सभ्याग्निं विना सर्व-होमेषु अन्त-होमं जुहुयुः। यजमानः आचार्यादिभ्यो यथोक्त-दक्षिणां देव-सन्निधौ सोदकं दद्यात्। सभ्याग्निं परिस्तीर्य अन्त-होमं हुत्वा, तम् अग्निं नित्य-होमाय विन्यसेत्। garbhālayeṣu sarveṣu kautuka-pīṭhe ratna-nyāsaṃ kṛtvā, sabhyāgniṃ vinā sarva-homeṣu anta-homaṃ juhuyuḥ. yajamānaḥ ācāryādibhyo yathokta-dakṣiṇāṃ deva-sannidhou sodakaṃ dadyāt. sabhyāgniṃ paristīrya anta-homaṃ hutvā, tam agniṃ nitya-homāya vinyaset.

महा-यात्रा-प्रवेशः (Grand Procession) - पश्चात् सुमुहूर्ते प्राप्ते, तत्-तत्-यागशालाम् आगत्य, तत्-तत्-आचार्यादयः ‘भूः प्रपद्ये’ इति क्रमेण कुसुमाञ्जलिं दत्त्वा, तं तं देवं प्रणम्य, सर्व-अलङ्कार-संयुक्तं, सर्व-वाद्य-घोष-समायुक्तं, तोयधारा-पुरस्सरं शकुन-सूक्तं समुच्चार्य, आचार्याः कुम्भान् आहृत्य शिरसा धारयन्तः अग्रतो गच्छेयुः। स्थापकाः कौतुकादीन् आदाय अनुगच्छेयुः। mahā-yātrā-praveśaḥ (Grand Procession) - paścāt sumuhūrte prāpte, tat-tat-yāgāśālām āgatya, tat-tat-ācāryādayaḥ ‘bhūḥ prapadye’ iti krameṇa kusumāñjaliṃ dattva, taṃ taṃ devaṃ praṇamya, sarva-alaṅkāra-saṃyuktaṃ, sarva-vādya-ghoṣa-samāyuktaṃ, toyadhāra-purassaraṃ śakuna-sūktaṃ samuccārya, ācāryāḥ kumbhān āhṛtya śirasā dhārayantaḥ agrato gaccheyuḥ. sthāpakāḥ kautukādīn ādāya anugaccheyuḥ.

आलयं प्रदक्षिणं कृत्वा — पूर्वं विष्णुम् आदिमूर्तिं, पश्चात् पुरुष-सत्य-अच्युत-अनिरुद्धांश्च, शयानं देवं, नरनारायण-नारसिंह-वाराहांश्च क्रमेण आनीय, तत्-तत्-गर्भगारं प्रविश्य, तत्-तत्-ध्रुवबेरस्य दक्षिण-पार्श्वे कुम्भान् संन्यसेयुः। ‘प्रतद्विष्णुः’ इति कौतुकबेराणि संस्थाप्य, पार्श्वयोः देव्यौ च पूर्ववत् स्थापयेयुः। ālayaṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā — pūrvaṃ viṣṇum ādimūrtiṃ, paścāt puruṣa-satya-acyuta-aniruddhāṃśca, śayānaṃ devaṃ, naranārāyaṇ-nārasiṃha-vārāhāṃśca krameṇa ānīya, tat-tat-garbhagāraṃ praviśya, tat-tat-dhruvaberasya dakṣiṇa-pārśve kumbhān saṃnyaseyuḥ. ‘pratadviṣṇuḥ’ iti kautukaberāṇi saṃsthāpya, pārśvayoḥ devyou ca pūrvavat sthāpayeyuḥ.

आवाहनं पूजा च - आत्म-सूक्तं जप्त्वा, ध्रुवबेरस्य पादौ स्पृष्ट्वा सूक्तानि जप्त्वा, तथैव मन्त्र-न्यासं (अक्षर-न्यासं) च कृत्वा; समाहितो देवं ध्यायन् पूर्ववत् समावाह्य, पुण्याहं कृत्वा, नित्य-अर्चना-विधानेन अभ्यर्च्य, पायसादीनि हवींषि निवेद्य, होमं हुत्वा, बलिं निर्वाप्य, बल्युद्धरणं च कारयेत्। एष विशेषः, अन्यत् सर्वं समानम्। āvāhanaṃ pūjā ca - ātma-sūktaṃ japtvā, dhruvaberasya pādou spṛṣṭvā sūktāni japtvā, tathaiva mantra-nyāsaṃ (akṣara-nyāsaṃ) ca kṛtvā; samāhito devaṃ dhyāyan pūrvavat samāvāhya, puṇyāhaṃ kṛtvā, nitya-arcanā-vidhānena abhyarcya, pāyasādīni havīṃṣi nivedya, homaṃ hutvā, baliṃ nirvāpya, balyuddharaṇaṃ ca kārayet. eṣa viśeṣaḥ, anyat sarvaṃ samānam.

षण्मूर्ति-प्रतिष्ठा चेत्, विष्णु-आदि-पञ्चमूर्त्तीनां शयनं च यथोक्त-विधिना प्रतिष्ठापयेत् ॥ ṣaṇmūrti-pratiṣṭhā cet, viṣṇu-adi-pañcamūrtīnāṃ śayanaṃ ca yathokta-vidhinā pratiṣṭhāpayet.

English Translation

  • The Final Offerings (Antahoma): Excluding the Sabhya and Pauṇḍarīka fires of the primary image and the designated chief fires of the other images, the priest offers the final oblation (antahoma) in all the other fires. They keep vigil for the remainder of the night.

  • Morning Awakening:

    • In the morning, the Ācārya, the Sthāpakas, and others bathe, enter the temple, and circumambulate it.
    • They return to the sacrificial halls, awaken all the deities as before, and worship them.
    • They perform the gem-placement (ratna-nyāsa) on the pedestals of the movable images (kautuka-pīṭha) in all the inner sanctums.
    • Excluding the Sabhya fire, they offer the final oblation (antahoma) in all other fires.
    • The patron offers the prescribed fees (dakṣiṇā) along with water in front of the deity to the Ācāryas and others.
    • Strewing grass around the Sabhya fire, he offers the final oblation and establishes that fire for daily temple worship (nitya-homa).

  • The Grand Procession (Pratiṣṭhā-praveśaḥ):

    • When the auspicious moment arrives, the Ācāryas enter the sacrificial halls, offer a handful of flowers saying “bhūḥ prapadye”, bow to the respective deities, and recite the Śakuna-sūkta.
    • Accompanied by all decorations, the sounding of all musical instruments, and preceded by a stream of water, the Ācāryas, carrying the pots on their heads, walk in front. The Sthāpakas, carrying the movable images, follow.
    • They circumambulate the temple clockwise, carrying first the primary Ādimūrti, and then Puruṣa, Satya, Achyuta, Aniruddha, the reclining deity, Nara-Nārāyaṇa, Nṛsiṃha, and Varāha in order.
    • Entering their respective sanctums, they place the pots to the right side of the fixed images (dhruva-bera). They establish the movable images (kautuka-bera) while chanting “pra tad viṣṇuḥ”, and place the two Goddesses on both sides as before.

  • Worship and Invocation: Reciting the Ātma-sūkta, touching the feet of the fixed image, and chanting the sūktas, they perform the placement of syllables (akṣara-nyāsa). Concentrating their minds and meditating on the Lord, they invoke Him as before. Having performed the puṇyāha-vācana, they worship Him according to the rules of daily worship, offer sweet rice (pāyasa) and other food oblations, offer the fire oblation, distribute the offerings (bali), and perform the lifting of the bali. This constitutes the special process; all else is identical to the standard rules. If establishing six images, he should consecrate the reclining posture of the five images starting with Viṣṇu according to the rules.

Section 5: Simplified Layouts for Unable Patrons (Aśakta-viṣaya-kalpaḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अशक्त-विषयक-कल्पः (Simplified layouts) - एवं सर्वासां मूर्तीनां पृथक् कर्तुम् अशक्तश्चेत्, प्राच्याम् एकां यागशालां पूर्ववत् कृत्वा; मध्ये पदे विष्णोः प्रागादि-ईशानान्तम् अष्टसु पदेषु यथाक्रमेण संवेशनं (शयन-वेदि-स्थापनं) — यथाक्रमेण अनन्तशयन-पुरुष-सत्य-नरनारायण-अच्युत-नृसिंह-अनिरुद्ध-वाराहाणाम् शयनवेदिं, तत्-तत्-प्राच्यां तत्-तत्-प्रधानाग्निं च कृत्वा एकाग्निविधानेन प्रतिष्ठापयेद् इति केचित् ॥ aśakta-viṣayaka-kalpaḥ - evaṃ sarvāsāṃ mūrtīnāṃ pṛthak kartum aśaktaścet, prācyām ekāṃ yāgāśālāṃ pūrvavat kṛtvā; madhye pade viṣṇoḥ prāgādi-īśānāntam aṣṭasu padeṣu yathākrameṇa saṃveśanaṃ — yathākrameṇa anantaśayana-puruṣa-satya-naranārāyaṇa-acyuta-nṛsiṃha-aniruddha-vārāhāṇāṃ śayanavediṃ, tat-tat-prācyāṃ tat-tat-pradhānāgniṃ ca kṛtvā ekāgnividhānena pratiṣṭhāpayed iti kecit.

एकतले नवमूर्तीनां प्रतिष्ठा-विशेषः - अशक्तानाम् एकतल-प्रासादे नव-षट्-पञ्च-मूर्तीनां प्रतिष्ठा-विशेषं वक्ष्ये — पूर्ववद् आचार्यम् एकं, स्थापकादींश्च वरयित्वा, यथोक्तम् अङ्कुरान् अर्पयित्वा, सम्भारान् सम्भृत्य, तथैव अक्षिमोचन-अधिवासौ च कृत्वा; ekatale navamūrtīnāṃ pratiṣṭhā-viśeṣaḥ - aśaktānām ekatala-prāsāde nava-ṣaṭ-pañca-mūrtīnāṃ pratiṣṭhā-viśeṣaṃ vakṣye — pūrvavad ācāryam ekaṃ, sthāpakādīṃśca varayitvā, yathoktam aṅkurān arpayitvā, sambhārān sambhṛtya, tathaiva akṣimocana-adhivāsou ca kṛtvā;

आलय-अभिमुखे, दक्षिणे वा पूर्ववद् यागशालां महावेदिं च कृत्वा, परितः अग्निकुण्डानि पौण्डरीकाग्निकुण्डं च कृत्वा, वास्तु-शुद्धिं च कृत्वा, सर्वेषु अग्निषु आघारं हुत्वा, पूर्ववत् कुम्भं संस्थाप्य; आलये बालागारे वा देवाभिमुखे धान्य-उपरि कुम्भं संन्यस्य; ālaya-abhimukhe, dakṣiṇe vā pūrvavad yāgāśālāṃ mahāvediṃ ca kṛtvā, paritaḥ agnikuṇḍāni pouṇḍarīkāgnikuṇḍaṃ ca kṛtvā, vāstu-śuddhiṃ ca kṛtvā, sarveṣu agniṣu āghāraṃ hutvā, pūrvavat kumbhaṃ saṃsthāpya; ālaye bālāgāre vā devābhimukhe dhānya-upari kumbhaṃ saṃnyasya;

आचार्यः उत्तराभिमुखः आसीत्वा, पूर्ववद् ध्यायन् कुम्भे सर्वान् देवान् समावाह्य, कुम्भं सङ्गृह्य अग्रतो गच्छेत्। स्थापकाः शकुन-सूक्तं समुच्चार्य, पूर्वं विष्णुम् आदिमूर्तिं, पश्चात् पुरुषादीन्, स्नपन-उत्सव-बलि-बेराणि च आदाय अनुगच्छेयुः। ācāryaḥ uttarābhimukhaḥ āsītvā, pūrvavad dhyāyan kumbhe sarvān devān samāvāhya, kumbhaṃ saṅgṛhya agrato gacchet. sthāpakāḥ śakuna-sūktaṃ samuccārya, pūrvaṃ viṣṇum ādimūrtiṃ, paścāt puruṣādīn, snapana-utsava-bali-berāṇi ca ādāya anugaccheyuḥ.

स्नानवेद्यां मध्ये विष्णुं, दक्षिणे देवीं श्रियं, पुरुष-सत्य-नरनारायण-नारसिंहांश्च; उत्तरे महीं, अच्युत-अनिरुद्ध-वाराहांश्च शयानं च संस्थाप्य, एषां दक्षिण-पार्श्वे कुम्भं संन्यस्य, स्नपन-उत्सव-बलि-बेराणि च तत्-क्रमेण संस्थाप्य, पूर्ववत् प्रत्येकं कलशैः संस्नाप्य, वस्त्र-उत्तरीयाद्यैः विभूष्य अभ्यर्च्य; snānavedyāṃ madhye viṣṇuṃ, dakṣiṇe devīṃ śriyaṃ, puruṣa-satya-naranārāyaṇa-nārasiṃhāṃśca; uttare mahīṃ, acyuta-aniruddha-vārāhāṃśca śayānaṃ ca saṃsthāpya, eṣāṃ dakṣiṇa-pārśve kumbhaṃ saṃnyasya, snapana-utsava-bali-berāṇi ca tat-krameṇa saṃsthāpya, pūrvavat pratyekaṃ kalaśaiḥ saṃsnāpya, vastra-uttarīyādyaiḥ vibhūṣya abhyarcya;

यज्ञालये नैर्ऋते देवान् संस्थाप्य, पूर्ववत् प्रतिसरं बद्ध्वा, ध्रुवबेरेऽपि प्रतिसरम् आबद्ध्य; पुरुषादीनां ध्रुवबेरं यदि स्यात्, आभासं विना प्रतिसरं बन्धयेत्। yajñāśālaye nairṛte devān saṃsthāpya, pūrvavat pratisaraṃ baddhvā, dhruvabere’pi pratisaram ābaddhya; puruṣādīnāṃ dhruvaberaṃ yadi syāt, ābhāsaṃ vinā pratisaraṃ bandhayet.

पूर्ववत् पृथक् पृथक् शयनानि आस्तीर्य, मध्ये विष्णुम् आदिमूर्तिं देवीभ्यां सह; दक्षिणे पुरुष-सत्य-नरनारायण-नृसिंहांश्च शयानं च तत्-तत्-देवीभ्यां सह समारोप्य; तेषां दक्षिणे स्नपनं, उत्तरे च उत्सव-बलि-बेरं च समारोप्य, पूर्ववद् आच्छादयेत्। pūrvavat pṛthak pṛthak śayanāni āstīrya, madhye viṣṇum ādimūrtiṃ devībhyāṃ saha; dakṣiṇe puruṣ-satya-naranārāyaṇa-nṛsiṃhāṃśca śayānaṃ ca tat-tat-devībhyāṃ saha samāropya; teṣāṃ dakṣiṇe snapanaṃ, uttare ca utsava-bali-beraṃ ca samāropya, pūrvavad ācchādayet.

यतो द्वारं ततस्तेषां मौलिं कृत्वा, उत्तराच्छादनं कृत्वा, सभ्याग्नौ पूर्ववद् धौत्रं प्रशंस्य, आवाहनादीनि कृत्वा, सर्वान् अभ्यर्च्य, चतुर्दिक्षु वेदान् अध्यापयेत्। yato dvāraṃ tatesteṣāṃ mouliṃ kṛtvā, uttarācchādanaṃ kṛtvā, sabhyāgnou pūrvavad dhoutraṃ praśaṃsya, āvāhanādīni kṛtvā, sarvān abhyarcya, caturdikṣu vedān adhyāpayet.

सभ्याग्नौ पूर्वोक्त-होमं हुत्वा, आहवनीयादिषु यथोक्तं होमं जुहुयुः; तथैव प्रायश्चित्त-होमं हुत्वा, सर्व-परिवाराणां तत्-तत्-देवत्यं तत्-तत्-होमे सभ्ये वा तथैव हुत्वा, नृत्त-गेय-वाद्यैः रात्रि-शेषं नयेत्। sabhyāgnou pūrvokta-homaṃ hutvā, āhavanīyādiṣu yathoktaṃ homaṃ juhuyuḥ; tathaiva prāyaścitta-homaṃ hutvā, sarva-parivārāṇāṃ tat-tat-devatyaṃ tat-tat-home sabhye vā tathaiva hutvā, nṛtta-geya-vādyaiky rātri-śeṣaṃ nayet.

प्रातः-काल-कृत्यम् - अथाचार्यः प्रभाते स्नात्वा, आलयम् आविश्य, देवान् उत्थाप्य, पूर्ववद् विष्णु-आदीनां सर्वेषां कौतुकादीनां पीठेषु रत्नादीनि संन्यसेयुः; यजमानः आचार्यादिभ्यो दक्षिणां दद्यात्। सभ्याग्निं परिस्तीर्य अन्तहोमं हुत्वा, तद्-अग्निं नित्य-होमाय विन्यसेत्। prātaḥ-kāla-kṛtyam - athācāryaḥ prabhāte snātvā, ālayam āviśya, devān utthāpya, pūrvavad viṣṇu-ādīnāṃ sarveṣāṃ kautukādīnāṃ pīṭheṣu ratnādīni saṃnyaseyuḥ; yajamānaḥ ācāryādibhyo dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāt. sabhyāgniṃ paristīrya antahomaṃ hutvā, tad-agniṃ nitya-homāya vinyaset.

मुहूर्ते प्राप्ते सर्व-अलङ्कार-संयुक्तं, तोयधारा-पुरस्सरं शकुन-सूक्तं जप्त्वा, देवं ध्यायन् आचार्यः शिरसा कुम्भं धारयन् अग्रतो गच्छेत्। स्थापकाः विष्णु-आदि-मूर्तीः पूर्ववद् आदाय अनुगच्छेयुः। muhūrte prāpte sarva-alaṅkāra-saṃyuktaṃ, toyadhāra-purassaraṃ śakuna-sūktaṃ japtvā, devaṃ dhyāyan ācāryaḥ śirasā kumbhaṃ dhārayan agrato gacchet. sthāpakāḥ viṣṇu-ādi-mūrtīḥ pūrvavad ādāya anugaccheyuḥ.

आलय-प्रदक्षिणं कृत्वा, अभ्यन्तरं प्रविश्य, ध्रुवबेरस्य दक्षिणे कुम्भं संन्यस्य;

  • ब्रह्मस्थाने: विष्णु-आदि-मूर्तिं (Ādimūrti)।
  • तद्-द्वार-दक्षिणे अलिन्द्रे: प्राङ्मुखं पुरुषम्।
  • दक्षिणे: दक्षिणामुखं सत्यम्।
  • तत्-पश्चिमे: नरनारायणौ दक्षिणाभिमुखौ।
  • पश्चिमे: पश्चिमाभिमुखम् अच्युतम्।
  • तद्-उत्तरे: तथैव नृसिंहम्।
  • उत्तरे: उत्तराभिमुखम् अनिरुद्धम्।
  • तत्-पश्चिमे: तथैव वाराहम्।
  • पूर्वद्वार-उत्तरे अलिन्द्रे: शयानं प्राङ्मुखम् — पूर्ववत् स्थापयेयुः। तस्मिन् मुखमण्टपे अभ्यन्तरे वा दक्षिणे स्नपनं, उत्तरे च उत्सव-बलि-बेरं च स्थापयेत्। ālaya-pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā, abhyantaraṃ praviśya, dhruvaberasya dakṣiṇe kumbhaṃ saṃnyasya; ** brahmasthāne: viṣṇu-adi-mūrtiṃ.* ** tad-dvāra-dakṣiṇe alindre: prāṅmukhaṃ puruṣam.* ** dakṣiṇe: dakṣiṇāmukhaṃ satyam.* ** tat-paścime: naranārāyaṇou dakṣiṇābhimukhou.* ** paścime: paścimābhimukham acyutam.* ** tad-uttare: tathaiva nṛsiṃham.* ** uttare: uttarābhimukham aniruddham.* ** tat-paścime: tathaiva vārāham.* ** pūrvadvāra-uttare alindre: śayānaṃ prāṅmukham —* pūrvavat sthāpayeyuḥ. tasmin mukhamanṭape abhyantare vā dakṣiṇe snapanaṃ, uttare ca utsava-bali-beraṃ ca sthāpayet.

अन्तिम-आवाहनम् - आचार्यश्च आत्म-सूक्तं जप्त्वा, ध्रुवस्य पादं स्पृशन् सूक्तानि जप्त्वा, दक्षिणे च उत्तराभिमुखः तिष्ठन्, ध्रुवबेरे अक्षराणि विन्यस्य; समाहितः कुम्भस्थां शक्तिम् आदाय, ‘आयातु भगवान्’ इति ध्रुवबेरस्य मूर्ध्नि ‘विष्णुम् आवाहयामि’ इति समावाहयेत्। तथैव पुरुषादीनां ध्रुवबेरेषु तत्-तत्-मूर्त्तिभिः समावाहयेत्। तथैव पुरुषादीनां ध्रुवबेरं हीनं चेत्, आदिमूर्तेः ध्रुवबेरस्य मूर्ध्नि सर्वां मूर्तिम् आवाहयेत्। antima-āvāhanam - ācāryaśca ātma-sūktaṃ japtvā, dhruvasya pādaṃ spṛśan sūktāni japtvā, dakṣiṇe ca uttarābhimukhaḥ tiṣṭhan, dhruvabere akṣarāṇi vinyasya; samāhitaḥ kumbhasthāṃ śaktim ādāya, ‘āyātu bhagavān’ iti dhruvaberasya mūrdhni ‘viṣṇum āvāhayāmi’ iti samāvāhayet. tathaiva puruṣādīnāṃ dhruvabereṣu tat-tat-mūrttibhiḥ samāvāhayet. tathaiva puruṣādīnāṃ dhruvaberaṃ hīnaṃ cet, ādimūrteḥ dhruvaberasya mūrdhni sarvāṃ mūrtim āvāhayet.

ध्रुवस्य पादं परितः पुरुषादि-आवरणत्रय-देवान् समावाह्य, दक्षिणे श्रियं, वामे हरिणीं (भूमिं) समावाहयेत्। dhruvasya pādaṃ paritaḥ puruṣādi-āvaraṇatraya-devān samāvāhya, dakṣiṇe śriyaṃ, vāme hariṇīṃ (bhūmiṃ) samāvāhayet.

विष्णु-आदीनां कौतुकानां, स्नपन-उत्सव-बलि-बेराणां बीजमन्त्रन्यासम् अक्षरन्यासं च कृत्वा; पूर्ववत् सौवर्णादि-पात्रं शङ्खं वा पूतवारिणा आपूर्य, देवं ध्यायन्, ध्रुवबेरात् प्रणिधि-जले दीपात् दीपम् इव आवाह्य; विष्णु-आदीनां कौतुकेषु यथाक्रमेण आवाहयेत्। तदैव स्नपन-उत्सव-बलि-बेरेषु च आवाहयेत्। एष विशेषः, अन्यत् सर्वं समानम् ॥ viṣṇu-ādīnāṃ kautukānāṃ, snapana-utsava-bali-berāṇāṃ bījamantranyāsam akṣaranyāsaṃ ca kṛtvā; pūrvavat souvarṇādi-pātraṃ śaṅkhaṃ vā pūtavāriṇā āpūrya, devaṃ dhyāyan, dhruvaberaāt praṇidhi-jale dīpāt dīpam iv āvāhya; viṣṇu-yādīnāṃ kautukeṣu yathākrameṇa āvāhayet. tadāiva snapana-utsava-bali-bereṣu ca āvāhayet. eṣa viśeṣaḥ, anyat sarvaṃ samānam.

English Translation

  • The Single Sacrificial Hall Alternative: If unable to set up separate sacrificial halls for all the images, some say that the priest should construct a single sacrificial hall on the east. In the center, in the eight directions surrounding the central grid square of Viṣṇu, he sets up eight couch-altars in order:

    1. Reclining form on Ananta (Ananta-śayana)
    2. Puruṣa
    3. Satya
    4. Nara-Nārāyaṇa
    5. Achyuta
    6. Nṛsiṃha
    7. Aniruddha
    8. Varāha
    • He establishes their respective chief fires in their east, and performs the consecration using the single-fire method (ekāgni-vidhāna).

  • Alternative Consecration in a Single-Storeyed Temple:

    • For those unable to build multi-storeyed temples, I shall describe the simplified consecration in a single-storeyed temple. Having chosen one Ācārya and Sthāpakas, sown seeds, gathered the materials, and performed eye-opening and couch-placement:
    • They set up a single sacrificial hall facing the temple or on the south, construct the main altar (mahā-vedi), the surrounding fire-pits, and the Pauṇḍarīka fire-pit.
    • Having purified the site, they offer the initial ghee oblation in all the fires and establish the pot.
    • In the temple or the temporary shrine (bālālaya), they place the pot on a heap of grain facing the deity. Seated facing north and meditating, the Ācārya invokes all the deities into the pot, lifts it, and walks in front.

  • Bathing in the Assembly Hall:

    • Chanting the Śakuna-sūkta, the Sthāpakas carry the primary Ādimūrti, followed by Puruṣa and others, and the bathing, festival, and offering images.
    • On the bathing altar (snāna-vedi), they place Viṣṇu in the center, flanked by Goddess Śrī and the four forms (Puruṣa, Satya, Nara-Nārāyaṇa, Nṛsiṃha) on the right; and Goddess Bhūmi (Mahī), the three forms (Achyuta, Aniruddha, Varāha), and the reclining deity on the left.
    • They place the pot to their right, place the bathing, festival, and offering images in order, bathe them with individual pots, adorn them with garments and upper cloths, and worship them.

  • Simplified Couch Placement:

    • They place the deities in the southwest of the sacrificial hall and tie the protective thread (pratisara) on the images, including the fixed images.
    • They prepare individual couches. On the central couch, they place Ādimūrti along with the two Goddesses. On the right, they place Puruṣa, Satya, Nara-Nārāyaṇa, and Nṛsiṃha along with the reclining deity and their respective Goddesses. They place the bathing and festival/offering images to their right and cover them.
    • Arranging the images with their heads facing the doorway, they cover them, praise the hotṛ hymns in the Sabhya fire, perform the invocation, and cause the chanting of the Vedas in the four directions.

  • Oblations and Vigil: They offer the main oblations in the Sabhya fire, the prescribed oblations in the Āhavanīya and other fires, perform the penance homas, and offer the specific deity-mantras in their respective fires or in the Sabhya fire, keeping vigil through the night with dance, song, and music.

  • Morning Rituals: In the morning, having bathed, they enter the temple, awaken the deities, and place the gems on the pedestals. The patron offers fees to the priests. They strew grass around the Sabhya fire, offer the final oblation, and establish it for daily offerings.

  • Final Installation and Positioning:

    • When the auspicious moment arrives, carrying the pot on his head, the Ācārya walks in front, followed by the Sthāpakas carrying the images.
    • Circumambulating the temple and entering the inner sanctum, they place the pot to the right of the fixed image.
    • They position the deities as follows:
      • Center (Brahma-sthāna): The seated Ādimūrti (Viṣṇu).
      • Eastern gallery (Alistala/Alindra) to the right of the door: Puruṣa, facing east.
      • South: Satya, facing south.
      • South-South: Nara-Nārāyaṇa, facing south.
      • West: Achyuta, facing west.
      • West-West: Nṛsiṃha, facing west.
      • North: Aniruddha, facing north.
      • North-North: Varāha, facing north.
      • Northeast gallery of the eastern door: Reclining deity (Yoga-śayana), facing east.
    • They place the bathing, festival, and offering images in the front porch (mukha-maṇṭapa) or to the right of the inner sanctum.

  • Closing Invocation: Reciting the Ātma-sūkta, touching the feet of the fixed image, and writing the syllables on the fixed image, the Ācārya stands facing north on the right side. Taking the energy from the pot, saying “May the Lord come!”, he invokes Him upon the head of the fixed image. He invokes the respective deity forms into the other fixed images. If the other fixed images are damaged or absent, he invokes all the forms on the head of the primary Ādimūrti. Flanking Him, he invokes Śrī on the right and Harinī on the left. Translating the energy from the fixed image into the vessel water like one lamp lighting another, he invokes the energy into the movable and subsidiary images in order.

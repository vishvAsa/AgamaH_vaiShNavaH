(Ekakālotsavādi-vidhiḥ)

Section 1: Liturgical Rules for One-Day Festivals, Presiding Day-Deities, and the Concluding Bath

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ चतुष्पञ्चाशत्तमः पटलः एककालोत्सवादि-विधिः - atha catuṣpañcāśattamaḥ paṭalaḥ ekakālotsavādi-vidhiḥ -

(अशक्तौ) एवं कर्तुम् अशक्तश्चेत्, पूर्ववत् द्विगुणार्चनं हविर्निवेदनं कुम्भदेवाराधनं होमं बलिदानं च द्विकालं कृत्वा; देवेशं सायाह्ने पूर्ववद् अलङ्कृत्य उत्सवं कारयेत् इति ॥ (aśaktou) evaṃ kartum aśaktaścet, pūrvavat dviguṇārcanaṃ havisnivedanaṃ kumbhadevarādhanaṃ homaṃ balidānaṃ ca dvikālaṃ kṛtvā; deveśaṃ sāyāhne pūrvavad alaṅkṛtya utsavaṃ kārayet iti.

(अतिवातादि-दोषे) अतिवात-अतिवृष्टि-शत्रु-चोरादि-भीत्या सङ्कुले सति आलये प्रथमावरणे द्वितीये वा बलि-निर्वापनम् उत्सवं च कारयेत् ॥ (ativātādi-doṣe) ativāta-ativṛṣṭi-śatru-corādi-bhītyā saṅkule sati ālaye prathamāvaraṇe dvitīye vā bali-nirvāpanam utsavaṃ ca kārayet.

(अवताराणाम् उत्सवे विशेषः) अवताराणाम् उत्सवं यदि कुर्यात्, पूर्वेद्युः रात्रि-पूजा-अवसाने अभ्यर्च्य हविर्निवेद्य; प्रतिसरं बद्ध्वा; शयनाधिवासं कृत्वा; प्रभाते देवम् उत्थाप्य; पाद्य-आचमनं दत्त्वा; आस्थानमण्डपे सिंहासने विष्टरे वा संस्थाप्य; अभ्यर्च्य; राजवद् उपचारं कृत्वा; संस्नाप्य; अभ्यर्च्य; गोभ्यः तृण-मुष्टिं प्रदाय; महाहविः प्रभूतं वा निवेद्य; पानीय-आचमन-मुखवासं दत्त्वा; सायमन्ते उत्सवं कारयेत्। हिरण्य-पशु-घृत-तण्डुल-ताम्बूल-आदीन् आचार्याय तच्छिष्येभ्यः सोदकं दद्यात्। (avatārāṇām utsave viśeṣaḥ) avatārāṇām utsavaṃ yadi kuryāt, pūrvedyuḥ rātri-pūjā-avasāne abhyarcya havisnivedya; pratisaraṃ baddhvā; śayanādhivāsaṃ kṛtvā; prabhāte devam utthāpya; pādya-ācamanaṃ dattvā; āsthānamaṇḍape siṃhāsane viṣṭare vā saṃsthāpya; abhyarcya; rājavad upacāraṃ kṛtvā; saṃsnāpya; abhyarcya; gobhyaḥ tṛṇa-muṣṭiṃ pradāya; mahāhavis prabhūtaṃ vā nivedya; pānīya-ācamana-muṣavāsaṃ dattvā; sāyamante utsavaṃ kārayet. hiraṇya-paśu-ghṛta-taṇḍul-tāmbūl-ādīn ācāryāya tacchiṣyebhyaḥ sodakaṃ dadyāt.

तीर्थदिनात् पूर्वदिने प्रातःसन्ध्यार्चन-अन्ते पूर्ववद् होमाद्यं सर्वं कृत्वा; देवं चित्ररथ-अक्षादिना (मुद्रितपाठे चित्रर(क)ख्यादिना) सायुधम् अलङ्कृत्य; गज-अश्व-आदि-आरूढ-जनैः सार्धं मृगयोत्सवं कारयेत्। अथवा अवभृथ-दिनात् द्वितीये चतुर्थे वा शान्तम् अलङ्कृत्य मृगयोत्सवं कारयेत्। तद्रात्रौ (मुद्रितपाठे तद्रौ) पूर्ववत् सर्वं कृत्वा; देवं ग्रामं प्रदक्षिणीकारयित्वा; अभ्यन्तरं प्रविश्य, संस्नाप्य; अभ्यर्च्य; हविः निवेद्य; अभ्यन्तरे अन्तर्मण्डले शयनस्थाने वा पूर्ववत् प्रतिसरं बद्ध्वा; तथैव शाययित्वा; प्रभाते देवेशम् उत्थाप्य; पाद्याद्यैः अभ्यर्च्य; तीर्थस्नान-कालात् पूर्वं हविः निवेदयेत्। अन्यत् सर्वं पूर्ववत् कारयेत् ॥ tīrthadināt pūrvedine prātaḥsandhyārcana-ante pūrvavad homādyaṃ sarvaṃ kṛtvā; devaṃ citraratha-axādinā sāyudham alaṅkṛtya; gaja-aśva-ādi-ārūḍha-janaiḥ sārdhaṃ mṛgayotsavaṃ kārayet. athavā avabhṛtha-dināt dvitīye caturthe vā śāntam alaṅkṛtya mṛgayotsavaṃ kārayet. tadrātrou pūrvavat sarvaṃ kṛtvā; devaṃ grāmaṃ pradacṣiṇīkārayitvā; abhyantaraṃ praviśya, saṃsnāpya; abhyarcya; havis nivedya; abhyantare antarmaṇḍale śayanasthāne vā pūrvavat pratisaraṃ baddhvā; tathaiva śāyayitvā; prabhāte deveśam utthāpya; pādyādyaiḥ abhyarcya; tīrthasnāna-kālāt pūrvaṃ havis nivedayet. anyat sarvaṃ pūrvavat kārayet.

(उत्सवाधिदेवत-कथनम्) उत्सवाधिदैवतं तिथिवारनक्षत्रदैवतं च क्रमेण वक्ष्ये — प्रथमे ब्राह्मं, द्वितीये आर्षं, तृतीये रौद्रं, चतुर्थे वासवं, पञ्चमे सौम्यं, षष्ठे वैष्णवं, सप्तमे सर्वदैवतं, अष्टमे याम्यं, नवमदिने वारुणं च जुहुयात्। (utsavādidevata-kathanam) utsavādidevataṃ tithivāranaxatradevataṃ ca krameṇa vakṣye — prathame brāhmaṃ, dvitīye ārṣaṃ, tṛtīye raudraṃ, caturthe vāsavaṃ, pañcame soumyaṃ, ṣaṣṭhe vaiṣṇavaṃ, saptame sarvadevatam, aṣṭame yāmyaṃ, navamadine vāruṇaṃ ca juhuyāt.

प्रथमतिथौ आग्नेयं, द्वितीयायां प्राजापत्यं, तृतीयायां कौबेरं, चतुर्थ्यां वैघ्नं, पञ्चम्यां श्रीदैवत्यं, षष्ठ्यां कौमारं, सप्तम्याम् आदित्यं, अष्टम्यां रौद्रं, नवम्यां दौर्गं, दशम्यां याम्यं, एकादश्याम् ऐन्द्रं, द्वादश्यां वैष्णवं, त्रयोदश्यां मातृदैवत्यं, चतुर्दश्याम् ऐशं, पञ्चदश्यां सौम्यं च जुहुयात्। prathamatithou āgneyaṃ, dvitīyāyāṃ prājāpatyaṃ, tṛtīyāyāṃ kouberaṃ, caturthyāṃ vaighnaṃ, pañcamyāṃ śrīdaivataṃ, ṣaṣṭhyāṃ koumāraṃ, saptamyām ādityaṃ, aṣṭamyāṃ raudraṃ, navamyāṃ dourgaṃ, daśamyāṃ yāmyaṃ, ekādaśyām aindraṃ, dvādaśyāṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ, trayodaśyāṃ mātṛdaivataṃ, caturdaśyām aiśaṃ, pañcadaśyāṃ soumyaṃ ca juhuyāt.

सूर्यवारादिषु तत्-तत्-दैवतं जुहुयात्। sūryavārādiṣu tat-tat-daivataṃ juhuyāt.

‘अग्नये कृत्तिकाभ्यः स्वाहा’ इत्यादि तत्-तत्-नक्षत्रे जुहुयात्। वैष्णवं विष्णुसूक्तं पुरुषसूक्तं तदालयगत-परिषद्-देवानां मूर्तिमन्त्रांश्च आज्यमिश्र-चरुणा वैष्णवान्तं सायं प्रातः जुहोति। ‘agnaye kyttikābhyaḥ svāhā’ ityādi tat-tat-naxatre juhuyāt. vaiṣṇavaṃ viṣṇusūktaṃ puruṣasūktaṃ tadālayagata-pariṣad-devānāṃ mūrtimantrāṃśca ājyamiśra-caruṇā vaiṣṇavāntaṃ sāyaṃ prātaḥ juhoti.

तीर्थदिने प्रातरुत्सवे कृते, तत्र आस्थानमण्डपे विष्टरे देवं संस्थाप्य, अष्टोपचारैः अभ्यर्च्य, अभिमुखे गरुडं, दक्षिणे चक्रं, उत्तरे विष्वक्सेनं च धान्यपीठे संस्थाप्य, अभ्यर्च्य; देवाभिमुखे गोमयेन उपलिप्य; धान्यानि आस्तीर्य; उलूखल-मुसलौ (मुद्रितपाठे उलूखलमूसलौ) संन्यस्य, वस्त्रेण आवेष्ट्य; तयोः ब्रह्मेेशानौ (ब्रह्मोशौ) अभ्यर्च्य; हरिद्रायां लक्ष्मीम् अभ्यर्च्य; पुष्पादीन् संशोध्य; हरिद्राम् उलूखले प्रक्षिप्य; ‘अतो देवा’ (ऋ॰ सं॰ १-२२-१६) इति मन्त्रान् जप्त्वा; आचार्यः किञ्चिद् अवघातं कृत्वा; ततो देवदासीभिः भक्तैः वा अवघातं कारयित्वा; चूर्णीकृत्य; कलशान् चतुरः, द्वौ, एकं वा पूरयित्वा; देवाभिमुखे धान्योपरि दर्भान् आस्तीर्य; कलशान् संन्यस्य; वस्त्रेण आच्छाद्य; अधिदेवतां शिनीवालीम् अभ्यर्च्य; देवम् आराध्य; सुगन्धि-तैले अधिदैवतं विष्णुम् अभ्यर्च्य; वैष्णवं मन्त्रम् उच्चार्य; ततोऽभ्यञ्जनं (मुद्रितपाठे तनोऽभ्यञ्जनं) कृत्वा; ‘वेदाहम्’ इति चूर्णेन अभिषिच्य; तच्छिष्टेन चक्रादींश्च अभिषिच्य; तच्चूर्णं चक्रं च समादाय; ग्रामं प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य; चक्रसन्निधौ भक्तानां ब्राह्मणानां शिरःसु तत् चूर्णं विकिरेत्। तत् चूर्णं किञ्चिद् अपि शिरसा धारयेत्, तस्य अशुभानि विनश्यन्ति। बल्युत्सवाद्यनुगता ये सर्वे ते विप्रसमाः, तस्माद् अस्पृश्य-स्पर्शनं न दोषाय भवेत् ॥ tīrthadine prātarutsave kyte, tatra āsthānamaṇḍape viṣṭare devaṃ saṃsthāpya, aṣṭopacāraiḥ abhyarcya, abhimuxe garuḍaṃ, daxiṇe cakraṃ, uttare viṣvakṣenaṃ ca dhānyapīṭhe saṃsthāpya, abhyarcya; devābhimuxe gomeyena upalipya; dhānyāni āstīrya; ulūkhala-musalou saṃnyasya, vastreṇa āveṣṭya; tayyoḥ brahmeśānou abhyarcya; haridrāyāṃ laxmīm abhyarcya; puṣpādīn saṃśodhya; haridrām ulūkhale praxiṣya; ‘ato devā’ iti mantrān japtvā; ācāryaḥ kiñcid avaghātaṃ kṛtvā; tato devadāsībhiḥ bhaktairvā avaghātaṃ kārayitvā; cūrṇīkytya; kalaśān caturaḥ, dvou, ekaṃ vā pūrayitvā; devābhimuxe dhānyopari darbhān āstīrya; kalaśān saṃnyasya; vastreṇa ācchādya; adhidevatāṃ sinīvālīm abhyarcya; devam ārādhya; sugandhi-taile adhidevataṃ viṣṇum abhyarcya; vaiṣṇavaṃ mantram uccārya; tato’bhyañjanaṃ kṛtva; ‘vedāham’ iti cūrṇena abhiṣicya; tacciṣṭena cakrādīṃśca abhiṣicya; taccūrṇaṃ cakraṃ ca samādāya; grāmaṃ pradacṣiṇīkytya; cakrasannidhou bhaktānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ śiraḥsu tat cūrṇaṃ vikiret. tat cūrṇaṃ kiñcid api śirasā dhārayet, tasya aśubhāni vinaśyanti. balyutsavādyanugatā ye sarve te viprasamāḥ, tasmād aspṛśya-sparśanaṃ na doṣāya bhavet.

मध्याह्ने अवभृथं प्रदानं (अपराह्णे वा), पूर्वाह्ने देवेशं यानम् आरोप्य, चक्र-विष्वक्सेन-शान्तान् पुरस्कृत्य, तीर्थस्नानाय गच्छेत्। आलयाभिमुखे विहितम् ऐशान्याम् उत्तरे वा, तदभावे यथेष्ट-तटाके, नद्यां, नदीसङ्गमे, समुद्रे वा; तस्माद् धनुःसहस्राभ्याम् (मुद्रितपाठे द्द्रुनुससहस्राभ्याम्) ऊर्ध्वं न गच्छेत्। madhyāhne avabhṛthaṃ pradānaṃ (aparāhṇe vā), pūrvāhne deveśaṃ yānam āropya, cakra-viṣvakṣena-śāntān puraskṛtya, tīrthasnānāya gacchet. ālayābhimuxe vihitam aiśānyām uttare vā, tadabhāve yatheṣṭa-taṭāke, nadyāṃ, nadīsangaame, samudre vā; tasmād dhanuḥsahasrābhyām ūrdhvaṃ na gacchet.

तत्तीरे प्रपां कृत्वा, अलङ्कृत्य, यस्यां दिशि जलं तदभिमुखं देवं प्रतिष्ठाप्य, अभ्यर्च्य; तथैव चक्रादीन् संस्थाप्य; दश कलशान् (मुद्रितपाठे दशकलाशान्) आहृत्य; तन्तुना वेष्ट्य संशोध्य; सिद्धार्थोदक-अक्षतोदक-कुशोदक-जप्योदक-रत्नोदकैः प्रधानान् पूरयित्वा; उपस्नानान् शुद्धोदकैः पूरयित्वा; देवाभिमुखे धान्यवेद्यां मध्ये सिद्धार्थोदकं, पूर्वे अक्षतोदकं, दक्षिणस्यां कुशोदकं, प्रतीच्यां जप्योदकं, उदीच्यां रत्नोदकं च संन्यस्य; तत्-तत्-वामपार्श्वे तत्-तत्-उपस्नानकलशान् संन्यस्य; वस्त्रेण आच्छाद्य; अधिदेवान् — आदित्य-अप्स्वरः-काश्यप-बृहस्पति-मुनि-तक्षक-सर्वमन्त्र-विद्याधर-विष्णु-गन्धर्वान् दश एतान् कलशेषु क्रमेण अभ्यर्च्य; पुण्याहं कृत्वा; देवं पाद्याद्यर्घ्यान्तम् अभ्यर्च्य; मध्यमादि-पञ्च-कलशोदकैः ‘शन्नो देवीः’ (ऋ॰ सं॰ १०-९-४), ‘पूतस्तस्य’ (वै॰ म॰ प्र॰ ३), ‘इमा ओषधयः’ (ऋ॰ सं॰ १०-९७-१), ‘अभित्वा शूर’ (ऋ॰ सं॰ ७-३२-२२), ‘चत्वारि वाक्’ (ऋ॰ सं॰ १-१६४-४५) इति, ‘वारीश्चतस्रः’ (वै॰ म॰ प्र॰ ३) इति सर्व-उपस्नानैश्च अभ्युक्ष्य; तच्छिष्ट-उदकैः चक्रं संस्नाप्य; तच्चक्रं विष्वक्सेनं गरुडं च तीर्थजले निमज्जयेत्। केवलं चक्रं वा निमज्जयेत्। पश्चात् सर्वजनाः तत्तीर्थे जले देवसन्निधौ स्नानं कुर्युः; ते सर्वे पापविमुक्ताः विष्णुलोकं गच्छन्ति। tattīre prapāṃ kṛtvā, alaṅkṛtya, yasyāṃ diśi jalaṃ tadabhimuxaṃ devaṃ pratiṣṭhāpya, abhyarcya; tathaiva cakrādīn saṃsthāpya; daśa kalaśān āhṛtya; tantunā veṣṭya saṃśodhya; siddhārthodaka-axatodaka-kuśodak-japyodak-ratnodakaiḥ pradhānān pūrayitvā; upasnānān śuddhodakaiḥ pūrayitvā; devābhimuxe dhānyaveddyāṃ madhye siddhārthodakaṃ, pūrve axatodakaṃ, daxiṇasyāṃ kuśodakaṃ, pratīcyāṃ japyodakaṃ, udīcyāṃ ratnodakaṃ ca saṃnyasya; tat-tat-vāmapārśve tat-tat-upasnānakalaśān saṃnyasya; vastreṇa ācchādya; adhidevān — āditya-apsaraḥ-kāśyapa-bṛhaspati-muni-taxaka-sarvamantra-vidyādhara-viṣṇu-gandharvān daśa etān kalaśeṣu krameṇa abhyarcya; पश्चाद् आस्थानमण्डपे देवं संस्थाप्य, चतुर्दिक्षु चतुर्वेद-अध्ययनं कारयित्वा, स्तोत्रैः जयशब्दैश्च संस्तूय; अभ्यन्तरे स्वे स्वे स्थाने संस्थाप्य; देव्योः चक्रादींश्च समविंशति-भेदैः अभ्यर्च्य; विष्णुसूक्तेन पञ्चप्रणामान् कृत्वा; अष्टाक्षरेण पुष्पाञ्जलिं दत्त्वा; यज्ञालयं गत्वा, अग्निं प्रज्वाल्य, परिस्तीर्य, पूर्णाहुतिं हुत्वा, अग्निं विसृज्य; कुम्भवेदिम् आसाद्य; कुम्भस्थान् देवान् मध्यादि-क्रमेण प्रणवेन ध्रुवबेरे समारोपयेत् ॥ paścād āsthānamaṇḍape devaṃ saṃsthāpya, caturdikṣu caturveda-adhyayanaṃ kārayitvā, stotraiḥ jayaśabdaiśca saṃstūya; abhyantare sve sve sthāne saṃsthāpya; devyoḥ cakrādīṃśca samaviṃśati-bhedaiḥ abhyarcya; viṣṇusūktena pañcapraṇāmān kṛtvā; aṣṭāxareṇa puṣpāñjaliṃ dattvā; yajñālayaṃ gatvā, agniṃ prajvālya, paristīrya, pūrṇāhutiṃ hutvā, agniṃ visrjya; kumbhavedim āsādya; kumbhasthān devān madhyadi-krameṇa praṇavena dhruvabere samāropeyet.

बालालयं चेत् कौतुके समारोपयेत्। bālālayaṃ cet koutuke samāropeyet.

तद्रात्रौ (मुद्रितपाठे तद्रौ) ध्वजावरोहणं कुर्यात्। कलश-बलिद्रव्यैः युक्तमन्त्रं कटाहे प्रक्षिपेत्। चक्रादीन् विना पूर्ववद् आलयगतेभ्यो ग्रामे ब्रह्मादिभ्यश्च वाद्यघोषसमायुक्तं बलिं दत्त्वा; सन्धिस्थान् देवान् विसृज्य; वाद्यघोषं विना तद्ग्रामस्थ-देवालयेषु, शून्यागारे, चैत्यवृक्षमूले, मातृ-दुर्गा-स्कन्द-विघ्नेश-आशार्कादीनां (मुद्रितपाठे धनेशाशाखादीनां) गृहेषु, श्मशानसमीपे, वापी-कूप-तटाक-आरामस्थानेषु, ग्रामचतुःसीमसु, भूतक्रूरदेवस्थानेषु च एवं बलिं विक्षिप्य, अग्निसन्निधौ स्नात्वा; आलयं प्रविश्य; देवं प्रणम्य; ध्वजमन्त्रेण ध्वजावरोहणं कृत्वा; तद्रात्रौ उत्सवान्त-स्नपनं च कारयेत् ॥ tadrātrou dhvajāvarohaṇaṃ kuryāt. kalaśa-balidravyāiḥ yuktamanthaṃ kaṭāhe praxiṣet. cakrādīn vinā pūrvavad ālayagatedbyo grāme brahmādibhyaśca vādyaghoṣasaṃyuktaṃ baliṃ dattvā; sandhisthān devān visrjya; vādyaghoṣaṃ vinā tadgrāmastha-devālayeṣu, śūnyāgāre, caityavṛxamūle, mātṛ-durgā-skanda-vighneśa-āśārkādīnāṃ gṛheṣu, śmaśānasamīpe, vāpī-kūpa-taṭāka-ārāmasthāneṣu, grāmacatuḥsīmasu, bhūtakrūradevasthāneṣu ca evaṃ baliṃ vixipya, agnisannidhou snātvā; alayaṃ praviśya; devaṃ praṇamya; dhvajamantreṇa dhvajāvarohaṇaṃ kṛtvā; tadrātrou utsavānta-snapanaṃ ca kārayet.

तद्दिनात् तृतीयायां पञ्चम्यां वा रात्र्याम् एवं बलिं दत्त्वा ध्वजावरोहणं स्नपनं च कारयेत् इत्येके। taddināt tṛtīyāyāṃ pañcamyāṃ vā rātryām evaṃ baliṃ dattvā dhvajāvarohaṇaṃ snapanaṃ ca kārayet ityeke.

(पुष्पयागविधिः) अथ पुष्पयागविधिं वक्ष्ये — प्रतिष्ठान्ते च उत्सवान्ते विषुवे अयने सूर्य-चन्द्रोपरागे युगान्ते सङ्क्रान्त्यां श्रवणे राज्ञः ग्रामस्य यजमानस्य च जन्मर्क्षे दुस्स्वप्न-राष्ट्रविभ्रम-दुर्भिक्ष-अनावृष्टि-परचक्रभय-महाव्याधिपीडनादि-सर्वोपद्रवेषु च विधिना देवस्य स्नपनं कृत्वा; तदनन्तरे द्वितीयेऽहनि पुष्पयागं कुर्यात्। (puṣpayāgavidhiḥ) ath puṣpayāgavidhiṃ vakṣye — pratiṣṭhānte ca utsavānte viṣuve ayene sūrya-candroporāge yugānte saṅkrāntyāṃ śravaṇe rājñaḥ grāmasya yajamānasya ca janmaxtre dussvapna-rāṣṭravibhrama-durbhixa-anāvrṣṭi-paracakrabhaya-mahāvyādhipīḍanādi-sarvopadraveṣu ca vidhinā devasya snapanaṃ kṛtvā; tadanantere dvitīye’hani puṣpayāgaṃ kuryāt.

प्रतिष्ठोत्सवौ विना अन्येषु विधिना अङ्कुरार्पणं कृत्वा कर्म समारभेत। ततो देवेशम् आस्थानमण्डपे सर्वालङ्कारसंयुक्ते धान्यराशौ संस्थाप्य; अभिमुखे धान्यपीठे पुष्पाधिदेवम् आराध्य, पुष्पाणि संन्यस्य; नव-वस्त्र-उत्तरीयाद्यैः देवम् अलङ्कृत्य; त्रयोदशोपचारैः अभ्यर्च्य; देवस्य दक्षिणे औपासनाग्निकुण्डं कृत्वा, आघारं हुत्वा, हौत्रं प्रशंस्य, देवेशादि-तदालयगत-देवानाम् आवाहनान्तं, जुष्टाकार-स्वाहाकारौ कृत्वा; तथैव पुष्पेशस्य कृत्वा; देवस्य वैष्णवं विष्णुसूक्तं पुरुषसूक्तं प्राजापत्यं व्याहृतीश्च हुत्वा; पद्मेन बिल्वपत्रेण वा विष्णुगायत्रीम् अष्टोत्तरशतं हुत्वा; श्रीदैवत्यं श्रीसूक्तं श्रीदेव्याः, पञ्चभूमिदेवानाम् अपि मन्त्रान् मूर्तिमन्त्रान् च हुत्वा; पुष्पाधिदैवतं वैष्णवान्तं द्वादशकृत्वो जुहुयात्। pratiṣṭhātsovou vinā anyeṣu vidhinā aṅkurārpaṇaṃ kṛtvā karma samārabheta. tato deveśam āsthānamaṇḍape sarvālaṅkārasaṃyukte dhānyarāśou saṃsthāpya; abhimuxe dhānyapīṭhe puṣpādidevam ārādhya, puṣpāṇi saṃnyasya; nava-vastra-uttarīyādyāiḥ devam alaṅkṛtya; trayodaśopacāraiḥ abhyarcya; devasya daxiṇe oupāsanāgnikuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā, āghāraṃ hutvā, houtraṃ praśaṃsya, deveśādi-tadālayagata-devānām āvahanāntaṃ, juṣṭākāra-svāhākārou kṛtvā; tathaiva puṣpeśasya kṛtvā; devasya vaiṣṇavaṃ viṣṇusūktaṃ puruṣusūktaṃ prājāpatyaṃ vyāhṛtīśca hutvā; padmena bilvapatreṇa vā viṣṇugāyatrīm aṣṭottaraśataṃ hutvā; śrīdaivataṃ śrīsūktaṃ śrīdevyāḥ, pañcabhumidevānām api mantrān mūrtimantrān ca hutvā; puṣpādidevataṃ vaiṣṇavāntaṃ dvādaśakṛtvo juhuyāt.

ऋत्विजः सर्वे ‘दधिक्राव्णः’ (ऋ॰ सं॰ ४-३९-६) इति दधिप्राशनं कृत्वा नव-वस्त्र-उत्तरीय-कुण्डलादिभिः अलङ्कृताः वाग्यता भवेयुः। शङ्ख-डिण्डिम-काहल-झल्लरी-मर्द्दल-वीणा-वेणु-मृदङ्गाद्यैः, दक्षिणे पुराणवचनैः, पश्चिमे स्तोत्रध्वनिभिः, उत्तरे सूत्र-शिक्षादिभिश्च घोषयेत्। प्रागादि चतुर्दिक्षु चतुर्वेदान् अध्यापयेत्। ṛtvijaḥ sarve ‘dadhikrāvṇaḥ’ iti dadhiprāśanaṃ kṛtva nava-vastra-uttarīya-kuṇḍalādibhiḥ alaṅkṛtāḥ vāgyatā bhaveyuḥ. śaṅkha-ḍiṇḍima-kāhala-jhallarī-marddala-vīṇā-veṇu-mṛdaṅgādyaiḥ, daxiṇe purāṇavacanaaiḥ, paścime stotradhvanibhiḥ, uttare sūtra-śixādibhiśca ghoṣayet. prāgādi caturdikṣu caturvedān adhyāpayet.

प्रागादि चतुर्दिक्षु दक्षिणवामयोः वा आचार्य-ऋत्विजश्च परात्परतरं गुह्याद्गुह्यतरं सर्ववेदार्थसारम् अष्टाक्षरं द्वादशाक्षरं विष्णुगायत्रीं वा जपन्तः (मुद्रितपाठे जपातः) पुष्पाञ्जलिं पादतो दद्युः; अष्टोत्तरशताऽवसाने पाद्याद्यर्घ्यान्तम् अर्चयेत्। स्तनदघ्नेषु पुष्पेषु पुष्पाणि अधो नयेत्। पुराण-म्लान-वस्त्र-निक्षिप्तानि, दुर्गन्ध-दुर्मनुष्य-अधिष्ठित-देश-जातानि पुष्पाणि वर्जयेत्। द्व्यहातीतानि पद्मानि पुराणानि त्यक्त्वा अन्यानि पुष्पाणि आहरेत्। prāgādi caturdikṣu daxiṇavāmayoḥ vā ācārya-ṛtvijaśca parātparataraṃ guhyādguhyataraṃ sarvavedārthasāram aṣṭāxaraṃ dvādaśāxaraṃ viṣṇugāyatrīṃ va japantaḥ puṣpāñjaliṃ pādato dadyuḥ; aṣṭottaraśatā’vasāne pādyādyarghyāntam arcayet. stanadaghneṣu puṣpeṣu puṣpāṇi adho nayet. purāṇa-mlāna-vastra-nixiṣtāni, durgandha-durmanuṣya-adhiṣṭhita-deśa-jātāni puṣpāṇi varjayet. dvyahātītāni padmāni purāṇāni tyaktvā anyāni puṣpāṇi āharet.

एवं कृत्वा महाहविः प्रभूतं वा निवेद्य; पानीयाचमनं मुखवासं दत्त्वा; अनुलेपनादीनि कृत्वा; अष्टाक्षरमहामन्त्रेण देवं प्रार्थयेत्। वैष्णवं व्याहृतीश्च हुत्वा, अन्तर्होमं जुहुयात्। ग्रामम् आलयं वा नृत्त्व-गीत-वाद्य-युतं प्रदक्षिणं कारयेत्। evaṃ kṛtvā mahāhavis prabhūtaṃ vā nivedya; pānīyācamanaṃ muṣavāsaṃ dattva; anulepanādīni kṛtvā; aṣṭāxaramahāmantreṇa devaṃ prārthayet. vaiṣṇavaṃ vyāhṛtīśca hutvā, antarhomaṃ juhuyāt. grāmam ālayaṃ vā nṛtta-gīta-vādya-yutaṃ pradacṣiṇaṃ kārayet.

एतेन अश्वमेध-फलं प्राप्नोति। पुत्रकामः पुत्रान्, धनकामो धनानि, प्रतिष्ठाकामः प्रतिष्ठां, जयार्थी विजयं, श्रीकामः श्रियं च लभेत। सर्वकामावाप्त्यर्थं सर्वदोषोपशान्त्यर्थं प्रायश्चित्तार्थं च एतत् कुर्यात्। एतेन अर्चायां न्यूनं संपूर्णं भवति। आचार्यादिभ्यो दक्षिणां दद्यात्। एतन् न अशुश्रूषवे, नाऽभक्ताय, नाऽसम्मताय, नाऽसच्छिष्याय, नाऽसत्पात्राय दद्यात्। सुभक्ताय सुशिष्याय सुसंयतायैव दद्यात्। इति आह मरीचिः ॥ etena aśvamedha-phalaṃ prāpnoti. putrakāmaḥ putrān, dhanakāmo dhanāni, pratiṣṭhākāmaḥ pratiṣṭhāṃ, jayārthī vijayaṃ, śrīkāmaḥ śriyaṃ ca labhet. sarvakāmāvāptyarthaṃ sarvadoṣopaśāntyarthaṃ prāyaścittārthaṃ ca etat kuryāt. etena arcāyāṃ nyūnaṃ saṃpūrṇaṃ bhavati. ācāryādibhyo daxiṇāṃ dadyāt. etan na aśuśrūṣave, nā’bhaktāya, nā’sammatāya, nā’sacchiṣyāya, nā’satpātrāya dadyāt. subhaktāya suśiṣyāya susaṃyatāyaiva dadyāt. iti āha marīciḥ.

अथातो यजमानः श्रद्धया प्रधानाचार्यपूर्व पञ्चपदार्थिभ्यः पञ्चपरिचारकेभ्यश्च यथोक्तदक्षिणां सोदकं देवसन्निधौ दद्यात्। वैष्णवान् सम्पूज्य; नर्तकान् गायकान् परिचारकान् अन्यांश्च यथार्हं पूजयेत्। अत्रोपयुक्तं सर्वम् आचार्यायैव दद्यात्। हरेः देवेशस्य उत्सवे प्रवृत्ते तद्द्वास्त्वङ्गानां देवानाम् उत्सवं न कारयेत्। अन्यदेवोत्सवे प्रवृत्ते सौम्यमूर्तेः हरेः उत्सवे न दोषाय भवेत्; नराः कल्याणं न कुर्युः। athāto yajamānaḥ śraddhayā pradhānācāryapūrvaṃ pañcapadārthibhyaḥ pañcaparicārakebhyaśca yathoktadaxiṇāṃ sodakaṃ devasannidhou dadyāt. vaiṣṇavān saṃpūjya; nartakān gāyakān paricārakān annyāṃśca yathārhaṃ pūjayet. atropayuktaṃ sarvam ācāryāyaiva dadyāt. hareḥ deveśasya utsave pravṛtte taddvāstvāṅgānāṃ devānām utsavaṃ na kārayet. anyadevotsove pravṛtte soumyamūrteḥ hareḥ utsove na doṣāya bhavet; narāḥ kalyāṇaṃ na kuryuḥ.

एवं यः कुरुते भक्त्या तस्य कायकृतं पापं तत्क्षणाद् एव नश्यति। दश पूर्वान् दश अवरान् आत्मानं च एकविंशतिकं देवैः अपि अभिलक्ष्यं (मुद्रितपाठे देवैरुप्प(न)भिलक्ष्यं) विष्णोः परमपदं नयेत्। तद्ग्रामवासिनां सर्वेषां व्याधयः शान्तिं यान्ति प्रजावृद्धिश्च भवतीति विज्ञायते ॥ evaṃ yaḥ kurute bhaktyā tasya kāyakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ tatkṣaṇād eva naśyati. daśa pūrvān daśa avarān ātmānaṃ ca ekaviṃśatikaṃ devāiḥ api abhilaxyaṃ viṣṇoḥ paramapadaṃ nayet. tadgrāmavāsināṃ sarveṣāṃ vyādhayaḥ śāntiṃ yānti prajāvṛddhiśca bhavatīti vijñāyate.

English Translation

  • Inability to Perform Elaborate Rituals (aśaktou): If the patron is unable to perform the elaborate nine-day festival, he should carry out the double worship, food offerings, pot-deity worship, fire-offerings, and bali-offerings twice daily as before, decorate the Lord in the evening, and perform the procession.

  • Festivals in Times of Danger: In times of strong winds, heavy rain, or fear of enemies and thieves, one should perform the bali-offering and the festival procession within the first or second temple enclosure (āvaraṇa).

  • Festivals for Incarnations (avatāras): If performing the festival for the Lord’s incarnations, having worshipped Him at the end of the night worship on the previous day, offered food, tied the wrist-band, and laid Him to sleep, he should wake Him in the morning, give pādya and ācamana, place Him on a throne or seat in the assembly hall, worship Him with royal services, bathe Him, offer a handful of grass to cows, present the great food offering or abundant food, give water, rinsing water, and mouth-fresheners, and perform the procession in the evening. He should donate gold, cows, ghee, rice, and betel leaves along with water to the priest and his disciples.

  • The Hunting Festival (mṛgayotsava): On the day before the concluding bath day, at the end of the morning worship, having completed all fire-offerings as before, decorating the Lord with weapons on a multi-colored chariot or vehicle (citra-ratha), he should perform the hunting festival accompanied by people riding on elephants, horses, and other mounts. Alternatively, he should perform the hunting festival on the second or fourth day after the concluding bath day, decorating Him peacefully. That night, having performed all rites as before, leading the Lord in procession around the village, entering the temple, bathing Him, worshipping Him, and offering food; he should tie the wrist-band as before in the sleeping chamber within the inner enclosure, lay Him to sleep, wake Him in the morning, worship Him with pādya, etc. and offer food before the time of the concluding bath. All other rites should be done as before.

  • Presiding Deities of the Nine Festival Days: I shall now describe in order the presiding deities of the festival days, dates, weekdays, and constellations: Brahmā on the first day; the Sages (Ārṣa) on the second; Rudra on the third; Indra (Vāsava) on the fourth; Soma on the fifth; Viṣṇu on the sixth; the All-deities (Sarva-daivata) on the seventh; Yama on the eighth; and Varuṇa on the ninth day.

  • Presiding Deities of the Lunar Dates (Tithis): Offer oblations to: Agni on the first tithi (prathamā); Prajāpati on the second; Kubera on the third; Vighneśa (Gaṇeśa) on the fourth; Śrī (Lakṣmī) on the fifth; Kumāra (Subrahmaṇya) on the sixth; Āditya (Sun) on the seventh; Rudra on the eighth; Durgā on the ninth; Yama on the tenth; Indra on the eleventh; Viṣṇu on the twelfth; the Mother Goddesses (Mātṛs) on the thirteenth; Iśāna (Śiva) on the fourteenth; and Soma on the fifteenth tithi.

  • Weekday and Star Oblations: Offer oblations to the respective ruling deities of Sunday and other weekdays. Offer oblations to the respective ruling stars reciting “Agnaye kṛttikābhyaḥ svāhā” and so on. In the morning and evening, the priest should offer oblations ending with Vaishnava mantras, using cooked rice mixed with ghee, for Viṣṇu, the Viṣṇu-sūkta, Puruṣa-sūkta, and the form-mantras of the deities of the temple enclosures.

  • The Turmeric Crushing Ritual (Cūrṇa-saṃskāra): On the concluding bath day, having completed the morning festival, placing the Lord on a seat in the assembly hall, worshipping Him with eight services, placing Garuḍa in front, the Cakra on His right, and Viṣvakṣena on His left on a grain pedestal, worshipping them, smearing the floor in front of the deity with cow dung, spreading grains, placing a mortar and pestle upright, wrapping them with a cloth, worshipping Brahmā and Iśāna on them, and worshipping Goddess Lakṣmī on the turmeric roots. Having purified the flowers and other items, throwing the turmeric roots into the mortar, chanting the Ato devā and other hymns, the priest should strike it slightly, and then have the pounding completed by temple maidens (devadāsīs) or devotees.

  • Turmeric Bath and Sprinkling: Having ground it into powder, filling four, two, or one water pots, spreading Darbha grass on grains in front of the deity, placing the pots on it, covering them with a cloth, worshipping the presiding deity Śinīvālī, worshipping the Lord, and applying scented oil to the Lord while invoking Viṣṇu as the presiding deity. Reciting the Vaishnava mantra, applying the oil, bathing the Lord with the turmeric powder reciting “Vedāham”, bathing the Cakra and other deities with the remaining powder, and taking the powder and the Cakra, they should circumambulate the village. At the temple gate or in front of the Cakra, the priest should sprinkle the turmeric powder on the heads of the devoted Brahmins. Even a tiny speck of this powder borne on one’s head destroys all inauspiciousness. All those who follow the bali-procession are equal to Brahmins; therefore, touching during the procession does not cause any defect.

  • The Concluding Bath (Avabhṛtha): At noon (or in the afternoon or morning), mounting the Lord on a vehicle, placing the Cakra, Viṣvakṣena, and the peaceful deities in front, he should proceed for the concluding bath. The bath is prescribed in front of the temple, towards the Northeast or North, or in a desired temple tank, river, confluence of rivers, or the sea. One should not go more than two thousand bow-lengths (dhanus) away from the temple. Setting up a pavilion on the shore, decorating it, placing the Lord facing the direction of the water, and worshipping Him; place the Cakra and other deities in the same way.

  • The Ten Bathing Vessels: Gathering ten pots, wrapping them with thread, purifying them, fill the primary pots with mustard-seed water (siddhārthodaka), unhusked rice water (akṣatodaka), Darbha grass water (kuśodaka), mantra water (japyodaka), and gem water (ratnodaka), and fill the secondary pots with pure water. In front of the deity, on the grain altar, place: mustard water in the center, unhusked rice water in the East, Darbha water in the South, mantra water in the West, and gem water in the North. Place the secondary pots on their left sides, cover them with a cloth, and worship the ten deities: Āditya, Apsaras, Kāśyapa, Bṛhaspati, Muni, Takṣaka, Sarvamantra, Vidyādhara, Viṣṇu, and Gandharva in order in the pots.

  • The Holy Dip: Having performed the purification ritual, worshipping the Lord with services from pādya to arghya, bathing Him with the water from the five central pots reciting the Śaṃ no devīḥ, Pūtastasya, Imā oṣadhayaḥ, Abhi tvā śūra, and Catvāri vāk hymns, and sprinkling Him with all the secondary water pots reciting “Vārīṣcatasraḥ”; bathe the Cakra with the remaining water. Then, dip the Cakra, Viṣvakṣena, and Garuḍa in the sacred bathing waters. Alternatively, one may dip only the Cakra. Afterwards, all the devotees should bathe in the sacred water in the presence of the Lord. They are all freed from sins and reach the world of Viṣṇu.

  • Return to the Temple: Having bathed the Lord with pure water, dried Him with a cloth, decorated Him with new garments, upper cloths, and ornaments, worshipped Him with eight services, decorated the Cakra and other deities with garments, and mounted Him on the vehicle; they should enter the temple while scattering parched grains, unhusked rice, and flowers in the street, with the Cakra, Viṣvakṣena, and the peaceful deities in front, the priest chanting the Śakuna-sūkta, and accompanied by water streams, all ornaments, musical instruments, and hymns. At the temple gate, the officiating priests should welcome the Lord with lamps, sprouts, and water pots.

  • Re-infusion of Divine Energy: Placing the Lord in the assembly hall, having the four Vedas recited in the four directions, praising Him with hymns and cries of victory, establishing Him in His respective place in the sanctum, and worshipping the two Goddesses, the Cakra, and others with twenty specific varieties of service. Offering five prostrations reciting the Viṣṇu-sūkta, offering a flower-offering with the eight-syllable mantra, going to the sacrificial hall, lighting the fire, spreading grass, offering the final oblation (pūrṇāhuti), dismissing the fire; approaching the pot altar, he should transfer the deities from the pots back into the stationary deity (dhruva-bera) in order starting from the center, using the syllable Om. If it is a temporary temple (bālālaya), he should transfer them into the kautuka-bera.

  • Lowering the Flag (Dhvajāvarohaṇa): That night, he should lower the flag. He should pour the remaining water from the pots and the offering substances into a large cauldron. Accompanied by musical instruments, he should scatter the bali-offerings to Brahmā and others in the village, excluding the Cakra and other icons, dismiss the enclosure deities, and then without musical instruments, scatter the bali-offerings at the village temples, empty houses, roots of sacred trees, and houses of the Matrikas, Durgā, Skanda, Gaṇeśa, and Āditya, near the cremation ground, at stepwells, wells, tanks, and groves, at the four boundaries of the village, and at the shrines of fierce spirits (bhūtas). Having bathed near the fire, entering the temple, bowing to the Lord, and lowering the flag with the flag-mantra, he should perform the concluding bath (snapana) of the festival that night. Some say that one should perform the bali, flag lowering, and bath on the third or fifth night from that day.

  • The Flower Sacrifice (Puṣpayāga-vidhiḥ): Now, the rules for the flower sacrifice (puṣpayāga-vidhiḥ): At the end of a consecration, at the end of a festival, on equinoxes (viṣuva), solstices (ayana), solar and lunar eclipses, at the end of cosmic ages (yugānta), on transit days (saṅkrānti), under the constellation Śravaṇa, under the birth star of the king, the village, or the patron, and in times of bad dreams, national unrest, famine, drought, fear of foreign invasion, and great diseases; having bathed the deity in accordance with the rules, one should perform the flower sacrifice on the following day.

  • Fire Installations for Flower Sacrifice: Except for consecrations and festivals, one should begin the rite by performing the seed-planting (aṅkurārpaṇa) according to the rules. Placing the Lord in the assembly hall on a heap of grains, decorated with all ornaments; worship the presiding deity of flowers (puṣpādhi-deva) on a grain pedestal in front, deposit the flowers, decorate the Lord with new garments and upper cloths, and worship with thirteen services. Making an Aupāsana fire-pit on the right side of the Lord, offering the introductory libation, praising the chanter’s role, invoking the Lord and the deities of the enclosures, performing the juṣṭa and svāhā offerings, and doing the same for the lord of flowers (puṣpeśa). Offering Vaishnava, Viṣṇu-sūkta, Puruṣa-sūkta, and Prajāpatya oblations and the vyāhṛtis. Offering the Viṣṇu-gāyatrī one hundred and eight times using lotus flowers or Bilva leaves, and offering oblations to Goddess Śrī with the Śrī-sūkta, and the mantras and form-mantras of the deities of the five earths. He should offer twelve oblations ending with Vaishnava mantras to the presiding deity of flowers.

  • The Flower Worship: All the priests, having partaken of yogurt reciting “Dadhikrāvṇaḥ”, decorated with new garments, upper cloths, and earrings, should remain silent. They should make proclamations: with conchs, kettledrums, horns, cymbals, drums, lutes, flutes, and clay drums; with Puranic recitations in the South; with hymns in the West; and with the Sūtras and Śikṣā in the North. They should have the four Vedas taught in the four directions starting with the East. In the four directions or on the right and left sides, the priest and the assistant priests, repeating the eight-syllable mantra, the twelve-syllable mantra, or the Viṣṇu-gāyatrī—which are the most supreme, most secret, and the essence of all Vedas—should offer flower-offerings (puṣpāñjali) at the Lord’s feet. At the end of one hundred and eight recitations, worship with pādya and arghya. He should pile up flowers to breast-height and then bring them down. He must reject flowers that are old, withered, wrapped in dirty cloths, malodorous, or grown in places occupied by impure people. Excluding lotuses older than two days, he should gather fresh flowers.

  • Concluding Services and Merit: Offering flowers to breast-height twenty times is the highest (uttama), twelve times is the middle (madhyama), and eight times is the lowest (adhama) service. Having done so, offering the great food-offering or abundant food, giving water, rinsing water, mouth-fresheners, applying ointments, he should pray to the Lord with the great eight-syllable mantra. Offering Vaishnava and vyāhṛti oblations, he should perform the final fire-offering. He should lead the Lord in procession around the village or the temple enclosure accompanied by dance, song, and music. By this, he obtains the fruit of an horse-sacrifice. He who desires a son obtains sons; he who desires wealth, wealth; he who desires status, status; he who seeks victory, victory; and he who seeks prosperity, prosperity. One should perform this to attain all desires, pacify all defects, and as an expiation. By this, any deficiency in the worship is completed. He should give sacrificial fees to the priest and others. He must not give this manual to one who does not serve, who is faithless, who does not agree, who is a bad disciple, or who is unworthy. He should only give it to a good devotee, a good disciple, and a self-controlled person—so says Marīci.

  • Fees and Auspicious Rites: Therefore, the patron with faith should give the specified fees along with water in the presence of the Lord to the five classes of temple officials headed by the chief priest, and to the five classes of servants. Worshipping the Vaishnavas, he should honor the dancers, singers, servants, and others appropriately. He should give all the used items to the chief priest only. When the festival of Lord Viṣṇu is in progress, one should not conduct festivals for the ancillary deities of the temple. While another deity’s festival is in progress, the festival of the peaceful form of Viṣṇu does not cause any defect; however, people should not perform other auspicious ceremonies (like marriages) during this time.

  • Efficacy of the Festival: He who performs this with devotion is instantly freed from all sins committed by the body. He leads ten ancestors, ten descendants, and himself—twenty-one generations in total—to the supreme abode of Viṣṇu, which is desired even by the gods. All the residents of that village are freed from diseases, attain peace, and their offspring increase—so it is understood.

  • The One-Day Festival (Ekāhotsava): Now, the rules for the one-day festival (ekāhotsava-vidhiḥ): One should begin it under the birth star of the king, the village, or the patron, or on the five special days of Viṣṇu. Having performed the seed-planting before that day, gathered the materials; having worshipped the Lord especially at the end of the daily worship on the previous day, offered food, tied the wrist-band as before, laid Him to sleep, woken Him up, and sprinkled water around the fire in the daily fire-pit or hearth at the end of the morning twilight worship. On the concluding bath day, having offered fire-offerings of ghee or cooked rice mixed with ghee with the form-mantras of the deities of the temple enclosures and the day-deity; having made the bali-offerings as before, decorated the Lord, mounted Him on the vehicle, led Him in procession around the village or temple accompanied by all musical instruments and ornaments, entered the temple, performed the turmeric-crushing ritual, bathed Him with turmeric, placed the five pots with secondary vessels in front of the Lord, sprinkled Him, bathed the Cakra with the remaining water, taken the Cakra, entered the bathing waters, dipped it, given the holy bath to the people, dressed Him in another garment, entered the temple, bathed the Lord with pure water, bathed Him with scented water, worshipped Him, offered the great food-offering or abundant food, given water, rinsing water, and mouth-fresheners, and established Him in His original place—he should worship Him—so says Marīci.

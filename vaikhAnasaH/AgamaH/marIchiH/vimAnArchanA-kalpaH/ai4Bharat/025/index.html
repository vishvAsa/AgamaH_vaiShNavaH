(Pātra-paricchadādi-saṃskāra-vidhiḥ)

Section 1: General Consecration of Vessels and Utensils

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ पञ्चविंशः पटलः - अथ पात्रपरिच्छदादीनां संस्कारं वक्ष्ये — यजमान-अनुकूले शुभे नक्षत्रे देवेशं प्रणम्य अङ्कुरार्पणं कृत्वा आलय-अभिमुखे च औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा आधारं हुत्वा, अग्रे पश्चिमतः देवेशं संस्थाप्य, समविंशति-विग्रहैः अभ्यर्च्य, देव-अभिमुखे धान्य-स्थण्डिलं कृत्वा नवकलशान् न्यास्तीर्य, पात्रादीनि पञ्चगव्येन प्रोक्ष्य प्रक्षाल्य अभ्युक्ष्य स्थण्डिले सन्यस्य वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा पर्यग्निं च कारयेत्; अग्निं परिस्तीर्य वैष्णवं, विष्णुसूक्तं, पुरुषसूक्तं, तत्-तद्-अधिदेवता-मूर्ति-मन्त्रं च जुहुयात्; तत्-तद्-अधिपान्, देवेशं च, पात्रादिषु समावाह्य अभ्यर्च्य पुण्याहं वाचयेत्; atha pañcaviṃśaḥ paṭalaḥ - atha pātraparicchadādīnāṃ saṃskāraṃ vakṣye — yajamāna-anukūle śubhe nakṣatre deveśaṃ praṇamya aṅkurārpaṇaṃ kṛtvā ālaya-abhimukhe ca aupāsana-agni-kuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā ādhāraṃ hutvā, agre paścimatḥ deveśaṃ saṃsthāpya, samaviṃśati-vigrahaiḥ abhyarcya, deva-abhimukhe dhānya-sthaṇḍilaṃ kṛtvā navakalśān nyāstīrya, pātrādīni pañcagavyena prokṣya prakṣālya abhyukṣya sthaṇḍile sanyasya vāstu-homaṃ hutvā paryagniṃ ca kārayet; agniṃ paristīrya vaiṣṇavaṃ, viṣṇusūktaṃ, puruṣasūktaṃ, tat-tad-adhidevatā-mūrti-mantraṃ ca juhuyāt; tat-tad-adhipān, deveśaṃ ca, pātrādiṣu samāvāhya abhyarcya puṇyāhaṃ vācayet;

यजमानम् आचार्यं संपूज्य दक्षिणां दद्यात्, अतोदेवादीन् अष्टाक्षरं च जपन् देव-सन्निधौ तत्-तत्-कर्मणि संयोज्य देवम् अभ्यर्च्य यथाशक्ति हविः निवेद्य ब्राह्मणान् भोजयेत्; अधिदैवते अज्ञाते सर्वत्र सेनेशम् अर्चयेत् । एतत् सामान्यम् ॥ yajamānam ācāryaṃ saṃpūjya dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāt, atodevādīn aṣṭākṣaraṃ ca japan deva-sannidhau tat-tat-karmaṇi saṃyojya devam abhyarcya yathāśakti haviḥ nivedya brāhmaṇān bhojayet; adhidaivate ajñāte sarvatra seneśam arcayet. etat sāmānyam.

English Translation

  • Now I shall describe the consecration (saṃskāra) of the ritual vessels and utensils (pātra-paricchadādi).

  • On an auspicious asterism (nakṣatra) favourable to the patron (yajamāna), having bowed to the Lord of Gods (deveśa), one should perform the sprouting rite (aṅkurārpaṇa) and, facing the temple, prepare the aupāsana fire-pit (agnikuṇḍa) and offer the foundation oblation (ādhāra).

  • To the west of the fire, the Lord should be installed and worshipped with the twenty-fold liturgy (samaviṃśati-vigraha).

  • Facing the deity, a grain-platform (dhānya-sthaṇḍila) should be prepared, nine consecrated water-pots (navakalśa) spread upon it, and the vessels sprinkled, washed, and aspersed with the five products of the cow (pañcagavya). Having placed them on the platform, the vāstu-homa and circumambulation by fire (paryagni) should be performed.

  • After spreading the fire (agni-paristīrya), oblations should be offered with the Vaiṣṇava hymn, the Viṣṇu-sūkta, the Puruṣa-sūkta, and the individual deity-image mantras (mūrti-mantra) of each presiding deity.

  • The presiding deities and the Lord should be invoked into the respective vessels, worshipped, and the puṇyāha (auspicious) benediction recited.

  • The patron should honour the officiant (ācārya) and give him a fee (dakṣiṇā). Chanting the Atodeva and the Eight-syllable (aṣṭākṣara) mantras, each vessel should be assigned to its ritual function in the deity’s presence. The deity should be worshipped, food offerings (havis) given according to one’s ability, and Brahmins fed.

  • When the presiding deity of a particular vessel is unknown, Senēśa (Viṣvaksena) should be worshipped everywhere in its place. This is the general procedure (sāmānya).

Section 2: Special Rite for Ornaments (Ābharaṇādīnāṃ Viśeṣavidhiḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ आभरणादीनां विशेषविधिः - अथ विशेषतः वक्ष्ये — मकुटाद्य-आभरण-जालं नाना-रत्न-युक्तं सुवर्णेन तत्-तद्-वर्गानां योग्य-मानेन कारयेत् । देवम् अभ्यर्च्य अभिमुखे सभ्य-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा आधारं हुत्वा वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा पर्यग्निं कारयेत्; पञ्चगव्येन प्रक्षाल्य सकलशैः संस्नाप्य पूर्ववत् स्थण्डिलं कृत्वा नवकलशान् न्यास्तीर्य सुवर्ण-पात्रे तानि संन्यस्य तत्-तद्-अधिदेवान् देवेशं च अभ्यर्च्य, अग्निं परिस्तीर्य तन्त्र-हृद्-दैवत्यं, वैष्णवं, विष्णुसूक्तं, पुरुषसूक्तं, सर्वदैवत्यं च हुत्वा, स्नपन-उक्त-विधिना देवेशं संस्नाप्य वक्षो-माल्यैः अलंकृत्य पुण्याहं वाचयेत्; आचार्यं संपूज्य दक्षिणां दद्यात् । atha ābharaṇādīnāṃ viśeṣavidhiḥ - atha viśeṣataḥ vakṣye — makuṭādya-ābharaṇa-jālaṃ nānā-ratna-yuktaṃ suvarṇena tat-tad-vargānāṃ yogya-mānena kārayet. devam abhyarcya abhimukhe sabhya-agni-kuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā ādhāraṃ hutvā vāstu-homaṃ hutvā paryagniṃ kārayet; pañcagavyena prakṣālya sakalaśaiḥ saṃsnāpya pūrvavat sthaṇḍilaṃ kṛtvā navakalśān nyāstīrya suvarṇa-pātre tāni saṃnyasya tat-tad-adhidevān deveśaṃ ca abhyarcya, agniṃ paristīrya tantra-hṛd-daivatyaṃ, vaiṣṇavaṃ, viṣṇusūktaṃ, puruṣasūktaṃ, sarvadaivatyaṃ ca hutvā, snapana-ukta-vidhinā deveśaṃ saṃsnāpya vakṣo-mālyaiḥ alaṃkṛtya puṇyāhaṃ vācayet; ācāryaṃ saṃpūjya dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāt.

चतुर्दिक्षु स्तोत्र-नृत्त-गीत-(जय)-वाद्यैः स्तोत्रैः जय-शब्दैः च संस्तूय, आचार्यः देवेशं प्रणम्य ‘आत्मसूक्तं’ जप्त्वा देवं ध्यायन् एकादशाक्षरादि पुरुषसूक्तं जप्त्वा ‘भूतो भूते’ इति मकुटाद्य-आभरणादि तत्-तत् अलंकृत्य आस्थान-मण्डपे संस्थाप्य, स्नपन-उक्त-विधिना देवेशं संस्नाप्य अभ्यर्च्य महाहविः प्रभूतं वा निवेद्य अर्चा-पीठे संस्थाप्य अर्चयेत् । caturdikṣu stotra-nṛtta-gīta-(jaya)-vādyaiḥ stotraiḥ jaya-śabdaiḥ ca saṃstūya, ācāryaḥ deveśaṃ praṇamya ‘ātmasūktaṃ’ japtvā devaṃ dhyāyan ekādaśākṣarādi puruṣasūktaṃ japtvā ‘bhūto bhūte’ iti makuṭādya-ābharaṇādi tat-tat alaṃkṛtya āsthāna-maṇḍape saṃsthāpya, snapana-ukta-vidhinā deveśaṃ saṃsnāpya abhyarcya mahāhaviḥ prabhūtaṃ vā nivedya arcā-pīṭhe saṃsthāpya arcayet.

English Translation

  • Now I shall describe the special rite (viśeṣa-vidhi) in detail.

  • Preparation of Ornaments: The network of ornaments beginning with the crown (makuṭādi-ābharaṇa-jāla) should be fashioned from gold, studded with various gems, using the appropriate measure for each category.

  • Fire Rite: Having worshipped the deity and facing Him, the sabhya fire-pit should be prepared. The foundation oblation and vāstu-homa should be offered, and the circumambulation by fire (paryagni) performed. The ornaments should be washed with pañcagavya and bathed with water from the consecrated pots. A grain-platform should be prepared as before, nine water-pots spread upon it, and the ornaments placed in a gold vessel. Each presiding deity and the Lord should be worshipped.

  • Oblations: After spreading the fire, oblations should be offered with the tantra-hṛdaya-daivatya, Vaiṣṇava, Viṣṇu-sūkta, Puruṣa-sūkta, and sarva-daivatya mantras. The Lord should be bathed according to the snapana (bathing) rite, adorned with a chest-garland (vakṣo-mālya), and the puṇyāha benediction recited. The officiant should be honoured and given a fee.

  • Adorning the Deity: In all four directions, hymns of praise (stotra), dance (nṛtta), songs (gīta), and victorious instrumental music (jaya-vādya) should resound. The officiant, having bowed to the Lord and recited the Ātma-sūkta while meditating upon the deity, should chant the Puruṣa-sūkta beginning with the eleven-syllable metre and, reciting ‘bhūto bhūte’ (= “in being after being”), adorn the deity with the crown and other ornaments one by one. The deity should then be installed in the audience pavilion (āsthāna-maṇḍapa), bathed by the snapana rite, worshipped, offered a grand food-offering (mahāhavis) or an abundant offering, and finally placed upon the worship pedestal (arcā-pīṭha) for regular worship.

Section 3: Consecration of the Thousand-stream Vessel and the Śaṅkha-Padma Treasures (Sahasradhārā, Śaṅkhapadmanidhī)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

सहस्रधारां, शङ्ख-पद्म-निधी च विधिना कृत्वा, आलय-अभिमुखे गोमयेन उपलिप्य औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा आधारं हुत्वा, अग्रे पश्चिमतः देवं संस्थाप्य अभ्यर्च्य, अभिमुखे धान्य-पीठं कृत्वा नवकलशान् न्यास्तीर्य, पञ्चगव्येन प्रक्षाल्य सन्यस्य, वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा पर्यग्निं च कृत्वा, वैष्णवं, विष्णुसूक्तं, सौम्यं, निज-मूर्ति-मन्त्रं च हुत्वा; पात्रे चन्द्र-मण्डलं ध्यात्वा, पद्मे पद्मनिधिं शङ्खे शङ्खनिधिं समभ्यर्च्य, पुण्याहं वाचयित्वा अग्निं विसृजेत् । sahasradhārāṃ, śaṅkha-padma-nidhī ca vidhinā kṛtvā, ālaya-abhimukhe gomayena upalipya aupāsana-agni-kuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā ādhāraṃ hutvā, agre paścimataḥ devaṃ saṃsthāpya abhyarcya, abhimukhe dhānya-pīṭhaṃ kṛtvā navakalśān nyāstīrya, pañcagavyena prakṣālya sanyasya, vāstu-homaṃ hutvā paryagniṃ ca kṛtvā, vaiṣṇavaṃ, viṣṇusūktaṃ, saumyaṃ, nija-mūrti-mantraṃ ca hutvā; pātre candra-maṇḍalaṃ dhyātvā, padme padmanidhiṃ śaṅkhe śaṅkhanidhiṃ samabhyarcya, puṇyāhaṃ vācayitvā agniṃ visṛjet.

आचार्यं पूजयित्वा दक्षिणां दद्यात्; देवेशं संस्नाप्य अष्टोपचारैः अभ्यर्च्य वक्षो-माल्य-गन्धादैः अलंकृत्य, नृत्त-गेय-वाद्यैः स्तोत्रैः जय-शब्दैः च घोषयित्वा, आचार्यः प्राङ्मुखः उदङ्मुखः वा स्थित्वा हस्ताभ्यां पद्म-मुद्रां कृत्वा मकुटोपरि धारयन्, शङ्ख-पद्म-अभ्य-पात्र-स्थ-जलं सहस्रधारा-पात्र-स्थ-अपये समर्पयेत्; वसोःपवित्रं पुरुषसूक्तं वा समुच्चार्य अभिषिच्य, पश्चात् आचमनं दत्वा प्लोतेन विमृज्य अभ्यर्च्य हविः निवेद्य, नित्य-नैमित्तिक-आदि-द्विवेलं कारयेत्; एतत् स्नपनं सर्व-दोष-उपशमनं सर्व-सम्पत्-करम् इति विज्ञायते ॥ ācāryaṃ pūjayitvā dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāt; deveśaṃ saṃsnāpya aṣṭopacāraiḥ abhyarcya vakṣo-mālya-gandhādaiḥ alaṃkṛtya, nṛtta-geya-vādyaiḥ stotraiḥ jaya-śabdaiḥ ca ghoṣayitvā, ācāryaḥ prāṅmukhaḥ udaṅmukhaḥ vā sthitvā hastābhyāṃ padma-mudrāṃ kṛtvā makuṭopari dhārayan, śaṅkha-padma-abhya-pātra-stha-jalaṃ sahasradhārā-pātra-stha-apye samarpayet; vasoḥpavitraṃ puruṣasūktaṃ vā samuccārya abhiṣicya, paścāt ācamanaṃ datvā plotena vimṛjya abhyarcya haviḥ nivedya, nitya-naimittika-ādi-dvivelaṃ kārayet; etat snapanaṃ sarva-doṣa-upaśamanaṃ sarva-sampat-karam iti vijñāyate.

English Translation

  • Preparation: Having fashioned the thousand-stream vessel (sahasradhārā) and the Conch-treasure (śaṅkha-nidhi) and Lotus-treasure (padma-nidhi) vessels according to the prescribed method, and facing the temple, the ground should be plastered with cow-dung. The aupāsana fire-pit should be prepared, the foundation oblation offered, the deity installed to the west of the fire and worshipped.

  • Platform and Purification: A grain-platform should be erected in front, nine water-pots placed upon it, and the vessels washed with pañcagavya and placed on the platform. The vāstu-homa and circumambulation by fire should be performed. Oblations should be offered with the Vaiṣṇava, Viṣṇu-sūkta, Saumya (gentle), and the deity’s own image-mantra.

  • Invocation of Treasures: Meditating on a lunar disc (candra-maṇḍala) within the vessel, the Lotus-treasure (padma-nidhi) should be worshipped in the lotus vessel and the Conch-treasure (śaṅkha-nidhi) in the conch vessel. The puṇyāha benediction is recited and the fire dismissed.

  • Bathing Ceremony: The officiant should be honoured with a fee. The Lord should be bathed, worshipped with the eight standard offerings (aṣṭopacāra), and adorned with a chest-garland and sandalwood paste. Accompanied by dance, song, instrumental music, hymns, and shouts of “jaya!”, the officiant — facing east or north, forming the lotus-mudrā with both hands, and holding them above the crown — should pour the water from the conch-and-lotus vessels into the thousand-stream vessel. Reciting the Vasoḥpavitra or the Puruṣa-sūkta, he should consecrate the deity by sprinkling (abhiṣeka). Afterwards, ācamana (sipping water) should be offered, the deity dried with a cloth (plota), worshipped, and a food-offering presented. This should be performed twice daily (dvivela) on regular and special occasions (nitya-naimittika).

  • This bathing rite (snapana) is known to pacify all faults (sarva-doṣa-upaśamana) and bring all prosperity (sarva-sampat-kara).

Section 4: Protective Lamp Rite (Rakṣā-dīpa-vidhiḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

रक्षा-दीप-विधिः - रक्षा-दीप-विधिं वक्ष्ये — नीराजन-पात्रं कृत्वा पूर्ववत् संस्कृत्य, तत्-पात्रे तद्-आधारे घृतेन तैलेन वा पिचु-वर्ति-युत-दीपं ‘शुभज्योतिः’ (वैं.म.६) इति सन्दीप्य, दिक्-कु-पिण्ड-अर्थं हरिद्रा-चूर्णितेन अग्निना दश-पिण्डान् पञ्च-पिण्डान् वा मुष्टि-मात्रं दत्तरं कृत्वा, अलामे अक्षतं पुष्पं वा गन्ध-द्रव्यं च पृथक् पात्रं सङ्गृह्य, देवेशं संपूज्य शङ्ख-ध्वनि-नृत्यं सर्व-वाद्यैः स्तोत्रैः स्वस्ति-घोषैः सह, यजमानः देवदासी वा तत्-पात्रं शिरसा धृत्वा शनैः शनैः गत्वा आलयं प्रविश्य अभिमुखे संस्थापयेत् । rakṣā-dīpa-vidhiḥ - rakṣā-dīpa-vidhiṃ vakṣye — nīrājana-pātraṃ kṛtvā pūrvavat saṃskṛtya, tat-pātre tad-ādhāre ghṛtena tailena vā picu-varti-yuta-dīpaṃ ‘śubhajyotiḥ’ (vaiṃ.m.6) iti sandīpya, dik-ku-piṇḍa-arthaṃ haridrā-cūrṇitena agninā daśa-piṇḍān pañca-piṇḍān vā muṣṭi-mātraṃ dattaraṃ kṛtvā, alāme akṣataṃ puṣpaṃ vā gandha-dravyaṃ ca pṛthak pātraṃ saṅgṛhya, deveśaṃ saṃpūjya śaṅkha-dhvani-nṛtyaṃ sarva-vādyaiḥ stotraiḥ svasti-ghoṣaiḥ saha, yajamānaḥ devadāsī vā tat-pātraṃ śirasā dhṛtvā śanaiḥ śanaiḥ gatvā ālayaṃ praviśya abhimukhe saṃsthāpayet.

आचार्यः पात्रे चन्द्रं दीपे सूर्यं च अग्निं अर्च्य देवस्य पादयोः पुष्पाञ्जलिं दत्वा, तत्-काले शङ्ख-नाद-समन्त्रितं नृत्त-गेय-वाद्यैः युक्तं, ‘श्रिये जात’ (ऋ.सं.७-४-४) इति विम्बस्य मूर्धादि पाद-पर्यन्तं प्रणव-आस्कारं त्रिः-प्रदक्षिणं कारयित्वा, पूर्व-स्थाने न्यस्य देवेशं प्रणम्य, ‘अतोदेवा’ (ऋ.सं.१-२-७) इति प्राग्-आदि दिक्-कु-पिण्डान् विसृज्य, पाद्य-आचमनं दत्वा, ‘तद्विप्रासः’ (ऋ.सं.१-२-७), ‘इमे गन्धाः’ (वैं.म.प्र.६) इति गन्धेन ऊर्ध्व-पुण्ड्रं कृत्वा, अष्टाक्षरेण पुष्पाञ्जलिं ददात्; पूर्ववत् आहृत्य आलयात् बहिः गत्वा भूत-पीठस्य दक्षिण-पार्श्वे निक्षिपेत्; एवं सायं, प्रच्छत्र-पट-उद्धरणे च, उत्सवे, स्नपने, बल्य्-उत्सवे च, अन्येषु पुण्य-क्षेत्रेषु, अर्चना-अन्ते कारयेत् ॥ ācāryaḥ pātre candraṃ dīpe sūryaṃ ca agniṃ arcya devasya pādayoḥ puṣpāñjaliṃ datvā, tat-kāle śaṅkha-nāda-samantritaṃ nṛtta-geya-vādyaiḥ yuktaṃ, ‘śriye jāta’ (ṛ.saṃ.7-4-4) iti vimbasya mūrdhādi pāda-paryantaṃ praṇava-āskāraṃ triḥ-pradakṣiṇaṃ kārayitvā, pūrva-sthāne nyasya deveśaṃ praṇamya, ‘atodevā’ (ṛ.saṃ.1-2-7) iti prāg-ādi dik-ku-piṇḍān visṛjya, pādya-ācamanaṃ datvā, ’tadviprāsaḥ’ (ṛ.saṃ.1-2-7), ‘ime gandhāḥ’ (vaiṃ.m.pr.6) iti gandhena ūrdhva-puṇḍraṃ kṛtvā, aṣṭākṣareṇa puṣpāñjaliṃ dadāt; pūrvavat āhṛtya ālayāt bahiḥ gatvā bhūta-pīṭhasya dakṣiṇa-pārśve nikṣipet; evaṃ sāyaṃ, pracchatra-paṭa-uddharaṇe ca, utsave, snapane, baly-utsave ca, anyeṣu puṇya-kṣetreṣu, arcanā-ante kārayet.

English Translation

  • Lamp Preparation: Now I shall describe the rite of the protective lamp (rakṣā-dīpa). A nīrājana (waving-lamp) vessel should be fashioned and consecrated as before. In that vessel upon its base, a wick-lamp with cotton wicks (picu-varti) should be lit using ghee or oil, chanting ‘śubhajyotiḥ’ (Vaikhānasa Mantra 6).

  • Directional Offerings: For the directional guardian offerings (dik-ku-piṇḍa), ten or five rice-balls (piṇḍa), each fist-sized, should be prepared using turmeric-powdered fire. Unbroken rice (akṣata) or flowers and fragrant substances should be separately gathered in a vessel.

  • Procession: Having worshipped the Lord with conch-blowing, dancing, all instruments, hymns, and auspicious proclamations (svasti-ghoṣa), the patron (yajamāna) or a temple-attendant (devadāsī) should carry the vessel on the head, proceeding slowly into the temple, and place it before the deity.

  • Waving the Lamp: The officiant should worship the Moon in the vessel and the Sun and Fire in the lamp, and offer a handful of flowers at the deity’s feet. At that moment, accompanied by conch-sounds, mantras, dance, song, and instruments, the lamp should be waved from the deity’s head to feet chanting ‘śriye jāta’ (Ṛg Veda 7.4.4) with the Praṇava-salutation (oṃkāra), three times clockwise (triḥ-pradakṣiṇa).

  • Conclusion: The vessel is placed back in its original position. Having bowed to the Lord, the directional rice-balls are released starting from the east, chanting ‘atodevā’ (Ṛg Veda 1.2.7). Foot-washing water and ācamana are offered. The vertical forehead mark (ūrdhva-puṇḍra) is applied with sandalwood chanting ‘tadviprāsaḥ’ (Ṛg Veda 1.2.7) and ‘ime gandhāḥ’ (Vaikhānasa Mantra Praśna 6), and a flower-offering is made with the eight-syllable mantra (aṣṭākṣara).

  • The remains are carried out of the temple and deposited at the southern side of the bhūta-pīṭha (spirit-pedestal).

  • This rite should be performed at evening worship, during the raising of the canopy-cloth (pracchatra-paṭa-uddharaṇa), during festivals (utsava), bathing rites (snapana), offering rites (bali-utsava), and at other auspicious occasions, at the conclusion of worship (arcanā-ante).

Section 5: Consecration of Sandals (Pādukā-saṃskāraḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

पादुका-संस्कारः - अथ पादुका-संस्कारं वक्ष्ये — अवटे जलद्रोण्यां वा वरुणम् अभ्यर्च्य जल-अधिवासं कृत्वा, पूर्ववद् औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा आधारं हुत्वा, आस्थान-मण्डपे धान्य-राशौ वह्नेः उपरि पादुके सन्यस्य, वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा पर्यग्निं च कारयेत्; शुद्ध्य्-अर्थं प्रोक्षणीः प्रोक्ष्य पुण्याहं कृत्वा, अग्निं परिस्तीर्य वैष्णवं विष्णुसूक्तं पुरुषसूक्तं शेषिकं च हुत्वा अग्निं विसृजेत्; आचार्य-दक्षिणां दद्यात् । pādukā-saṃskāraḥ - atha pādukā-saṃskāraṃ vakṣye — avaṭe jaladroṇyāṃ vā varuṇam abhyarcya jala-adhivāsaṃ kṛtvā, pūrvavad aupāsana-agni-kuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā ādhāraṃ hutvā, āsthāna-maṇḍape dhānya-rāśau vahneḥ upari pāduke sanyasya, vāstu-homaṃ hutvā paryagniṃ ca kārayet; śuddhyarthaṃ prokṣaṇīḥ prokṣya puṇyāhaṃ kṛtvā, agniṃ paristīrya vaiṣṇavaṃ viṣṇusūktaṃ puruṣasūktaṃ śeṣikaṃ ca hutvā agniṃ visṛjet; ācārya-dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāt.

सर्व-अलंकार-संयुक्तं स्वस्ति-सूक्तेन आलयं प्रदक्षिणी-कृत्य देव-अभिमुखे सन्यस्य, तयोः मध्ये शेषम् अभ्यर्च्य विष्णुसूक्तेन देवस्य पादयोः संयोज्य, ततः देवं समविंशति-विग्रहैः अभ्यर्च्य, महाहविः प्रभूतं वा निवेदयेत्; ततः-प्रभृति विष्णु-उत्सव-काले शिरसा धारयन् अग्रतः गच्छेत्; शिबिकायां हस्ते वा समारोप्य नयेद् इति केचित्; नित्यं बल्य्-अन्ते बलि-बेर-पीठे सन्यस्य पाद्य-अध्य्-अर्चनं पूजयित्वा, अभिमुखे पादुके सन्यस्य पूर्ववत् शेषम् अभ्यर्च्य पादुके संयोज्य, देवेन सह अभ्यन्तरं प्रविश्य गर्भ-आगारे संन्यसेत् ॥ sarva-alaṃkāra-saṃyuktaṃ svasti-sūktena ālayaṃ pradakṣiṇī-kṛtya deva-abhimukhe sanyasya, tayoḥ madhye śeṣam abhyarcya viṣṇusūktena devasya pādayoḥ saṃyojya, tataḥ devaṃ samaviṃśati-vigrahaiḥ abhyarcya, mahāhaviḥ prabhūtaṃ vā nivedayet; tataḥ-prabhṛti viṣṇu-utsava-kāle śirasā dhārayan agrataḥ gacchet; śibikāyāṃ haste vā samāropya nayed iti kecit; nityaṃ baly-ante bali-bera-pīṭhe sanyasya pādya-adhyarcanaṃ pūjayitvā, abhimukhe pāduke sanyasya pūrvavat śeṣam abhyarcya pāduke saṃyojya, devena saha abhyantaraṃ praviśya garbha-āgāre saṃnyaset.

English Translation

  • Water-soaking: Now I shall describe the consecration of the divine sandals (pādukā). In a pit or water-trough, Varuṇa (deity of waters) should be worshipped and the sandals soaked in water (jala-adhivāsa).

  • Fire Rite: The aupāsana fire-pit should be prepared as before, the foundation oblation offered, and the sandals placed upon a grain-heap above the fire in the audience pavilion (āsthāna-maṇḍapa). The vāstu-homa and circumambulation by fire should be performed. For purification, the sprinkling vessels (prokṣaṇī) should be used and the puṇyāha benediction recited. Oblations should be offered with the Vaiṣṇava, Viṣṇu-sūkta, Puruṣa-sūkta, and Śeṣa (Ādiśeṣa) mantras; then the fire dismissed. The officiant’s fee should be given.

  • Circumambulation and Installation: Fully adorned, the sandals should be carried around the temple while chanting the Svasti-sūkta, placed facing the deity, and Śeṣa (the serpent Ādiśeṣa) worshipped between the pair. Using the Viṣṇu-sūkta, the sandals should be joined to the deity’s feet. The deity should then be worshipped with the twenty-fold liturgy and a grand food-offering presented.

  • Festival Procession: Henceforth, during Viṣṇu-festivals, [the attendant] should carry the sandals on the head and walk ahead of the procession. Some authorities say they may be placed on a palanquin (śibikā) or carried in the hand.

  • Daily Practice: Every day, at the conclusion of the bali (offering) rite, the sandals should be placed on the bali-bera pedestal, the foot-washing and worship performed, then the sandals placed facing the deity and Śeṣa worshipped as before. After joining the sandals, they should be taken inside with the deity and placed in the inner sanctum (garbhāgāra).

Section 6: Consecration of Vehicles and Mounts (Vāhanādīnāṃ Saṃskāraḥ)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

वाहनादीनां संस्कारः - अथ वाहनादीनां संस्कारं वक्ष्ये — पञ्चगव्यैः संशोध्य वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा पर्यग्निं कारयेत्; औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा कुम्भं साध्य, तत्-तद्-अधिदेवं देवेशं च ध्यात्वा आवाह्य समाभिः कलशैः संस्नाप्य, मण्डपे प्रपायां वा धान्ये स्थण्डिलं कृत्वा नवकलशान् न्यास्तीर्य उपरि सन्यस्य नवकलश-वीर-च्छायां पुण्याहं वाचयेत्; अग्निं परिषिच्य वैष्णवं, विष्णुसूक्तं, पुरुषसूक्तं, तत्-तद्-अधिदैवत्यं, मूर्ति-मन्त्रान् च व्याहृत्य्-अन्तं हुत्वा अग्निं विसृजेत्; vāhanādīnāṃ saṃskāraḥ - atha vāhanādīnāṃ saṃskāraṃ vakṣye — pañcagavyaiḥ saṃśodhya vāstu-homaṃ hutvā paryagniṃ kārayet; aupāsana-agni-kuṇḍaṃ kṛtvā kumbhaṃ sādhya, tat-tad-adhidevaṃ deveśaṃ ca dhyātvā āvāhya samābhiḥ kalaśaiḥ saṃsnāpya, maṇḍape prapāyāṃ vā dhānye sthaṇḍilaṃ kṛtvā navakalśān nyāstīrya upari sanyasya navakalśa-vīra-cchāyāṃ puṇyāhaṃ vācayet; agniṃ pariṣicya vaiṣṇavaṃ, viṣṇusūktaṃ, puruṣasūktaṃ, tat-tad-adhidaivatyaṃ, mūrti-mantrān ca vyāhṛtyantaṃ hutvā agniṃ visṛjet;

आचार्यं पूजयित्वा दक्षिणां दद्यात्; देवेशं प्रणम्य आचार्यः समाहितः देवं ध्यायन् — वाहनेषु गरुडं, शयनेषु शेषं (आदिशेषं), आसनेषु शिवादीन्, अन्येषु भूत-तत्-तद्-विधि-देवान् — आवाह्य अभ्यर्च्य, देवेशं प्रणम्य शकुन-सूक्तेन देवम् आदाय ‘भूर् असि भू’ (वैं.म.प्र.६) इत्यादिना शयनादिषु प्रतिष्ठाप्य, ‘प्र तद् विष्णुः’ (ऋ.सं.१-२-२४) इति पुष्पाञ्जलिं दत्वा, देवेशं प्रणम्य अर्घ्य-अन्तम् अर्चां अलंकृत्य, शकुन-सूक्तं समुच्चार्य सर्व-अलंकार-संयुक्तं ग्राम-अलयं वा प्रदक्षिणं कृत्वा कारयित्वा आस्थान-मण्डपे संस्थाप्य नृत्य-गीत-वाद्यैः विनोदं कारयेत्; शुद्ध-स्नपन-विधिना संस्नाप्य अभ्यर्च्य महाहविः प्रभूतं निवेद्य अर्चा-स्थाने संस्थाप्य अर्चयेत् — इत्य् आह मरीचिः ॥ ācāryaṃ pūjayitvā dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāt; deveśaṃ praṇamya ācāryaḥ samāhitaḥ devaṃ dhyāyan — vāhaneṣu garuḍaṃ, śayaneṣu śeṣaṃ (ādiśeṣaṃ), āsaneṣu śivādīn, anyeṣu bhūta-tat-tad-vidhi-devān — āvāhya abhyarcya, deveśaṃ praṇamya śakuna-sūktena devam ādāya ‘bhūr asi bhū’ (vaiṃ.m.pr.6) ityādinā śayanādiṣu pratiṣṭhāpya, ‘pra tad viṣṇuḥ’ (ṛ.saṃ.1-2-24) iti puṣpāñjaliṃ datvā, deveśaṃ praṇamya arghya-antam arcāṃ alaṃkṛtya, śakuna-sūktaṃ samuccārya sarva-alaṃkāra-saṃyuktaṃ grāma-alayaṃ vā pradakṣiṇaṃ kṛtvā kārayitvā āsthāna-maṇḍape saṃsthāpya nṛtya-gīta-vādyaiḥ vinodaṃ kārayet; śuddha-snapana-vidhinā saṃsnāpya abhyarcya mahāhaviḥ prabhūtaṃ nivedya arcā-sthāne saṃsthāpya arcayet — ity āha marīciḥ.

English Translation

  • Now I shall describe the consecration of the vehicles (vāhana) and other mounts.

  • Purification: They should be purified with pañcagavya, the vāstu-homa performed, and the circumambulation by fire carried out. The aupāsana fire-pit should be prepared and a kumbha (water-pot) consecrated. The presiding deity of each vehicle, along with the Lord, should be meditated upon, invoked, and bathed with equal pots of water.

  • Platform: In the pavilion or water-shed (prapā), a grain-platform should be prepared, nine pots spread upon it, and the vehicles placed above. The puṇyāha benediction is recited under the canopy of the nine pots. Oblations should be offered with the Vaiṣṇava, Viṣṇu-sūkta, Puruṣa-sūkta, the presiding deity mantras, and the image-mantras, concluding with the vyāhṛtis. Then the fire should be dismissed.

  • Invocation of Mount-Deities: The officiant should honour the ācārya with a fee, bow to the Lord, and then — concentrated in mind and meditating on the deity — invoke the following into the respective mounts:

    • In the vehicles (vāhana): Garuḍa (the divine eagle)
    • In the couches/beds (śayana): Śeṣa (Ādiśeṣa, the cosmic serpent)
    • In the seats/thrones (āsana): Śiva and others
    • In other items: the appropriate elemental and ritual deities

  • Installation: Having worshipped them and bowed to the Lord, the officiant should lift the deity with the Śakuna-sūkta, install Him on the couch etc. chanting ‘bhūr asi bhū’ (Vaikhānasa Mantra Praśna 6), and offer a flower-handful chanting ‘pra tad viṣṇuḥ’ (Ṛg Veda 1.2.24). After bowing to the Lord and completing worship up to the arghya offering, the deity — fully adorned — should be taken in procession around the village or temple precincts while chanting the Śakuna-sūkta, then installed in the audience pavilion (āsthāna-maṇḍapa) with entertainment of dance, song, and music.

119 चौपासनाशिकुण्डकृत्वा च औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा Fire-pit compound.
119 नवकलशण्यास्तीर्य नवकलशान् न्यास्तीर्य Nine pots spacing.
119 वास्तुहोमंहुत्वा वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा Spacing.
119 अग्निपरिस्तीर्य अग्निं परिस्तीर्य Spacing.
119 तत्तदधिदेवतंमूर्तिमंत्रंच तत्-तद्-अधिदेवता-मूर्ति-मन्त्रं च Compound word spacing.
119 तत्तदधिपान तत्-तद्-अधिपान् Compound word.
120 दक्षिणांदद्यात् दक्षिणां दद्यात् Spacing.
120 अतोदेवादीन् अतोदेवादीन् (Preserved; mantra name.)
120 सेनेशमर्चयेत् सेनेशम् अर्चयेत् Spacing.
120 सभ्याशिकुण्डकृत्वा सभ्य-अग्नि-कुण्डं कृत्वा Fire-pit name.
120 पश्चाग्न्येनप्रक्षाल्य पञ्चगव्येन प्रक्षाल्य Pañcagavya not paścāgni.
120 सकलशैः सकलशैः (स-कलशैः) “With pots.”
120 तानिसंन्यच्य तानि संन्यस्य Spacing and spelling.
120 अग्निंपरिस्तीर्यंतन्त्रहैदेवत्यं अग्निं परिस्तीर्य तन्त्र-हृद्-दैवत्यं Heart-mantra deity.
120 स्तपनोक्तविधिना स्नपन-उक्त-विधिना Bathing (snapana), not heating (stāpana).
120 वक्षमाल्यौरलंकृत्य वक्षो-माल्यैः अलंकृत्य Chest-garland.
120 एकाडक्षरादि एकादशाक्षरादि Eleven-syllable metre.
120 ‘भूतोभूते’ श्विति ‘भूतो भूते’ इति Mantra quotation.
120 तत्ततलंकृत्य तत्-तत् अलंकृत्य Spacing.
120 आस्थानम् (पट्)ण्डवे आस्थान-मण्डपे Audience pavilion.
120 निश्योमूर्तिमंत्रंच निज-मूर्ति-मन्त्रं च Own image-mantra.
120 पुण्याहंवाचरित्वा पुण्याहं वाचयित्वा Spacing.
120 आचार्यंपूजयित्वा आचार्यं पूजयित्वा Spacing.
120 देवेशंसंस्नाय देवेशं संस्नाप्य Bathing transitive.
120 वक्षमाल्यगन्धाचैरलंकृत्य वक्षो-माल्य-गन्धादैः अलंकृत्य Compound.
120 हस्ताभ्यांपद्ममुद्रत्य हस्ताभ्यां पद्म-मुद्रां कृत्वा Lotus-mudrā.
120 शंखपद्माश्व्यपात्रस्थजलं शङ्ख-पद्म-अभ्य-पात्र-स्थ-जलं Water in conch-lotus vessels.
120 सहस्रधारापात्रस्थपये सहस्रधारा-पात्र-स्थ-अपये Thousand-stream vessel.
120 वसोःपवित्रं वसोःपवित्रं (Preserved; hymn name.)
120 समुच्चार्याऽभिषिच्य समुच्चार्य अभिषिच्य Spacing.
120 पश्चादाचमनं पश्चात् आचमनं Spacing.
120 प्लोतेनविमृज्य प्लोतेन विमृज्य Spacing.
120 नित्यनैमित्तिकादिद्विवेळं नित्य-नैमित्तिक-आदि-द्विवेलं Compound.
120 स्तपनंसर्वदोषोपशमनं स्नपनं सर्व-दोष-उपशमनं Bathing (snapana).
121 पिचुवर्त्तियुतदीपं पिचु-वर्ति-युत-दीपं Cotton-wick lamp compound.
121 दिक्कुपिण्डार्थं दिक्-कु-पिण्ड-अर्थं Directional offering compound.
121 हरिद्राचूर्णितेनाऽग्निनेन हरिद्रा-चूर्णितेन अग्निना Turmeric-fire.
121 तत्पात्रंशिरसा तत्-पात्रं शिरसा Spacing.
121 ध्रुवा धृत्वा “Having held” (not dhruva).
121 शनैःशनैर्गत्वा शनैः शनैः गत्वा Spacing.
121 आलयंप्रविश्य आलयं प्रविश्य Spacing.
121 अभिमु खेसंस्थापयेत् अभिमुखे संस्थापयेत् Spacing.
121 शिर्यंचाग्नय्यर्च्य सूर्यं च अग्निं अर्च्य Sun and Fire worship.
121 देवस्यपादयोः देवस्य पादयोः Spacing.
121 पुष्पांजलिंदत्वा पुष्पाञ्जलिं दत्वा Spacing.
121 शंखनादस मन्त्रितं शङ्ख-नाद-समन्त्रितं Conch-sound with mantras.
121 इतिर्विंबसय इति विम्बस्य “Of the image.”
121 प्रणवाऽऽस्कारं प्रणव-आस्कारं Praṇava salutation.
121 पूर्वस्थानेन्यस्य पूर्व-स्थाने न्यस्य Spacing.
121 प्रगादि प्राग्-आदि “Starting from east.”
121 दिक्कुपिण्डानविसृज्य दिक्-कु-पिण्डान् विसृज्य Spacing.
121 गन्धेनोर्ध्वपुण्ड्रं गन्धेन ऊर्ध्व-पुण्ड्रं Vertical mark.
121 पूर्ववत्र माहृत्य पूर्ववत् आहृत्य “As before, having collected.”
121 आलयाब्रहिर्गत्वा आलयात् बहिः गत्वा “Having gone outside the temple.”
121 प्रच्छत्रपटोद्धरणे प्रच्छत्र-पट-उद्धरणे Canopy cloth raising.
121 बल्युत्सवे बल्य्-उत्सवे Offering festival.
121 पुण्यक्षेषु पुण्य-क्षेत्रेषु Sacred occasions.
121 अथपादुकासंस्कारं अथ पादुका-संस्कारं Spacing.
121 पूर्ववदीपासनाश्निकुण्डं पूर्ववद् औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं Fire-pit.
121 मंटपेधान्यराशौ मण्डपे धान्य-राशौ Pavilion grain-heap.
121 वह्रोपरि वह्नेः उपरि “Above the fire.”
121 पर्यन्नि पर्यग्निं Circumambulation by fire.
121 शुद्धर्थं शुद्ध्य्-अर्थं “For purification.”
121 अग्निंपरिस्तीर्यं अग्निं परिस्तीर्य Spacing.
121 अग्निंविसृजेत् अग्निं विसृजेत् Spacing.
121 तयोर्मध्ये तयोः मध्ये “Between the two.”
121 देवस्यपादयोस्संयोज्य देवस्य पादयोः संयोज्य Spacing.
121 निवेद्यशेत् निवेदयेत् Spelling.
121 विष्णोत्सवकाले विष्णु-उत्सव-काले Festival time.
121 शिरसाधारयन् शिरसा धारयन् “Carrying on head.”
121 अग्रतोगच्छेत् अग्रतः गच्छेत् “Should walk ahead.”
121 बल्यन्ते बल्य्-अन्ते “At end of bali.”
121 पाद्याध्यर्ऽनिं पाद्य-अध्य्-अर्चनं Foot-worship.
121 पूर्ववच्छेषमभ्यर्च्य पूर्ववत् शेषम् अभ्यर्च्य “Worshipping Śeṣa as before.”
121 सहाभ्यन्तरं सह अभ्यन्तरं “Together inside.”
121 गर्भागारेसंन्यसेत् गर्भ-आगारे संन्यसेत् Inner sanctum.
122 पञ्चगव्यैस्संशोध्य पञ्चगव्यैः संशोध्य Spacing.
122 वास्तुहोमंहुत्वा वास्तु-होमं हुत्वा Spacing.
122 पर्यश्निकारयेत् पर्यग्निं कारयेत् Fire circumambulation.
122 औपासनाश्निकुण्डं औपासन-अग्नि-कुण्डं Fire-pit name.
122 कुम्भंसाध्य कुम्भं साध्य Spacing.
122 शयनेषुशोभां शयनेषु शेषं Śeṣa (Ādiśeṣa), not śobhā.
122 आसनेषु शिवादीन् आसनेषु शिवादीन् (Preserved.)
122 अन्येषुभूतत्तद्विधिदेवानावाह्य अन्येषु भूत-तत्-तद्-विधि-देवान् आवाह्य Spacing.
122 देवेशंप्रणम्य देवेशं प्रणम्य Spacing.
122 ‘भूरसिंभू’ ‘भूर् असि भू’ Earth-mantra.
122 रित्यादिना इत्यादिना “Beginning with.”
122 ‘प्रतद्विष्णु’ ‘प्र तद् विष्णुः’ Ṛg Veda hymn.
122 रिति इति “Thus.”
122 अर्ध्यन्तमर्चाडलंकृत्य अर्घ्य-अन्तम् अर्चां अलंकृत्य Worship through arghya.
122 शकुनसूक्तसमुचार्य शकुन-सूक्तं समुच्चार्य Bird-hymn chanting.
122 ग्राममल्यं ग्राम-अलयं Village temple.
122 आस्थानमण्टपेसंस्थाप्य आस्थान-मण्डपे संस्थाप्य Pavilion.
122 नृच्यगीतवाद्यैर्विनोदंकारयेत् नृत्य-गीत-वाद्यैः विनोदं कारयेत् Spacing.
122 शुद्धस्तपनविधिना शुद्ध-स्नपन-विधिना Bathing rite.
122 संस्नाय संस्नाप्य Transitive bathing.
122 अर्चस्थाने अर्चा-स्थाने Worship station.
122 संस्थाप्याऽर्चयेदित्याह संस्थाप्य अर्चयेत् इत्य् आह “Should worship — thus says.”

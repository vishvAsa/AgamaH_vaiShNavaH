Section 1: The Threefold Classification of Fire-Offerings and Worship Around the Altar (Homa-traividhyam, Vedy-utsavādi-deva-pūjanam)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

अथ द्वात्रिंशः पटलः - हौत्र-शंसन-आदि-सर्वदेवत्य-पारमात्मिक-अनुक्रमणिका-विधिः atha dvātriṃśaḥ paṭalaḥ - hautra-śaṃsana-ādi-sarvadevatya-pāramātmika-anukramaṇikā-vidhiḥ

होम-त्रैविध्यम् - एवं कृत्वा पश्चात् सर्वेषां परिवाराणाम् आलयं परितो होमम् आचरेत्। homa-traividhyam - evaṃ kṛtvā paścāt sarveṣāṃ parivārāṇām ālayaṃ parito homam ācaret.

  • अधम-होमः (Lowest grade): Perform homas for Lakṣmī-Bhūmī (Śrī-Bhūmyoḥ), Brahmā-Śiva (Brahma-Īśānayoḥ), Bhṛgu-Mārkaṇḍeya, and Vīśa-Śeṣa (Vīśa-Śeṣayoḥ).
  • मध्यम-होमः (Middle grade): Perform homas for the temple-guardians (vimāna-pālas), Indra and the world-guardians (lokapālas), and the Sun and Śeṣa.
  • उत्तम-होमः (Highest grade): Perform homas for the door-guardians (dvāra-pālas), Chakra, Banner, Conch, the five elements (mahābhūtas), and Arjuna. ** adhama-homaḥ: perform homas for śrī-bhūmyoḥ, brahma-īśānayoḥ, bhṛgu-mārkaṇḍeyoḥ, vīśa-śeṣayoḥ.* ** madhyama-homaḥ: perform homas for vimāna-pālān, indrādi-lokapālān, āditya-śeṣayoḥ.* ** uttama-homaḥ: perform homas for dvāra-pālān, cakra-dhvaja-śaṅkha-mahābhūta-pāka-arjunānām.*

वेदिं परितः देवार्चन-क्रमः - यज्ञ-शालां महा-वेदिं वा परितः सर्वान् देवान् समभ्यर्च्य; महा-वेद्याः उपरिष्ठात् प्राग्-आदि-चतुर्-दिक्षु ध्रुव-कौतुकयोः आवरण-त्रय-देवान्, गर्भालय-अधिपान्; vediṃ paritaḥ devārcana-kramaḥ - yajña-śālāṃ mahā-vediṃ vā paritaḥ sarvān devān samabhyarcya; mahā-vedyāḥ upariṣṭhāt prāg-ādi-catur-dikṣu dhruva-kautukayoḥ āvaraṇa-traya-devān, garbhālaya-adhipān;

दक्षिणे ब्रह्माणं मार्कण्डेयम्, उत्तरे शङ्करं ख्यातीशं (ख्यातीशं च), द्वारेषु धात्रादि-द्वारपालान्, विमानपालादीन् सर्वान् देवान् प्रथम-आवरण-आदि-आवरण-क्रमेण तत्-तत्-दिक्षु समर्चयेत् ॥ dakṣiṇe brahmāṇaṃ mārkaṇḍeyam, uttare śaṅkaraṃ khyātīśaṃ, dvāreṣu dhātrādi-dvārapālān, vimānapālādīn sarvān devān prathama-āvaraṇa-ādi-āvaraṇa-krameṇa tat-tad-dikṣu samarcayet.

प्रथमावरणे एव सर्व-आवरण-देवानां निवेश-पक्षः - अथवा — इन्द्रं, न्यक्षं, श्रियं, हविःपालम्, अग्निं, भौमं, जगद्ध्रुवं, विवस्वन्तं, यमं, दुर्गां, समरोहिणीः, सप्त-मातृः (सममातृः), नीलं च, महाकालीं, पुरुषं, वरुणं, मित्रं, बुधं, ज्येष्ठां, पुष्येशं, वायुं, शुक्रं, सप्त-ऋषीन् (समर्षीन्), गङ्गां, धान्यं, क्षत्तारं, निशाकरं, पाञ्चभौतिकं, शान्तम्, ईशानं, बहिरक्षकं, वाग्देवीं च — prathamāvaraṇe eva sarva-āvaraṇa-devānāṃ niveśa-pakṣaḥ - athavā — indraṃ, nyakṣaṃ, śriyaṃ, haviḥpālam, agniṃ, bhaumaṃ, jagaddhruvaṃ, vivasvantaṃ, yamaṃ, durgāṃ, samarohiṇīḥ, sapta-mātṛḥ, nīlaṃ ca, mahākālīṃ, puruṣaṃ, varuṇaṃ, mitraṃ, budhaṃ, jyeṣṭhāṃ, puṣyeśaṃ, vāyuṃ, śukraṃ, sapta-ṛṣīn, gaṅgāṃ, dhānyaṃ, kṣattāraṃ, niśākaraṃ, pāñcabhautikaṃ, śāntam, īśānaṃ, bahirakṣakaṃ, vāgdevīṃ ca —

एवं पूर्व-आदि-प्रदक्षिणं, प्राङ्-मुखे श्रीभूत-गरुड-चक्र-ध्वज-शङ्ख-यूथाधिप-विष्णु-भूतान् यथा-क्रमेण एकादश-उपचारैः अभ्यर्च्य, तत्-तद्-नाम्ना अर्चयेद् इति केचित् ॥ evaṃ pūrva-ādi-pradakṣiṇaṃ, prāṅ-mukhe śrībhūta-garuḍa-cakra-dhvaja-śaṅkha-yūthādhipa-viṣṇu-bhūtān yathā-krameṇa ekādaśa-upacāraiḥ abhyarcya, tat-tad-nāmnā arcayed iti kecit.

English Translation

  • Three Classes of Fire Offerings: Having completed the preliminary rites, the priest offers homas around all the subsidiary shrines. They are classified into three grades:

    • Lowest (Adhama): Offering fires to Lakshmī and Bhūmī, Brahmā and Śiva, Bhṛgu and Mārkaṇḍeya, and Vīśa and Śeṣa.
    • Middle (Madhyama): Offering fires to the temple-guardians (vimāna-pālas), Indra and the world-guardians (lokapālas), and the Sun (Āditya) and Śeṣa.
    • Highest (Uttama): Offering fires to the gateway guards (dvāra-pālas), Chakra, Banner, Conch, the five elements, and Arjuna.

  • Worship Around the Altar: Having worshipped all the deities around the sacrificial hall or the main altar (mahā-vedi), the priest worships the deities of the three enclosures (āvaraṇa-traya) and the sanctum lords to the east, south, west, and north of the main altar.

  • Directional Assignments: He worships Brahmā and Mārkaṇḍeya to the south; Śaiva deities (Śaṅkara) and Khyātīśa (Khyāti-pati) to the north; and the gate-guardians (Dhātṛ etc.) and temple-guardians at the doors, in clockwise order starting from the first enclosure.

  • Consolidated Enclosure Alternative: Alternatively, the priest may arrange all the enclosure deities in the first enclosure:

    • Indra, Nyakṣa, Lakshmī (Śrī), Haviḥpāla, Agni, Bhauma, Jagaddhruva, Vivasvat, Yama, Durgā, the Samarohoṇīs, the seven mothers (sapta-mātṛs), Nīla, Mahākālī, Puruṣa, Varuṇa, Mitra, Budha, Jyeṣṭhā, Puṣyeśa, Vāyu, Śukra, the seven sages (sapta-ṛṣīs), Gaṅgā, Dhānya, Kṣattṛ, the Moon (Nisha-kara), the elemental deities, Śānta, Īśāna, Bahirakṣaka, and Sarasvatī (Vāgdevī).
    • Moving clockwise starting from the east, and facing east, he worships: Śrībhūta, Garuḍa, Chakra, Banner, Conch, the troop-commanders (yūthādhipa), and the elemental spirits of Viṣṇu, offering eleven services (ekādaśa-upacāras) and chanting their respective names.

Section 2: Hotr Hymns and Oblations in the Sabhya Fire (Hautra-praśaṃsanam, Praṇidhāv-āvāhanaṃ ca)

Sanskrit (Devanagari) & Transliteration (IAST)

हौत्र-प्रशंसनम् - प्रभूत-बलिम् आहत्य बलि-निर्वापं च कारयेत्। पश्चाद् होतारम् आहूय वस्त्र-उत्तरीय-आभरणैः अभिपूजयेत् (अभिपुजयेत्) । hautra-praśaṃsanam - prabhūta-balim āhatya bali-nirvāpaṃ ca kārayet. paścād hotāram āhūya vastra-uttarīya-ābharaṇair abhipūjayet.

स होता सभ्यस्य पुरतः तिष्ठेत्; सभ्य-अध्वर्युः अग्निं प्रज्वाल्य ‘होतरेहि’ इति वदति। स होता ‘अध्वर्यो देवेतः’ (अध्वर्योर्दिवते) इति उक्त्वा, आचम्य, प्रणव-पूर्वं ‘नमः प्रवक्त्रे’ (वै॰ म॰ प्र॰ ८) इति वदन् तिष्ठन् सोच्चं (उच्चैः) वैखानस-सूत्र-उक्त-विधिना हौत्रं प्रशंसयेत्। sa hotā sabhyasya purataḥ tiṣṭhet; sabhya-adhvaryuḥ agniṃ prajvālya ‘hotarehi’ iti vadati. sa hotā ‘adhvaryo devetaḥ’ iti uktvā, ācamya, praṇava-pūrvaṃ ’namaḥ pravaktre’ (vai. ma. pra. 8) iti vadaṇ tiṣṭhan soccaṃ (uccaiḥ) vaikhānasa-sūtra-ukta-vidhinā hautraṃ praśaṃsayet.

अध्वर्युः तेन उक्तम् ओंकारं श्रुत्वा, सप्तदश-पालाश-इध्मान् (समदशपालाशेश्ध्मन्) पद्म-मुकुल-अग्रान् घृत-अक्तान् गृहीत्वा, अग्नौ ‘ओं स्वाहा’ इति क्षिपेत्; adhvaryuḥ tena uktam oṃkāraṃ śrutvā, saptadaśa-pālāśa-idhmān padma-mukula-agrān ghṛta-aktān gṛhītvā, agnau ‘oṃ svāhā’ iti kṣipet;

होता ‘ब्राह्मणो भारतः’ (वै॰ म॰ प्र॰ ८) इति अन्तम् उक्त्वा, यजमानस्य गोत्र-प्रवरं क्रमेण वदेत्। ग्रामो (यजमानो) यजमानः चेत्, काश्यप-प्रवरं वदेत्। hotā ‘brāhmaṇo bhārataḥ’ (vai. ma. pra. 8) iti antam uktvā, yajamānasya gotra-pravaraṃ krameṇa vadet. grāmo yajamānaḥ cet, kāśyapa-pravaraṃ vadet.

‘आयातु’ (वै॰ म॰ प्र॰ ८) इति मन्त्र-अन्ते ‘विष्णु-आदि-महाभूत-अन्तम् आवह’ इति वदेत् ॥ ‘āyātu’ (vai. ma. pra. 8) iti mantra-ante ‘viṣṇu-ādi-mahābhūta-antam āvaha’ iti vadet.

प्रणिधौ आवाहनं सभ्ये उक्तहोमः - तथैव अध्वर्युः — आदिमूर्ति-आदि-शर्व-ान्तं दक्षिणे प्रणिधौ, धात्रादि-महाभूत-ान्तम् उत्तर-प्रणिधौ समावाह्य; आवाहन-क्रमेण जुष्टाकारं (making acceptable) कृत्वा, तत्-क्रमेण आज्य-आहुतीः जुहोति। praṇidhau āvāhanaṃ sabhye uktahomaḥ - tathaiva adhvaryuḥ — ādimūrti-ādi-śarva-antaṃ dakṣiṇe praṇidhau, dhātrādi-mahābhūta-antam uttara-praṇidhau samāvāhya; āvāhana-krameṇa juṣṭākāraṃ kṛtvā, tat-krameṇa ājya-āhutīr juhoti.

सभ्य-अग्निं परिषिच्य, पालाश-इध्मान् गृहीत्वा, मूल-अग्राभ्यां घृतं स्पर्शयित्वा, देवं ध्यायन् विष्णु-गायत्र्या जुहोति। sabhya-agniṃ pariṣicya, pālāśa-idhmān gṛhītvā, mūla-agrābyāṃ ghṛtaṃ sparśayitvā, devaṃ dhyāyan viṣṇu-gāyatryā juhoti.

अग्निहोत्र-हवण्या आज्यं गृहीत्वा, ‘अतो देवा’ (ऋ॰ १-२२-१६) इत्यादिना हुत्वा; पश्चाद् उपभृता आज्यम् आदाय, विष्णु-सूक्तैः च हुत्वा; जुह्वां पञ्च-वारम् आदाय, वैष्णवं षोडश-वारं द्वादश-वारं वा जुहोति। agnihotra-havaṇyā ājyaṃ gṛhītvā, ‘ato devā’ (ṛ. 1-22-16) ityādinā hutvā; paścād upabhṛtā ājyam ādāya, viṣṇu-sūktaiś ca hutvā; juhvāṃ pañca-vāraṃ ādāya, vaiṣṇavaṃ ṣoḍaśa-vāraṃ dvādaśa-vāraṃ vā juhoti.

स्रुवेण आज्यम् आदाय, पञ्च-वारुणं, मूल-होमं, ‘यद्देवा देवहेडनम्’ इति कूष्माण्ड-होमं च सकृत् हुत्वा; sruveṇa ājyam ādāya, pañca-vāruṇaṃ, mūla-homaṃ, ‘yaddevā devaheḍanam’ iti kūṣmāṇḍa-homaṃ ca sakṛt hutvā;

पश्चात् — ‘स्वस्ति चैवेहा स्वाहा, प्रजापतये स्वाहा, आदित्येभ्यः स्वाहा, विश्वेभ्यो देवेभ्यः स्वाहा, मरुद्गणेभ्यः स्वाहा, भूम्यै च पृथिव्यै च महते च स्वाहा’ इति एतैः दशभिः मन्त्रैः शतशः जुहुयात्। paścāt — ‘svasti caivehā svāhā, prajāpataye svāhā, ādityebhyaḥ svāhā, viśvebhyo devebhyaḥ svāhā, marudgaṇebhyaḥ svāhā, bhūmyai ca pṛthivyai ca mahate ca svāhā’ iti etaiḥ daśabhiḥ mantrair śataśaḥ juhuyāt.

एषा सहस्र-आहुतिः — एनाम् उपांशु (whispered/silent) यजेत्। eṣā sahasra-āhutiḥ — enām upāṃśu yajet.

वैष्णवं, विष्णु-सूक्तं, पुरुष-सूक्तं, ध्रुव-कौतुकयोः पुरुषादि-आवरण-त्रय-देवानां वैष्णवं च तत्-तत्-मन्त्रैः जुहुयात् ॥ vaiṣṇavaṃ, viṣṇu-sūktaṃ, puruṣa-sūktaṃ, dhruva-kautukayoḥ puruṣādi-āvaraṇa-traya-devānāṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ ca tat-tat-mantrair juhuyāt.

English Translation

  • The Hotr Rites: Having gathered the grand food offerings (prabhūta-bali), the priest distributes them. Next, summoning the hotṛ (reciting priest), he honors him with garments, upper-cloths, and ornaments.

  • The Dialogue: The hotṛ stands in front of the Sabhya fire-pit. The Sabhya assistant (adhvaryu), having kindled the fire, says: “O Hotṛ, come!” The hotṛ replies: “O Adhvaryu, devotee of the gods!”, sips water (ācamana), and standing, chants aloud the hautra praises according to the Vaikhānasa manual, beginning with “namaḥ pravaktre” preceded by the praṇava.

  • The Fire Oblations: Hearing the hotṛ pronounce “Om”, the adhvaryu takes seventeen firewood sticks of Palāśa wood shaped like lotus buds at the tips and smeared with ghee, and casts them into the fire with “Oṃ svāhā”.

  • Declaring Ancestry: The hotṛ, chanting up to “brāhmaṇo bhārataḥ”, declares the lineage (gotra) and lineage-sages (pravara) of the patron in order. If the patron represents the village community collectively, he declares the Kāśyapa-pravara. At the end of the mantra “āyātu”, he says: “I invoke the deities from Viṣṇu down to the five elements.”

  • Invocations into Vessels: Likewise, the adhvaryu invokes the deities from Ādimūrti to Śarva into the southern vessel (dakṣiṇa-praṇidhi), and from Dhātṛ to the elements into the northern vessel (uttara-praṇidhi). Making them acceptable, he offers ghee oblations in order.

  • Fire Worship with Ghee: Having sprinkled water around the Sabhya fire and taken Palāśa wood, he smears both the base and tip with ghee, and meditating on the Lord, offers it with the Viṣṇu-Gāyatrī.

  • Using the Ladles: Taking ghee with the agnihotra-havaṇī spoon, he offers it with “ato devā” (Ṛg Veda 1.22.16); next, taking ghee with the upabhṛt ladle, he offers it with the Viṣṇu-sūkta hymns; taking ghee five times in the juhū ladle, he offers the Vaiṣṇava homa sixteen or twelve times.

  • Minor Homas: Taking ghee in the sruva, he offers the five Vāruṇa homas, the mūla-homa, and the Kūṣmāṇḍa-homa (penance for omissions) once.

  • The Thousand-fold Offering (Sahasrāhuti): Then, he should offer oblations one hundred times with each of the ten mantras ending in: “svasti caivehā svāhā, prajāpataye svāhā, ādityebhyaḥ svāhā, viśvebhyo devebhyaḥ svāhā, marudgaṇebhyaḥ svāhā, bhūmyai ca pṛthivyai ca mahate ca svāhā.” This constitutes the sahasra-āhuti (thousand-fold offering) and must be performed silently (upāṃśu).

  • Final Homas: He then offers the Vaiṣṇava homa, Viṣṇu-sūkta, Puruṣa-sūkta, and the specific Vaiṣṇava offerings for the enclosure deities of the fixed and movable images.

Section 3: The Mantras for All the Enclosure Deities (Sarvadevatya-mantra-kramaḥ)

Verse / Paragraph 1

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

सर्व-दैवत्य-मन्त्र-क्रमः - १. श्रियै (लक्ष्मी): ‘श्रिया जाता’ (वै॰ म॰ प्र॰ ८), ‘हिरण्यवर्णा’ (श्रीसूक्तं) इति द्वौ। २. मह्यै (भूमि): ‘मेदिनी देवी’ (भूमि-सूक्तं), ‘भूमिर्भूम्यै’ इति द्वौ। ३. ब्रह्मणे: vaiṣṇava-anta-homānantaraṃ kartavyam - parivārāṇāṃ tat-tat-homeṣu tat-tan-mantraṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ ca dvādaśa-vāraṃ, aṣṭ-vāraṃ, catur-vāraṃ vā paryāyataḥ juhoti.

Translation (English):

  • Final Oblations: In the fire offerings of the subsidiary deities, the priest offers their respective mantras and the Vaiṣṇava mantras twelve, eight, or four times in rotation.

Verse / Paragraph 2

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

विष्णु-सूक्तेन पञ्चसु अग्निषु दधि-क्षीर-घृत-लाजैः युक्तम् उपजुहूर् (उपजु हु) आहुत्या; सभ्य-अग्निं पौण्डरीक-अग्निं च विना, सर्वेषु अग्निषु अन्त-होमं हुत्वा, अग्नीन् विसृज्य;

Transliteration (IAST):

viṣṇu-sūktena pañcasu agniṣu dadhi-kṣīra-ghṛta-lājaiḥ yuktam upajuhūr āhutyā; sabhya-agniṃ pauṇḍarīka-agniṃ ca vinā, sarveṣu agniṣu anta-homaṃ hutvā, agnīn visṛjya;

Translation (English):

  • The Consolidated Final Homa (Anta-homa): Chanting the Viṣṇu-sūkta, he offers oblations of yogurt, milk, ghee, and puffed rice using the upajuhū ladle in the five main fires. Excluding the Sabhya and Pauṇḍarīka fires, he performs the final consolidated oblation (anta-homa) in all other fire-pits.

Verse / Paragraph 3

Sanskrit (Devanagari):

नृत्य-गेय-वाद्यैः च रात्रि-शेषं व्यपोहयेत् ॥

Transliteration (IAST):

nṛtya-geya-vādyaiḥ ca rātri-śeṣaṃ vyapohayet.

Translation (English):

  • Dismissal and Vigil: The fires are then dismissed. The remainder of the night should be spent in vigil with the entertainment of sacred dance, song, and musical instruments.
